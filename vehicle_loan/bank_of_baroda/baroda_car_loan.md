# Baroda Car Loan - Bank of Baroda

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Baroda Car Loan is a highly competitive auto loan scheme from Bank of Baroda offering financing up to 90% of on-road car price, daily reducing balance interest calculation, and zero foreclosure penalties.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% of the On-Road price
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Interest**: Interest calculated on daily reducing balance with zero prepayment penalty

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Baroda BRLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.75% to 9.90% p.a. based on CIBIL score (750+ gets lowest tier).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried (min 1 year service) or Self-Employed (min 2 years business vintage).
- Age 21 to 70 years; Minimum net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs.
- Credit score 701+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 5 Crore.
- Margin: 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 84 months (7 years).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Waived under festive offers; regular Rs. 1,500 + GST.
- Prepayment: Zero charges.

## 7. Required Documents
- Car proforma invoice, KYC, salary slips (3 months), Form 16 / ITR, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick in-principle digital sanction via bob World app.
- Zero advance EMI.
