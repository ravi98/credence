# Baroda Two-Wheeler Loan - Bank of Baroda

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Baroda Two-Wheeler Loan provides funding for purchasing new motorcycles, scooters, electric two-wheelers, and high-capacity superbikes with fast processing and competitive interest rates.

- **Funding**: Funding up to 85% of on-road price
- **Superbike**: Superbike category funding up to Rs. 10 Lakhs
- **Repayment**: Repayment tenures up to 48 months

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Baroda BRLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.75% to 15.50% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Age 21 to 65 years; Minimum monthly income of Rs. 15,000.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 2 Lakhs for standard bikes; up to Rs. 10 Lakhs for premium superbikes.
- Margin: 15% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 4 years (48 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 1% to 2% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer proforma invoice, KYC, salary slip or ITR, 3 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast approval at dealership.
- Zero foreclosure charges.
