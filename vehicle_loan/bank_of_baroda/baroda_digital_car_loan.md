# bob Digital Car Loan - Bank of Baroda

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
bob Digital Car Loan provides a 100% digital, paperless vehicle loan sanction journey. Borrowers can complete eligibility checks, KYC verification, vehicle selection, and sanction letter generation online in minutes.

- **Instant**: Instant paperless sanction through bob World digital journey
- **Direct**: Direct API integration with dealer proforma invoices
- **Pre-approved**: Pre-approved offers for existing BoB account holders

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Baroda BRLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.75% to 9.80% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried or self-employed with digital KYC and bank statement upload.
- CIBIL score 720+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs digitally.
- Margin: 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil under promotional schemes.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Fully digital paperless journey: Aadhaar e-KYC, NetBanking statement fetch, dealer quote.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Sanction letter generated in under 10 minutes.
- Seamless disbursement to dealer.
