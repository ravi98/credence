# Kotak Prime Two-Wheeler Loan - Kotak Mahindra Prime

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Kotak Prime Two-Wheeler Loan provides quick on-spot loan sanction at motorcycle and scooter dealerships across India with up to 100% on-road financing.

- **Up**: Up to 100% on-road funding
- **On-spot**: On-spot dealership approvals within 15 minutes
- **Repayment**: Repayment tenures up to 48 months

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Kotak Prime Benchmark Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.75% to 22.00% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum monthly income Rs. 15,000; Age 21 to 65 years.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to 100% on-road price (up to Rs. 3 Lakhs).
- Margin: 0% to 15%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 48 months.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 2.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: As per policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, 3 months bank statement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Dealership kiosks and fast sanction.
- Special EV two-wheeler rates.
