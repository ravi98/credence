# Kotak Prime New Car Loan - Kotak Mahindra Prime

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Kotak Mahindra Prime is India's dedicated vehicle financing subsidiary of Kotak Mahindra Bank. It offers customized car loans, structured balloon EMI plans, and extensive dealer partnerships.

- **Specialized**: Specialized auto finance entity with pan-India dealer relationships
- **Innovative**: Innovative repayment options including Balloon EMIs and Step-Up plans
- **Up**: Up to 90% - 100% on-road funding for premium vehicles

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Kotak Prime Benchmark Rate (KPBR).
- **Effective Rate**: 8.85% to 11.75% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum net annual income: Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 21 to 65 years; CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Need-based financing up to 100% on-road price.
- Margin: 0% to 10%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years (84 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.50% + GST.
- Foreclosure: Permitted after lock-in.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, salary slips / ITRs, 6 months bank statement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Balloon EMI schemes reducing initial monthly outflows.
- Doorstep documentation.
