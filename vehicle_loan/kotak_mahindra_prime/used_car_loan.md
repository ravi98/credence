# Kotak Prime Used Car Loan - Kotak Mahindra Prime

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Kotak Prime Used Car Loan provides flexible financing for purchasing certified pre-owned cars with rapid valuation, clear title verification, and simple EMIs.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 80% of vehicle valuation
- **Tenures**: Tenures up to 5 years
- **Professional**: Professional vehicle valuation and title search

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Kotak Prime Benchmark Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.50% to 16.00% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried or self-employed with stable income; CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- Margin: 20% on valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 5 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 1.50% to 2.00% + GST.
- Foreclosure: As per policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- RC, vehicle insurance, valuation certificate, KYC, income documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep service and fast hypothecation transfer.
