# Bajaj Finserv Used Car Finance - Bajaj Finance

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Bajaj Finserv Used Car Finance offers flexible loans for purchasing pre-owned cars or obtaining liquidity by refinancing an existing car. It features fast disbursals, high loan-to-value limits, and doorstep RC transfer assistance.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 85% - 90% of certified vehicle valuation
- **Quick**: Quick disbursal within 24 to 48 hours
- **Flexible**: Flexible tenures extending up to 6 years (72 months)

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Bajaj Finance Base Lending Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.50% to 18.00% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried or self-employed with net annual income of Rs. 2.50 Lakhs+; Age 21 to 65 years; CIBIL 685+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- Margin: 10% to 15% on valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 6 years (72 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 3.00% + GST.
- Prepayment: Permitted after initial lock-in period.

## 7. Required Documents
- Vehicle RC copy, insurance, seller NOC, KYC, income documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Car refinancing facility (cash against car).
- Doorstep documentation and RC transfer facilitation.
