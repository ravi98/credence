# Bajaj Finserv Two-Wheeler Loan - Bajaj Finance

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Bajaj Finserv Two-Wheeler Loan is India's leading dealership-point two-wheeler financing solution. It offers up to 100% on-road funding, instant 3-minute approvals, and minimal documentation at thousands of dealerships.

- **Up**: Up to 100% on-road price financing with zero down payment options
- **Instant**: Instant spot approval in 3 minutes with minimal documentation
- **Flexible**: Flexible EMI tenures up to 48 months

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Bajaj Finance Cost of Funds.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.00% to 24.00% p.a. based on credit tier and dealership offers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Age 21 to 65 years; Minimum monthly income of Rs. 12,000.
- CIBIL score 685+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to 100% on-road price (up to Rs. 3 Lakhs).
- Margin: 0% to 10%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Flexible tenures from 12 to 48 months.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 3.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: As per policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Aadhaar card, PAN card, bank account verification for e-NACH.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero down payment schemes during festive campaigns.
- Over 6,000+ dealership partner desks across India.
