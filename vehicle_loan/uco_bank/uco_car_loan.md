# UCO Car Loan - UCO Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
UCO Car Loan provides accessible auto financing for individuals purchasing new passenger cars and SUVs. It features competitive repo-linked rates and zero prepayment penalties.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% on-road cost
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Zero**: Zero foreclosure penalty

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: UCO Bank RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.70% to 9.85% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 21 to 65 years.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: 10% to 15% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil under campaigns.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quote, KYC, income documents, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Digital sanction via UCO mBanking Plus.
- Zero prepayment penalty.
