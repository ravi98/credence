# Maha Super Car Loan - Bank of Maharashtra

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Maha Super Car Loan is Bank of Maharashtra's flagship auto financing scheme offering one of the lowest interest rates in the industry, zero processing fees under festive campaigns, and up to 90% on-road funding.

- **Rock-bottom**: Rock-bottom interest rates among PSU banks
- **Up**: Up to 90% financing on On-Road price
- **Zero**: Zero processing fee and zero foreclosure charges

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BOM RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.70% to 9.75% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 21 to 65 years.
- CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 2 Crore.
- Margin: 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years (84 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Waived.
- Prepayment: Zero penalty.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, salary slips, Form 16, 6 months bank statement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- In-principle approval via MahaMobile app.
- Fast turnaround.
