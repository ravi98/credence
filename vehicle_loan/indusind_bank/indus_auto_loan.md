# IndusInd Auto Loan - IndusInd Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IndusInd Bank Auto Loan is a flagship vehicle financing product with deep automotive roots. It offers up to 100% financing, rapid approvals, and customized repayment schedules.

- **Deep**: Deep automotive lending expertise and strong dealer network
- **Up**: Up to 100% financing on vehicle cost
- **Tenures**: Tenures extending up to 7 years (84 months)

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IndusInd Bank EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.85% to 11.50% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum net annual income of Rs. 2.50 Lakhs; Age 21 to 65 years; CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Need-based quantum.
- Margin: 0% to 10%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.00% + GST.
- Foreclosure: As per policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer invoice, KYC, salary slips / ITRs, 6 months bank statement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep service and quick digital sanctions via IndusMobile.
- Commercial vehicle financing options.
