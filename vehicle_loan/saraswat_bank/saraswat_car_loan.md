# Saraswat Vahan Car Loan - Saraswat Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Saraswat Vahan Car Loan provides affordable vehicle financing from India's largest urban co-operative bank, featuring attractive interest rates, high LTV, and personalized branch service.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% of on-road price
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Low**: Low processing fees and transparent terms

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Saraswat Base Lending Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.85% to 10.25% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried or self-employed; Age 21 to 65 years.
- Nominal shareholding membership required.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 1 Crore.
- Margin: 10% to 15% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 0.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: Zero penalty for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, salary slips / ITRs, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Direct branch relationship manager support.
- Transparent charges.
