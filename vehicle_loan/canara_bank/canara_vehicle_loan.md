# Canara Vehicle Loan - Canara Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Canara Vehicle Loan offers affordable retail financing for the purchase of new or used cars and two-wheelers. It provides high LTV ratios, flexible repayment terms, and competitive repo-linked rates.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% of the On-Road price
- **Repayment**: Repayment tenure up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Zero**: Zero foreclosure charges and zero hidden costs

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Canara Bank RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.70% to 9.80% p.a. for new cars based on CIBIL rating.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried or self-employed with minimum net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs.
- Age: 21 to 65 years; CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 3 Crore for cars; up to Rs. 2 Lakhs for two-wheelers.
- Margin: 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years (84 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil under promotional campaigns.
- Prepayment: Zero penalty.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, 3 months salary slips or 2 years ITR, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- In-principle sanction via Canara ai1 Mobile App.
- Dedicated dealer financing desks.
