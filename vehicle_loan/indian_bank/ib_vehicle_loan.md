# IB Vehicle Loan - Indian Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IB Vehicle Loan from Indian Bank finances new cars, used cars, and two-wheelers. It offers competitive rates linked to Repo rate, easy paperwork, and flexible EMIs.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% of on-road vehicle cost
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years for new cars
- **Zero**: Zero prepayment charges

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Indian Bank IND EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.75% to 9.90% p.a. for new cars.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 21 to 65 years.
- CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Need-based up to Rs. 3 Crore.
- Margin: 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years (84 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil under promotional schemes.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, salary slips / ITRs, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast processing via IndOASIS app.
- Zero foreclosure charges.
