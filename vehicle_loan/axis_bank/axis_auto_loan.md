# Axis Bank New Car Loan - Axis Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Axis Bank New Car Loan provides up to 100% on-road financing for purchasing new passenger cars, multi-utility vehicles, and luxury cars with competitive interest rates and flexible tenures.

- **Up**: Up to 100% on-road financing on select vehicle models
- **Tenures**: Tenures extending up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Special**: Special tie-ups with leading automotive manufacturers

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Axis Bank RBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.85% to 11.50% p.a. based on credit tier.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Net annual income of Rs. 2.40 Lakhs (salaried) / Rs. 3 Lakhs (self-employed).
- Age: 21 to 70 years; CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Need-based quantum without cap.
- Margin: 0% to 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years (84 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.00% (Min Rs. 3,500 + GST).
- Foreclosure: Permissible as per policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer proforma invoice, KYC, salary slips / ITRs, 6 months bank statement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep service and digital tracking via Axis Mobile.
- Edge Rewards points on loan sanction.
