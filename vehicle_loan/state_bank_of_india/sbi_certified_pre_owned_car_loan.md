# SBI Certified Pre-Owned Car Loan - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Certified Pre-Owned Car Loan finances the purchase of inspected and certified second-hand passenger cars. The vehicle age should not exceed 5 years at purchase, and the loan must mature before the vehicle turns 8 to 10 years old.

- **Finances**: Finances used passenger cars up to 5 years old
- **Loan**: Loan quantum up to 80% of the certified vehicle valuation
- **Flexible**: Flexible tenure up to 5 years (60 months)

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: SBI EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.25% to 14.50% p.a. based on vehicle age and CIBIL score.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried or self-employed individuals with net annual income of at least Rs. 3 Lakhs.
- Age: 21 to 65 years.
- CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Maximum Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- Margin: Minimum 20% of certified vehicle valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 5 years (60 months), provided vehicle age does not exceed 8-10 years at loan maturity.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 0.50% of loan amount (Min Rs. 1,500 + GST).
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- RC copy, vehicle insurance, seller NOC, valuation report, KYC, income proof.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Direct tie-ups with certified pre-owned auto platforms (Maruti True Value, Hyundai Promise, etc.).
- Fast disbursement upon hypothecation.
