# SBI Easy Auto Two-Wheeler Loan - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Easy Auto Two-Wheeler Loan provides affordable financing for purchasing new scooters, motorcycles, and electric two-wheelers. It features transparent rates, easy eligibility, and quick dealership approvals.

- **Covers**: Covers up to 85% of the on-road price of the two-wheeler
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 48 months (4 years)
- **Minimal**: Minimal documentation with instant approval via SBI YONO

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: SBI EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.50% to 15.25% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum net monthly income: Rs. 12,500 (salaried) / Net annual income Rs. 1.50 Lakhs (self-employed).
- Age: 21 to 65 years.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Minimum Rs. 20,000; Maximum Rs. 3 Lakhs.
- Margin: 15% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Repayment tenure up to 4 years (48 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 1% to 2% + GST.
- Foreclosure: Nil penalty.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, 3 months bank statements, latest salary slip or ITR.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Available at major two-wheeler dealerships.
- Instant digital sanction through YONO.
