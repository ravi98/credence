# SBI Green Car Loan (Electric Vehicles) - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Green Car Loan is India's dedicated electric vehicle financing initiative, encouraging eco-friendly personal mobility. It offers a 20 bps (0.20%) interest rate discount over standard car loan rates and extends tenure up to 8 years.

- **20**: 20 basis points (0.20%) lower interest rate than standard SBI car loans
- **Extended**: Extended repayment tenure up to 8 years (96 months)
- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% - 100% of on-road vehicle price

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: SBI EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.55% to 9.60% p.a. (20 bps below regular car loan).
- **Green Incentive**: Priority rate for EVs.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried individuals, self-employed professionals, and business owners.
- Age 21 to 70 years; Minimum net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs.
- CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Need-based quantum; up to 90% to 100% on-road price.
- Margin: 0% to 10%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Extended tenure up to 8 years (96 EMIs).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil or heavily discounted.
- Prepayment: Zero penalty.

## 7. Required Documents
- Proforma invoice for EV model from authorized dealer.
- KYC documents and income proof (salary slips / ITRs / bank statements).

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Aligns with Government of India EV promotion and sustainability goals.
- Zero advance EMI.
- Digital processing via YONO.
