# SBI New Car Loan - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI New Car Loan is India's most popular auto financing scheme, offering the lowest interest rates, on-road price financing, and zero advance EMI. It finances new passenger cars, multi-utility vehicles, and SUVs.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% of the On-Road price (including registration, road tax, and insurance)
- **Longest**: Longest repayment tenure of up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Zero**: Zero prepayment penalty and zero foreclosure charges

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: SBI External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR).
- **Effective Rate**: 8.75% to 9.80% p.a. based on CIBIL score (750+ secures lowest tier).
- **Special Rebate**: 0.25% concession for corporate salary package (CSP) account holders.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Salaried**: Permanent employees with minimum net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 21 to 70 years.
- **Self-Employed / Professionals**: Net Profit / Gross Taxable Income of Rs. 3 Lakhs p.a. as per latest 2 years ITR.
- **Agriculturists**: Minimum net annual income of Rs. 4 Lakhs with landholding proof.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: No upper ceiling; determined by net annual income and debt-to-income (DTI) ratio.
- **LTV / Margin**: Up to 90% of On-Road price (10% borrower margin).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Repayment tenure from 1 year to 7 years (up to 84 months).
- Repayment via NACH / Standing Instructions from SBI account.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charges**: Waived under regular festive offers; standard 0.40% (Min Rs. 1,000, Max Rs. 1,500 + GST).
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure**: Zero prepayment charges.

## 7. Required Documents
- KYC: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport / Voter ID.
- Income: Last 3 months' salary slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months bank statements.
- Vehicle: Proforma Invoice / Quotation from authorized car dealer.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero advance EMI (first EMI payable after 30 days).
- In-principle sanction via SBI YONO App within minutes.
- Optional SBI Life motor loan insurance cover.
