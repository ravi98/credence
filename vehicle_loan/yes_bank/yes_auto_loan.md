# YES Auto Loan - YES Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
YES Auto Loan offers competitive private-sector car financing with up to 100% on-road funding, speedy digital approvals, and doorstep documentation.

- **Up**: Up to 100% on-road financing for select car models
- **Tenures**: Tenures extending up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Quick**: Quick digital sanction via YES Online and YES Mobile

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: YES Bank Repo Linked Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.90% to 11.50% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried or self-employed with minimum net monthly income of Rs. 25,000; Age 21 to 65 years; CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 1 Crore.
- Margin: 0% to 10%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.00% + GST.
- Prepayment: Permitted as per bank policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Proforma invoice, KYC, 3 months salary slips / 2 years ITR, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Instant delivery order generation.
- Doorstep service.
