# Union Miles Vehicle Loan - Union Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Union Miles is Union Bank of India's premier auto financing facility. It offers attractive repo-linked interest rates, high quantum funding for passenger vehicles and two-wheelers, and zero prepayment penalties.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% of on-road car price
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Special**: Special concessions for electric vehicles

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Union Bank EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.70% to 9.85% p.a. for new four-wheelers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum net annual income: Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 18 to 70 years.
- CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Need-based quantum up to Rs. 5 Crore.
- Margin: 10% to 15% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Cars: Up to 7 years; Two-wheelers: Up to 5 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Waived under festive offers.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, salary slips, Form 16 / ITR, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast approval via Vyom Mobile App.
- Zero foreclosure charges.
