# PSB Auto Loan - Punjab & Sind Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PSB Auto Loan from Punjab & Sind Bank finances new passenger cars and two-wheelers. It offers competitive repo-linked rates and transparent repayment conditions.

- **Up**: Up to 90% on-road financing
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years
- **Zero**: Zero foreclosure charges

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PSB RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.80% to 9.95% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 21 to 65 years.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 1 Crore.
- Margin: 10% to 15% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Waived under festive offers.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quote, KYC, income proof, 6 months bank statement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero prepayment penalties.
- Quick branch sanction.
