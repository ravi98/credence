# ICICI Insta Auto Loan - ICICI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
ICICI Insta Auto Loan offers pre-approved instant car loan sanction letters generated in minutes via iMobile app or NetBanking. Customers can walk into any authorized car showroom and finalize purchase immediately.

- **Instant**: Instant pre-approved sanction letter generated in seconds via iMobile
- **Up**: Up to 100% financing on On-Road price for premier customer segments
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years (84 months)

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: ICICI Repo Linked Lending Benchmark.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.85% to 11.00% p.a. based on CIBIL and customer tier.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Pre-approved ICICI Bank customers or salaried employees with monthly salary Rs. 25,000+.
- Age: 21 to 65 years; CIBIL score 720+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to 100% On-Road price.
- Margin: 0% to 10%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years (84 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 0.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: Permissible after 6 EMIs as per bank policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Paperless for pre-approved customers; standard KYC and income proof for others.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Instant delivery order generation.
- Digital integration with major car dealerships nationwide.
