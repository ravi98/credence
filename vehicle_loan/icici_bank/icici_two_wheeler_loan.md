# ICICI Two-Wheeler Loan - ICICI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
ICICI Two-Wheeler Loan provides instant financing for new scooters, motorcycles, and superbikes. It features spot approvals at authorized dealerships and up to 100% on-road funding.

- **Up**: Up to 100% on-road financing for select customer cohorts
- **Spot**: Spot approval at 4,000+ partner dealerships
- **Tenures**: Tenures up to 48 months

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: ICICI Repo Linked Benchmark.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.50% to 20.00% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Monthly income of Rs. 15,000+; Age 21 to 65 years.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to 100% on-road price (up to Rs. 3 Lakhs).
- Margin: 0% to 15%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 48 months.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 2.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: As per policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quote, KYC, 3 months bank statement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Spot dealer approval.
- Special terms for electric two-wheelers.
