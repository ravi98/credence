# ICICI Used Car Loan - ICICI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
ICICI Used Car Loan finances the purchase of certified pre-owned passenger vehicles. It offers attractive repayment structures, minimal documentation, and fast hypothecation transfer.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 80% - 85% of certified car valuation
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 5 years (60 months)
- **Doorstep**: Doorstep inspection and vehicle document transfer support

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: ICICI Repo Linked Benchmark.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.25% to 15.50% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried or self-employed; Minimum income Rs. 2.50 Lakhs p.a.; CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- Margin: 15% to 20% on valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 5 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 1% to 1.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: As per policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- RC, vehicle insurance, valuation certificate, KYC, income documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Seamless ownership transfer assistance.
- Pre-approved offers on iMobile.
