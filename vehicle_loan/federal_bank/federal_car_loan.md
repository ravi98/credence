# Federal Car Loan - Federal Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Federal Car Loan provides competitive vehicle financing with high on-road funding, transparent charges, and fast digital sanctions through FedMobile.

- **Up**: Up to 100% of ex-showroom / 90% of on-road vehicle cost
- **Repayment**: Repayment tenures up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Zero**: Zero hidden charges with easy digital paperwork

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Federal Bank RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.85% to 11.00% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried or self-employed with minimum net monthly income of Rs. 20,000; Age 21 to 65 years.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Need-based quantum.
- Margin: 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 0.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: As per policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Proforma invoice, KYC, salary slips / ITR, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Digital sanction via FedMobile.
- NRI car loan facility available.
