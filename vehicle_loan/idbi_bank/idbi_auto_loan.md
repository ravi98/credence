# IDBI Auto Loan - IDBI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IDBI Auto Loan provides low repo-linked rates, up to 90% on-road funding, and tenures up to 7 years for new passenger car purchases.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% of On-Road price
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Zero**: Zero foreclosure charges and competitive repo-linked rates

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IDBI Bank RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.80% to 9.95% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 21 to 65 years; CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 2 Crore.
- Margin: 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Waived under festive offers.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, salary slips / ITRs, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast approval via IDBI GO Mobile+.
- Zero prepayment penalty.
