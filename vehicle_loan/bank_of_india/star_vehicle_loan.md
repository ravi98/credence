# Star Vehicle Loan - Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
BOI Star Vehicle Loan finances new passenger cars, SUVs, two-wheelers, and electric vehicles. It offers high loan-to-value ratios, daily reducing interest balances, and zero prepayment penalties.

- **Up**: Up to 90% financing on On-Road price of the car
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years for four-wheelers and 5 years for two-wheelers
- **Zero**: Zero advance EMI and zero prepayment penalties

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BOI RBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.75% to 9.85% p.a. for four-wheelers; 11.50% - 14.00% for two-wheelers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 21 to 65 years.
- CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 5 Crore for cars; up to Rs. 2 Lakhs for two-wheelers.
- Margin: 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Cars: Up to 7 years; Two-wheelers: Up to 5 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Waived under festive offers; regular Rs. 1,000 + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer invoice, KYC, salary slips / ITRs, 6 months bank statement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Digital sanction via BOI Omni Neo App.
- Zero foreclosure charges.
