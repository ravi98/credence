# HDFC Two-Wheeler Loan - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Two-Wheeler Loan offers 100% on-road financing for scooters, commuter motorcycles, electric two-wheelers, and high-end superbikes with minimal documentation.

- **Up**: Up to 100% on-road financing for select customer profiles
- **10-second**: 10-second approval for pre-approved HDFC Bank customers
- **Tenures**: Tenures up to 48 months

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Benchmark Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.50% to 20.00% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum monthly income of Rs. 15,000; Age 21 to 65 years.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to 100% on-road price (up to Rs. 3 Lakhs; higher for superbikes).
- Margin: 0% to 15%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 48 months (4 years).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 2.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: As per policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealership quotation, KYC, 3 months bank statements, salary slip.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Instant approval at 5,000+ two-wheeler dealerships across India.
- Special rates for women riders.
