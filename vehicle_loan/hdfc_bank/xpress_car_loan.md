# HDFC Xpress Car Loan - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Xpress Car Loan is India's first end-to-end 100% digital car loan platform. It allows both existing and new customers to get vehicle loan sanction and disbursement to dealers in under 30 minutes.

- **End-to-end**: End-to-end 30-minute digital disbursement directly to authorized car dealers
- **Up**: Up to 100% financing on On-Road price for select pre-approved profiles
- **Paperless**: Paperless journey powered by NetBanking and Aadhaar e-KYC

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Benchmark Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.85% to 11.25% p.a. based on applicant segment and credit score.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried (min monthly salary Rs. 25,000) or Self-Employed (ITR Rs. 3 Lakhs+).
- Age: 21 to 60 years (salaried) / 65 years (self-employed).
- Minimum CIBIL score 720+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to 100% On-Road price (up to Rs. 50 Lakhs digitally; higher via branch).
- Margin: 0% to 10%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Flexible tenures from 12 to 84 months (1 to 7 years).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 0.50% of loan amount + GST.
- Foreclosure: 3% to 5% after 6 months as per bank policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Digital journey: Aadhaar KYC, NetBanking/statement upload, dealer selection.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- 30-minute disbursal guarantee.
- Integrated with over 1,000 car dealership networks across India.
