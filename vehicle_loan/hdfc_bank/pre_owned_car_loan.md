# HDFC Pre-Owned Car Loan - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Pre-Owned Car Loan provides hassle-free financing for purchasing certified second-hand cars or refinancing existing owned cars with flexible tenures and swift processing.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 80% - 85% of certified used car valuation
- **Tenures**: Tenures up to 5 years (60 months)
- **Doorstep**: Doorstep document collection and quick transfer assistance

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Benchmark Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.00% to 15.50% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Salaried or self-employed with stable income.
- CIBIL score 700+; Vehicle age should not exceed 10 years at loan maturity.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- Margin: 15% to 20% on valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 5 years (60 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 1% to 1.50% + GST.
- Foreclosure: Applicable as per policy.

## 7. Required Documents
- Vehicle RC copy, insurance, seller NOC, valuation report, KYC, income documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast RC transfer support.
- Top-up loan available on existing used car loans.
