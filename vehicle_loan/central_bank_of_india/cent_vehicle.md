# Cent Vehicle Loan - Central Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Cent Vehicle is Central Bank of India's auto financing scheme offering low repo-linked rates, up to 90% on-road price financing, and simple documentation.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% on-road price
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Zero**: Zero foreclosure charges

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Central Bank RBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.75% to 9.85% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Net annual income of Rs. 2.40 Lakhs to Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 21 to 65 years.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore for cars; up to Rs. 2 Lakhs for two-wheelers.
- Margin: 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Waived under festive offers.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quote, KYC, income proof, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Sanction via Cent Mobile app.
- Zero prepayment penalty.
