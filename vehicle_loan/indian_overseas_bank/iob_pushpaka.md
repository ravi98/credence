# IOB Pushpaka Vehicle Loan - Indian Overseas Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IOB Pushpaka is Indian Overseas Bank's dedicated vehicle loan scheme providing credit assistance for personal cars, two-wheelers, and commercial transport.

- **Up**: Up to 90% on-road financing
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 84 months
- **Zero**: Zero foreclosure penalty

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IOB RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.80% to 9.95% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs; Age 21 to 65 years.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 2 Crore.
- Margin: 10% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Waived under campaigns.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, salary slips / ITRs, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast branch and online sanctions.
- Zero prepayment charges.
