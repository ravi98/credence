# PNB Saarthi Car Loan - Punjab National Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Saarthi is Punjab National Bank's comprehensive car finance scheme. It offers low floating interest rates linked to Repo Rate, high on-road financing, and customer-friendly repayment structures.

- **Financing**: Financing up to 90% of on-road car price
- **Tenure**: Tenure up to 7 years (84 months)
- **Special**: Special concessions for electric and green vehicles

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.75% to 9.85% p.a.
- **Concession**: 0.25% concession for corporate salary account holders.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum net annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs.
- Age: 21 to 65 years.
- CIBIL score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Need-based quantum without cap.
- Margin: 10% to 15% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 7 years (84 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Waived under festive offers; regular 0.25% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, salary slips, Form 16, 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- In-principle sanction via PNB ONE App.
- Zero prepayment penalties.
