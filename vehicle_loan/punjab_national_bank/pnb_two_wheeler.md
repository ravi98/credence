# PNB Two-Wheeler Loan - Punjab National Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Two-Wheeler Loan finances the acquisition of new motorcycles, scooters, and mopeds for personal transportation with affordable interest rates and low monthly installments.

- **Funding**: Funding up to 85% of on-road cost
- **Repayment**: Repayment tenure up to 48 months
- **Hassle-free**: Hassle-free dealer-level processing

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 11.65% to 14.90% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Minimum monthly income of Rs. 15,000; Age 21 to 65 years.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 2 Lakhs.
- Margin: 15% on on-road price.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Up to 4 years (48 months).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 1% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Dealer quotation, KYC, salary slips, 3 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero foreclosure charges.
- Available across all PNB branches.
