# Kotak Smart EMI Personal Loan - Kotak Mahindra Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Kotak Smart EMI Personal Loan is a digital-first personal credit product from Kotak Mahindra Bank. It provides immediate liquidity with structured, flexible monthly installments tailored to manage high-value lifestyle upgrades, electronic gadgets, household renovation, travel, or debt consolidation.

- **Instant Disbursal via Kotak811**: Pre-approved account holders can receive immediate loan credit in under 5 minutes through the Kotak mobile banking app.
- **High Borrowing Limits**: Credit sanctions available up to Rs. 40 Lakhs (and up to Rs. 1 Crore for executive corporate tiers).
- **100% Paperless Application**: Leverages digital verification, Account Aggregator, and e-NACH mandates.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Kotak Benchmark Lending Rate.
- **Floating/Fixed Rates**: 10.99% to 19.99% p.a. (Determined by credit score, employer category, and net monthly income).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Confirmed salaried employees of MNCs, Listed Corporates, Government/PSUs, and Private Ltd companies.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 58 years (or retirement age).
- **Minimum Net Monthly Salary**: Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 30,000 per month (Rs. 20,000 for select tier-2 locations).
- **Work Vintage**: Minimum 1 year of continuous employment.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+ (750+ secures lowest rate tier).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 40 Lakhs (up to Rs. 1 Crore for select premier profiles).
- **Multiplier**: Up to 15 to 20 times the Net Monthly Salary.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 72 months (1 to 6 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Kotak savings/salary account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 2.50% to 3.00% of loan amount + GST. Regularly discounted during festive campaigns.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure**: Permissible after 12 EMIs with applicable charges as per bank agreement.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employment Proof**: Corporate ID card.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Completely collateral-free without third-party guarantee.
- Fast-track digital underwriting with instant sanction letter generation.
