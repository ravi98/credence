# Kotak Medical Emergency Loan - Kotak Mahindra Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Kotak Medical Emergency Personal Loan is a specialized personal credit facility designed to provide emergency liquidity for urgent hospital admissions, surgeries, critical illness therapies, specialized medications, and unexpected post-operative rehabilitation expenses that exceed insurance coverage limits.

- **Priority Expedited Processing**: Prioritized digital underwriting ensures that funds reach the borrower's account within hours to meet hospital admission deadlines.
- **Covers Uninsured Medical Shortfalls**: Bridges the critical gap between hospital bills and health insurance room-rent sub-limits, co-payments, or non-covered consumables.
- **Unrestricted Medical Utilization**: Can be used for patient hospitalization, diagnostic tests, air-ambulance transfers, overseas medical care, or home nursing setups.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Kotak Benchmark Lending Rate.
- **Floating/Fixed Rates**: 10.99% to 16.50% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried employees and self-employed individuals.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 58 years (salaried) and 65 years (self-employed).
- **Minimum Net Monthly Income**: Rs. 25,000 per month.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 25 Lakhs based on treatment estimates and applicant repaying capacity.
- **Collateral**: 100% Unsecured; zero collateral required.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 60 months (1 to 5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Kotak account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.50% to 2.50% + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: Standard terms as per bank agreement after 12 months.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips / 2 years' ITR, 6 months' bank statements.
- **Medical Documentation (Optional/Surrogate)**: Hospital estimate letter / admission advice (if requesting expedited medical sanction override).

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Immediate financial relief preventing distress sale of investments or family gold.
- Seamless mobile application process directly from hospital premises.
