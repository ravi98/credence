# HDFC Bank Marriage Loan - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Bank Marriage / Wedding Personal Loan is an unsecured retail personal finance facility tailored to assist individuals and families in funding significant wedding-related expenses without liquidating long-term investments, fixed deposits, or mutual funds.

- **Complete Wedding Expense Coverage**: Unrestricted utilization covering banquet venue deposits, catering, jewellery purchases, bridal apparel, photography, event management, and honeymoon travel.
- **Fast Disbursals**: Instant pre-approved disbursals in seconds for existing account holders, and 24 to 48 hours for new applicants.
- **Joint Family Borrowing**: Option to add parents, fiancé, or spouse as co-borrower to enhance total loan eligibility.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Benchmark Rate.
- **Interest Slabs**: 10.50% to 18.50% p.a., determined by applicant CIBIL score and employer corporate category.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Permanent salaried employees and self-employed professionals.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
- **Minimum Net Income**: Rs. 25,000 net monthly salary.
- **Employment Stability**: Minimum 2 years of overall employment (at least 1 year with current employer).
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+ preferred.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 40 Lakhs based on repayment ability.
- **Collateral**: 100% Unsecured; zero collateral or guarantor required.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 84 months (1 to 7 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions from HDFC Bank account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 2.50% + GST.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure Terms**: Part-prepayment permissible after 12 EMIs (up to 25% of principal once a year).

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Driving License.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employment Proof**: Company ID card.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast digital processing eliminates wedding stress.
- Option to select flexible pocket-friendly EMIs to preserve post-wedding cash flow.
