# HDFC Loan for Professionals - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Personal Loan for Professionals is an unsecured, collateral-free credit solution specifically tailored for practicing self-employed professionals such as Doctors, Chartered Accountants, Architects, and Company Secretaries. It provides growth capital and personal liquidity without demanding hypothecation of clinical equipment or commercial mortgages.

- **Specialized Professional Limits**: Unsecured limits up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for Doctors (MBBS/MD/MS) and up to Rs. 35–40 Lakhs for practicing CAs.
- **Multipurpose Flexibility**: Funds can be utilized for personal lifestyle expenses, clinic/office renovation, software/tech upgrades, or practice working capital.
- **Simplified Balance Sheet Evaluation**: Underwriting focuses on professional pedigree, qualification vintage, and gross receipts rather than rigid asset-heavy ratios.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Benchmark Rate.
- **Fixed/Floating Rates**: 11.00% to 15.50% p.a. (Special lower spreads for medical specialists with 5+ years of practice).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**:
  - Medical Professionals: Doctors with MBBS, BDS, MD, MS, MDS degrees registered with MCI/DCI.
  - Financial Professionals: Chartered Accountants (CA) with active Certificate of Practice (COP) from ICAI.
  - Architects registered with Council of Architecture (COA).
  - Company Secretaries (CS) registered with ICSI.
- **Post-Qualification Experience (PQE)**: Minimum 4 to 5 years of active post-qualification practice.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 25 years; Maximum 65 years at loan maturity.
- **Business Financials**: Minimum net annual income of Rs. 1 Lakh+ with profitable operations for the preceding 2 consecutive years.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 720+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Doctors**: Rs. 2 Lakhs up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- **Chartered Accountants / Architects**: Rs. 2 Lakhs up to Rs. 35 Lakhs.
- **Collateral**: Nil (100% Unsecured).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 60 months (1 to 5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions from primary professional current/savings account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.50% to 2.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: Standard foreclosure terms after 12 months.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Professional Proof**: Degree certificate, Medical Council / ICAI / COA registration certificate, and Certificate of Practice.
- **Financial Proof**: 2 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts with computation sheets, and 6 to 12 months' operative bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep relationship management and dedicated professional priority desk.
- Option to bundle with specialized commercial equipment or clinic premises financing.
