# HDFC Regular Personal Loan - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Regular Personal Loan is India's leading private sector unsecured personal financing facility. Designed for speed, convenience, and absolute transparency, it provides funding for immediate personal liquidity needs such as debt consolidation, family celebrations, vacations, home improvements, or emergency hospitalization.

- **10-Second In-Principle Disbursals**: Existing pre-approved HDFC Bank customers can receive funds credited directly into their accounts in just 10 seconds via NetBanking or PayZapp.
- **High Unsecured Quantum**: Standard funding up to Rs. 40 Lakhs, with premium corporate cohorts eligible for up to Rs. 75 Lakhs.
- **Clean Collateral-Free Facility**: 100% unsecured without demanding collateral, mortgage, or third-party guarantors.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Benchmark Rate.
- **Fixed/Floating Rates**: 10.50% to 21.00% p.a., determined by applicant CIBIL score, corporate employer classification (Super A, Cat A, Cat B, Cat C), and monthly net salary.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Permanent salaried employees of Central/State Govt, PSUs, Reputed Public/Private Ltd Firms, and MNCs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
- **Minimum Net Monthly Salary**: Rs. 25,000 per month (Rs. 50,000+ for Tier-1 metro borrowers).
- **Service Vintage**: Minimum 2 years of overall employment (at least 1 year with current employer).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+ (750+ secures lowest rate tier).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 40 Lakhs (up to Rs. 75 Lakhs for select executives).
- **Multiplier**: Up to 15 to 20 times the Net Monthly Salary.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 84 months (1 to 7 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions from HDFC Bank account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 2.50% of the loan amount + GST (Min Rs. 1,999, Max Rs. 25,000). Regularly discounted during festive offers.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure Terms**: Part-prepayment permissible up to 25% of principal once a year after 12 EMIs; foreclosure charges up to 2% to 4% after 12 months.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Driving License.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employment Proof**: Company ID card and official email authentication.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- 24x7 instant approval via HDFC NetBanking, MobileBanking, and WhatsApp banking.
- Personal Accident Cover and Credit Protect insurance optional bundles.
