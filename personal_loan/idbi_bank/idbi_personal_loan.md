# IDBI Personal Loan Scheme - IDBI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IDBI Personal Loan Scheme is an unsecured consumer credit facility designed to assist individuals in funding immediate personal, family, or domestic obligations such as weddings, medical contingencies, holiday travel, home furnishing, or educational fees.

- **Unsecured Credit**: No collateral, hypothecation, or mortgage required.
- **Convenient Repayment Schedules**: Repayment tenures from 12 to 60 months.
- **Fixed/Floating Transparency**: Clear interest rate disclosures tied to benchmark rates.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IDBI Benchmark Lending Rate.
- **Interest Slabs**: 11.00% to 14.75% p.a. (Determined by credit score and employer category).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried individuals working with Central/State Govt, PSUs, and reputed corporate firms, as well as self-employed professionals.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
- **Minimum Income**: Rs. 20,000 per month.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 25,000 up to Rs. 5 Lakhs (up to Rs. 10 Lakhs for select corporate salary account holders).
- **Multiplier**: Up to 10 to 15 times the Net Monthly Salary.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 60 months (1 to 5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via IDBI Bank savings account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL on floating rate options.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Straightforward personal credit lines without collateral requirements.
- Prompt processing for customers with established IDBI Bank banking relationships.
