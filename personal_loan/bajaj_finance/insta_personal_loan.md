# Bajaj Finserv Insta Personal Loan - Bajaj Finance

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Bajaj Finserv Insta Personal Loan is an instant pre-approved personal credit facility offering ultra-fast cash disbursals to customers. By entering a mobile number and OTP, eligible customers can check pre-assigned credit limits and complete the disbursement process without lengthy paperwork.

- **Instant Disbursal**: Loan funds disbursed into customer bank account in 30 minutes to 4 hours post-verification.
- **Pre-Assigned Credit Limits**: Pre-approved offers generated using algorithmic underwriting based on past financial history.
- **Minimal or Zero Physical Documentation**: Digital journey supported by paperless verification, Aadhaar OTP authentication, and automated bank verification.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Bajaj Finance Internal Cost of Funds and Risk Grading.
- **Annual Percentage Rate**: 11.00% to 28.00% p.a. depending on credit score, risk bracket, and existing relationship.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried employees of private/public organizations, self-employed professionals, and business owners.
- **Age Bounds**: 21 to 67 years.
- **Nationality**: Indian citizen residing in India.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL score of 685 (preferred 720+).
- **Employment / Income**: Stable income proof required for non-pre-approved users; pre-approved users require no additional income proof.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: From Rs. 20,000 up to Rs. 12.76 Lakhs for standard instant offers (up to Rs. 40 Lakhs for select verified profiles).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures ranging from 12 months up to 63 months (extendable up to 5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: e-NACH auto-debit, standing instructions, and Bajaj Finserv App payment options.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 3.93% of the sanctioned loan amount + applicable GST.
- **Foreclosure / Part Pre-Payment**: Part-prepayment or complete pre-closure allowed after first EMI clearance as per NBFC policy and RBI guidelines.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, or Passport.
- **Income Proof**: Last 3 months' bank statements and recent salary slip (only if additional limit over pre-approved offer is demanded).
- **Banking**: Bank account verification via NetBanking or penny drop.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Direct verification with just mobile number and OTP.
- 100% digital paperless processing via Bajaj Finserv website and app.
- Transparent pre-approved loan amounts displayed upfront.
