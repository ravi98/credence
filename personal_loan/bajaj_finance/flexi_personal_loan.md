# Bajaj Finserv Flexi Personal Loan - Bajaj Finance

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Bajaj Finserv Flexi Personal Loan is an innovative overdraft-style credit facility that offers borrowers a pre-approved loan limit. Unlike conventional term loans where interest is charged on the entire sanctioned principal, the Flexi facility charges interest solely on the amount withdrawn and utilized, with options to withdraw, prepay, and re-borrow multiple times without incurring part-prepayment penalties.

- **Interest Only on Utilized Amount**: Significant savings in interest costs as interest is calculated daily on the drawn balance.
- **Multiple Withdrawals and Prepayments**: Borrowers can draw funds in multiple tranches and deposit excess surplus cash at will.
- **Interest-Only EMI Option**: Option to pay only the interest component as monthly EMIs for the initial part of the tenure, reducing monthly financial outflow by up to 45%.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Bajaj Finance Internal Cost of Funds and Base Lending Rate.
- **Annual Percentage Rate**: 11.00% to 24.00% p.a. depending on borrower credit profile, income tier, and employer categorization.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried individuals working with MNCs, public/private corporations, and self-employed professionals (doctors, CAs, consultants).
- **Age Bounds**: 21 to 67 years.
- **Minimum Monthly Salary**: Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 35,000 depending on location (metro vs non-metro).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL score of 685 (720+ preferred).
- **Work Vintage**: Minimum 1 year in current job / 2 years total work experience.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Minimum Rs. 1 Lakh up to Rs. 40 Lakhs based on repayment capacity and income.
- **Variants**: Flexi Hybrid Loan (interest-only initial period followed by principal+interest) and Flexi Term Loan.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures ranging from 12 months up to 96 months (8 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: e-NACH auto-debit, customer portal withdrawals/deposits, net banking.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 3.93% of the sanctioned credit facility + GST.
- **Annual Maintenance Charge (AMC)**: Nominal fee applicable on Flexi facility limits.
- **Part-Prepayment / Foreclosure**: Zero charges for part-prepayment or prepayments deposited into the Flexi account.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Driving License.
- **Income Proof**: Last 3 months' salary slips, Form 16, latest 3 to 6 months bank account statements.
- **Proof of Employment**: Employee ID card and official email verification.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Unlimited withdrawals up to the assigned credit limit via customer self-service portal / app.
- Prepayment with zero extra penalty.
- Helps maintain liquidity during emergencies or unpredictable financial requirements.
