# Federal Bank FedPremia Personal Loan - Federal Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
FedPremia Personal Loan is Federal Bank's premium personal credit product structured specifically for high-income salaried individuals and executives. It offers collateral-free financing for a wide spectrum of lifestyle, domestic, emergency, or financial obligations.

- **High Credit Quantum**: Funding up to Rs. 25 Lakhs without hypothecating any physical assets or providing third-party guarantors.
- **Fast-Track Digital Disbursal**: End-to-end digital verification and instant in-principle approval via FedMobile app.
- **Transparent Terms**: Competitive rates and zero hidden charges for premium salary relationship holders.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Federal Bank Benchmark Lending Rate.
- **Floating/Fixed Rates**: 11.49% to 18.99% p.a., determined by applicant CIBIL tier and employer corporate category.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Permanent salaried employees of Central/State Govt, PSUs, Reputed Public/Private Ltd Firms, and MNCs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
- **Minimum Net Monthly Salary**: Rs. 25,000 per month (higher thresholds for metro corporate profiles).
- **Service Stability**: Minimum 2 years of overall employment (at least 1 year with current employer).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+ (750+ preferred for best terms).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 25 Lakhs.
- **Multiplier**: Up to 15 to 20 times the Net Monthly Salary.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 60 months (1 to 5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Federal Bank salary/savings account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% to 2.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: Standard terms as per bank agreement after 12 months.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Driving License.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employment Proof**: Corporate employee identity card.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- 100% paperless onboarding for pre-qualified Federal Bank salary package holders.
- Straightforward balance transfer options from existing non-banking lenders.
