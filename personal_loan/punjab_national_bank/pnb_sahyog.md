# PNB Sahyog Personal Loan Scheme - Punjab National Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Sahyog Personal Loan Scheme is a dedicated unsecured personal credit solution offered by Punjab National Bank to provide emergency liquidity and financial support to permanent salaried employees of government departments, public sector undertakings, autonomous institutions, and recognized private companies.

- **Multipurpose Financial Support**: Unrestricted end-use covering weddings, medical crises, domestic/overseas vacations, educational fees, or home furnishings.
- **Clean Unsecured Sanction**: No tangible security, mortgage, or property collateral required.
- **Structured Check-Off Facility**: Available under check-off tie-ups where employer directly deducts EMIs from monthly salary payments, securing concessional rates.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB RLLR (Repo Linked).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - With Salary Check-Off Facility: 10.75% - 12.25% p.a.
  - Without Check-Off (Direct Standing Instruction): 11.75% - 14.25% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Confirmed permanent salaried employees with minimum 2 years of continuous service (at least 1 year with current employer).
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum retirement age (typically 58 to 60 years).
- **Minimum Net Take-Home Salary**: Must maintain minimum 40% of gross pay after all statutory deductions and proposed EMI.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Up to 15 to 20 times the Net Monthly Salary, subject to a cap of Rs. 15 Lakhs to Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Amount**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 60 months (5 years).
- **Repayment Mode**: Check-off deduction through employer DDO, or automated Standing Instruction from PNB salary account.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Government / Corporate ID Card.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employer Undertaking**: Check-off mandate letter from employer Drawing and Disbursing Officer (DDO).

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Express processing for employees maintaining salary accounts with PNB.
- Zero collateral requirement.
