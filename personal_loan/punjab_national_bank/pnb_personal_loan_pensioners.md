# PNB Personal Loan Scheme for Pensioners - Punjab National Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Personal Loan Scheme for Pensioners is a specialized credit program designed to meet the immediate personal, medical, family, or travel financial requirements of retired defense personnel, government pensioners, and family pensioners who draw their monthly pension directly through PNB branches.

- **Zero Processing Fees**: Complete 100% waiver of processing charges.
- **Nil Prepayment Penalties**: Borrowers can pay off the loan early at any time without fees.
- **Generous Age Allowance**: Repayment permissible up to 78 years of age.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB RLLR.
- **Interest Slabs**:
  - Defense Pensioners: 10.65% - 11.25% p.a.
  - Civil / State / PSU Pensioners: 11.15% - 11.75% p.a.
  - Family Pensioners: 11.65% - 12.15% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Central Govt, State Govt, Defense Forces, Paramilitary, and PSU pensioners drawing monthly pension via PNB.
- **Age Bounds**: Up to 75+ years at sanction (full loan liquidation by age 78).
- **Mandate**: PPO lodged with PNB and irrevocable instruction not to switch pension account.
- **Guarantee**: Spouse eligible for family pension joins as co-borrower/guarantor, or guarantee from adult earning children.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Up to 70 Years**: Up to 18x Net Monthly Pension (20x for Defense pensioners), capped at Rs. 10 Lakhs.
- **Above 70 up to 75 Years**: Up to 18x Net Monthly Pension (20x for Defense pensioners), capped at Rs. 7.50 Lakhs.
- **Above 75 Years**: Up to 12 months' pension amount, capped at Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Sanction**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & EMI Caps
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 60 months (5 years) or age 78 years; capped at 24 months for pensioners above 75 years.
- **Net Monthly Pension Cap**: EMI capped within 50% of monthly pension.
- **Repayment Mode**: Auto-debit from pension account on 1st of every month.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: NIL (Fully waived).
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Pension Records**: Copy of Pension Payment Order (PPO), Pension passbook, and last 3 months' pension credits.
- **Undertaking**: Irrevocable standing instruction / non-transfer mandate form.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Immediate financial relief for senior citizens with minimal paperwork.
- Automated monthly debit removes any risk of overdue interest or missed payments.
