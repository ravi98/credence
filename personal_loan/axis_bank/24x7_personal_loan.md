# Axis Bank 24x7 Personal Loan - Axis Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Axis Bank 24x7 Personal Loan is a completely digital, automated pre-approved lending service available around the clock to pre-selected Axis Bank customers via Axis Mobile app, Open by Axis Bank, and NetBanking.

- **Instant Disbursal in Seconds**: Loan amount credited to savings account instantly upon electronic signature verification, 24 hours a day, 365 days a year.
- **Zero Documentation & Zero Branch Visits**: 100% STP (Straight-Through Processing) without uploading physical salary slips or bank statements.
- **Micro & Macro Sizing**: Encompasses variants like 24x7 XS Loans (micro credit up to Rs. 1 Lakh) as well as full-scale pre-approved limits up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Axis Bank Policy Repo Linked Rate.
- **Interest Slabs**: 10.49% to 18.00% p.a. Pre-calculated and customized dynamically based on borrower credit score.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Pre-selected existing Axis Bank savings and salary account holders.
- **Checking Eligibility**: Visible directly in Axis Mobile App loan dashboard, or by SMS sending `PL <Last 4 digits of Account No>` to `5676782`.
- **Account Conduct**: Minimum 6 months of active, satisfactory account turnover without non-sufficient funds (NSF) bounces.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 720+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 25,000 up to Rs. 50 Lakhs (pre-determined based on internal algorithm).
- **Collateral**: 100% Unsecured.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible options from 12 to 60 months (1 to 5 years).
- **Repayment Mode**: Auto-configured standing instruction on the borrower's operative Axis Bank savings account.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% to 1.50% + GST (Discounted or flat fees often available during digital drives).
- **Prepayment Fee**: Standard terms as per digital loan agreement.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Zero Physical Documentation**: Verified through Aadhaar e-Sign / Two-Factor OTP authentication.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Available 24x7 even during late nights, bank holidays, and festive weekends.
- Transparent EMI schedule displayed with full repayment amortization before final confirmation.
