# Axis Bank Instant Personal Loan - Axis Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Axis Bank Instant Personal Loan is a quick-disbursement, collateral-free credit facility tailored to meet sudden personal, domestic, medical, wedding, or travel financing requirements with minimal documentation and expedited underwriting.

- **Fastest Digital Approval**: End-to-end digital processing through Axis Mobile App and net banking portal with swift disbursals.
- **High Unsecured Borrowing**: Funding from Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 40 Lakhs without pledging assets or collateral.
- **Extended Tenure up to 84 Months**: Repayment tenures up to 7 years to reduce the monthly EMI burden.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Axis Bank Policy Repo Linked Benchmark.
- **Fixed/Floating Interest Slabs**: 10.49% to 21.00% p.a., determined by applicant CIBIL score, net monthly income, and employer corporate category.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried individuals and self-employed professionals (Doctors, CAs, Architects).
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
- **Minimum Net Monthly Salary**:
  - Existing Axis Bank Customers: Rs. 15,000 per month.
  - New to Bank Customers: Rs. 25,000 to Rs. 30,000 per month.
- **Work Vintage**: Minimum 2 years of continuous work experience (at least 1 year with current employer).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+ (750+ secures prime rate tier).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 40 Lakhs.
- **Multiplier**: Up to 15 to 20 times the Net Monthly Salary.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 84 months (1 to 7 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Axis Bank account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% to 2.00% + GST.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure Terms**: Part-prepayment permissible after 12 EMIs; foreclosure charges up to 2% to 4% after 12 months.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Driving License.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employment Proof**: Company ID card.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero collateral security required.
- Easy balance transfer facility available to consolidate multiple high-cost credit cards and personal loans.
