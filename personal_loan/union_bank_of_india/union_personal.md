# Union Personal Loan - Union Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Union Personal Loan is an unsecured personal financing facility provided by Union Bank of India. It caters to salaried individuals and self-employed professionals to meet immediate personal funding requirements like family marriages, medical contingencies, educational commitments, holiday travel, or home improvement.

- **Preferential Government Pricing**: Concessional interest rate slabs starting at 10.70% p.a. for Central/State Government and Defense personnel.
- **High Sanction Quantum**: Loans up to Rs. 15 Lakhs (extending up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for select pre-approved salary corporate profiles).
- **Clean Unsecured Facility**: No collateral, mortgage, or third-party guarantee required for salaried applicants with salary accounts.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Union Bank EBLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - Salaried with Check-Off / Tie-up (CIBIL 800+): 10.70% - 11.75% p.a.
  - Salaried without Check-Off: 11.75% - 13.25% p.a.
  - Self-Employed Individuals: 13.25% - 14.85% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Permanent salaried employees of Government, PSUs, Listed Corporates, and established self-employed professionals.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum age up to 1 year prior to retirement (typically 58-60 years).
- **Service Vintage**: Minimum 1 year of continuous confirmed service.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Salaried**: Up to 15 to 20 times the Net Monthly Salary, capped at Rs. 15 Lakhs (up to Rs. 50 Lakhs on digital pre-approved channels).
- **Minimum Sanction**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 60 months (5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions from Union Bank salary account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% to 1.00% + GST. Periodically 100% waived during festive promotions.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 2 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick digital in-principle approval via Union Bank 'Vyom' app.
- Pre-approved personal loans available for pre-selected account holders.
