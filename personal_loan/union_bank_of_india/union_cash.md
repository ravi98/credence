# Union Cash - Union Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Union Cash is a specialized personal loan program offered by Union Bank of India exclusively for pensioners and family pensioners who draw their monthly pension through Union Bank of India branches. It serves to meet unforeseen personal expenses, medical costs, social obligations, or household maintenance.

- **Zero Processing Fees**: Complete fee waiver for all senior citizens and pensioners.
- **Collateral-Free Facility**: No mortgage or tangible collateral required.
- **Simplified Processing**: Rapid disbursement within 24 hours as the bank already maintains pensioner PPO records.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Union Bank EBLR.
- **Floating Rates**: 10.10% to 11.45% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Retired Central Government, State Government, Railways, Defense, and PSU employees, as well as family pensioners receiving monthly pension via Union Bank.
- **Age Bounds**: Up to 75 years of age at sanction (repayment completed before age 78).
- **Mandate**: PPO lodged with Union Bank with irrevocable undertaking not to change pension disbursement account.
- **Guarantee**: Personal guarantee of spouse (or legal heir/nominee).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Age up to 70 Years**: Up to Rs. 3 Lakhs (or 12 times net monthly pension).
- **Age above 70 Years**: Up to Rs. 2 Lakhs (or 10 times net monthly pension).
- **Family Pensioners**: Up to Rs. 1 Lakh.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**:
  - Up to 70 Years: Up to 60 months (5 years).
  - Above 70 Years: Up to 36 months (3 years).
- **Repayment**: Auto-debit from the pension disbursement savings account on pension credit day.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: NIL.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Pension Documents**: Copy of Pension Payment Order (PPO), Pension passbook, and last 3 months' pension credits.
- **Undertaking**: Irrevocable standing instruction / non-transfer mandate form.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick branch disbursal with minimal documentation.
- Hassle-free monthly recovery through automated standing instruction.
