# Saraswat Insta Personal Loan - Saraswat Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Saraswat Insta Personal Loan is an instant personal credit solution provided by Saraswat Bank, India's largest Scheduled Urban Co-operative Bank. It caters to salaried individuals and self-employed professionals to meet immediate emergency funding requirements like medical treatments, festival celebrations, weddings, or consumer asset purchases.

- **Fast Turnaround**: Rapid branch approval and disbursal within 24 to 48 hours.
- **Convenient Eligibility**: Modest income thresholds starting at Rs. 15,000 per month.
- **Co-operative Personalized Touch**: Combines dedicated community branch relationships with digital ease.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Saraswat Bank S-PLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 12.00% to 14.50% p.a. (Determined by credit score and banking relationship).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**:
  - Salaried: Minimum 2 years of overall employment (at least 1 year in current organization).
  - Self-Employed: At least 1 year of business continuity with ITR reflecting gross cash accruals of Rs. 1.5 Lakhs p.a.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
- **Minimum Net Income**: Rs. 15,000 to Rs. 25,000 per month (depending on location).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 25,000 up to Rs. 5 Lakhs based on repayment ability.
- **Collateral**: Nil (Unsecured).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures up to 60 months (5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Saraswat Bank savings account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate individual borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 2 years' ITR, computation of income, 12 months' business bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero third-party guarantor requirement for prime salaried profiles.
- Quick processing and localized branch approvals across Maharashtra, Goa, Karnataka, and Gujarat.
