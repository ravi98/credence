# YES Personal Loan - YES Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
YES Personal Loan is a high-quantum, collateral-free credit facility offered by YES Bank. Designed to cater to high-value personal expenses, it provides funding for destination weddings, luxury travel, medical emergencies, home renovation, or debt consolidation.

- **High Unsecured Borrowing**: Funding up to Rs. 50 Lakhs without pledging property, gold, or securities.
- **Fast Digital Approvals**: Online application journey with provisional in-principle sanction in as little as 60 seconds.
- **Flexible Tenure Options**: Repayment periods ranging from 12 to 60 months.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: YES Bank Policy Repo Linked Benchmark.
- **Interest Slabs**: 10.85% to 21.00% p.a., determined by applicant CIBIL score, corporate employer classification, and monthly net income.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried individuals working with recognized private/public companies, MNCs, and government bodies.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
- **Minimum Net Monthly Salary**: Rs. 25,000 per month.
- **Work Vintage**: Minimum 1 year of continuous employment.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+ (750+ secures lowest rate tier).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 50 Lakhs based on repayment ability.
- **Multiplier**: Up to 15 to 20 times the Net Monthly Salary.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 60 months (1 to 5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via YES Bank account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 2.50% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure Terms**: Permissible after 12 EMIs with applicable charges as per bank agreement.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employment Proof**: Corporate employee identity card.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero collateral security required.
- Easy balance transfer facility available to consolidate multiple high-cost credit cards and personal loans.
