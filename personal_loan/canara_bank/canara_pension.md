# Canara Pension Personal Loan - Canara Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Canara Pension is an empathetic, unsecured credit scheme created by Canara Bank to assist retired senior citizens and family pensioners who receive their monthly pension through Canara Bank. It provides convenient funds to manage medical bills, pilgrimage tours, emergency household repairs, or family expenses.

- **100% Processing Fee Waiver**: Zero processing charges for all pensioners.
- **Zero Prepayment Penalties**: Complete flexibility to pay off the loan early without any penalty.
- **Support for Family Pensioners**: Extends dedicated borrowing limits to surviving spouses drawing family pensions.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Canara Bank RLLR.
- **Floating Rates**: 10.40% to 11.75% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Pensioners of Central Government, State Governments, Railways, Defense, PSUs, and retired employees of Canara Bank.
- **Age Bounds**: Up to 70–75 years at loan sanction (repayment completed before age 78).
- **Mandate**: PPO lodged with Canara Bank with an irrevocable undertaking not to change pension disbursal bank.
- **Guarantee**: Spouse eligible for family pension joins as co-borrower/guarantor.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **General Pensioners**: Up to 15 to 20 months' pension, capped at Rs. 10 Lakhs (scaled by pensioner age).
- **Family Pensioners**: Up to 10 to 12 months' pension, capped at Rs. 3 Lakhs to Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Amount**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 60 months (5 years).
- **Repayment Mode**: Auto-debit on pension credit day from the Canara Bank savings account.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: NIL (Fully waived).
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Pension Records**: Copy of Pension Payment Order (PPO), Pension passbook, and last 3 months' pension credits.
- **Undertaking**: Irrevocable standing instruction / non-transfer mandate form.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick branch disbursal with minimal documentation.
- Ensures elderly individuals have immediate access to funds without liquidating long-term fixed deposits.
