# Canara Budget Personal Loan - Canara Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Canara Budget is the flagship unsecured personal loan scheme from Canara Bank for salaried employees. It offers convenient financial support for household expenditures, medical treatments, marriage expenses, family vacations, educational expenses, or debt consolidation.

- **High Salary Multiplier**: Sanctions up to 25 times the gross monthly salary.
- **Canara Budget Prime & Delight Variants**:
  - Canara Budget Prime: Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs for employees of top-tier corporates, PSUs, and government departments.
  - Canara Budget Delight: Up to Rs. 20 Lakhs for general corporate salaried employees.
- **Flexible Tenures up to 84 Months**: Repayment periods up to 7 years to keep monthly EMIs affordable.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Canara Bank RLLR.
- **Floating Rates**: 9.70% to 15.95% p.a. (Determined by Credit Risk Grade and salary account tie-up).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Confirmed permanent employees of Central/State Govt, PSUs, Reputed Joint Stock Companies, IT/BT firms, and recognized educational institutions.
- **Salary Account Requirement**: Must maintain primary salary account with Canara Bank.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years (or retirement age).
- **Net Take-Home (NTH)**: Minimum 25% (or Rs. 10,000) net take-home pay after deducting proposed loan EMI.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Canara Budget Prime**: Up to 25x gross salary, capped at Rs. 30 Lakhs.
- **Canara Budget Delight**: Up to 25x gross salary, capped at Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Sanction**: Rs. 50,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 84 months (7 years).
- **Repayment**: Direct Standing Instruction debit from the Canara Bank salary account on salary credit date.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% to 1.00% (Min Rs. 1,000, Max Rs. 5,000) + GST. Regularly waived during festive drives.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employment Proof**: Official employee identity card.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick branch in-principle approval within 24–48 hours.
- Completely unsecured with zero collateral or third-party guarantor.
