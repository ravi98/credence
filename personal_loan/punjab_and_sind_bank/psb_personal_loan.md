# PSB Personal Loan Scheme - Punjab & Sind Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PSB Personal Loan Scheme is a retail personal credit product from Punjab & Sind Bank (a premier public sector bank). It assists salaried individuals and pensioners in meeting immediate cash requirements for weddings, medical treatments, holiday travel, children's educational fees, or consumer asset purchases.

- **Clean Unsecured Facility**: Unsecured credit without tangible security or property mortgages.
- **Support for Both Salaried & Pensioners**: Standardized terms covering active employees and retired pensioners drawing pension via PSB.
- **Nil Prepayment Penalties**: Complete waiver of prepayment charges on floating rate personal loans.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PSB-EBLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - Government / Defense / PSU Salaried: 11.25% - 12.50% p.a.
  - Corporate Salaried: 12.50% - 13.50% p.a.
  - Pensioners: 11.50% - 12.25% p.a.
  - General / Other Salaried: 13.25% - 14.00% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Permanent salaried employees of Central/State Government, PSUs, Reputed Corporate Firms, and pensioners maintaining pension accounts with PSB.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years (salaried) and 70–75 years (pensioners).
- **Service Vintage**: Minimum 2 years of continuous employment for salaried.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Salaried**: Up to 18 times the Net Monthly Salary, capped at Rs. 10 Lakhs.
- **Pensioners**: Up to 15 times the Net Monthly Pension, capped at Rs. 5 Lakhs to Rs. 10 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Amount**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 60 months (5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via PSB savings/salary account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% to 1.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Pension Proof**: PPO copy, pension passbook, and last 3 months' pension credits.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero third-party guarantor requirement for government employees with salary account tie-up.
- Responsive customer support across pan-India branch locations.
