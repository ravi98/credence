# SBI Pension Loan - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Pension Loan is a compassionate, dedicated personal credit facility provided by State Bank of India to senior citizens and family pensioners who draw their monthly pension through SBI branches. It is designed to fulfill diverse financial requirements in retirement, such as medical treatment, children's weddings, domestic travel, or house repairs.

- **Insta Pension Loan on YONO**: Select pre-approved pensioners can complete the entire application digitally in 4 clicks on YONO with instant credit.
- **Extended Age Eligibility**: Available to senior citizens up to 76 years of age.
- **Lower EMI Burden**: Strictly caps EMI-to-Pension ratio at 33% to 50% to ensure pensioners retain comfortable living funds.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Linked to SBI 2-Year MCLR / EBLR.
- **Interest Slabs**:
  - Defense Pensioners: 11.15% - 11.50% p.a.
  - Central & State Government Pensioners: 11.30% - 11.75% p.a.
  - Family Pensioners: 11.75% - 12.30% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**:
  - Central Government & State Government Pensioners.
  - Defense Pensioners (Army, Navy, Air Force, Coast Guard, Paramilitary).
  - Corporate / PSU Pensioners maintaining pension drawing account with SBI.
  - Family Pensioners (Spouse receiving authorized family pension).
- **Age Window**: Maximum age up to 76 years at loan sanction (full repayment by age 78).
- **Pension Mandate**: Pension Payment Order (PPO) must be lodged with SBI, accompanied by an irrevocable undertaking not to transfer pension disbursement during the loan tenure.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Defense Pensioners**: Up to 18x Net Monthly Pension, max Rs. 14 Lakhs (up to age 72); Rs. 5 Lakhs (age 72-74); Rs. 2.5 Lakhs (age 74-76).
- **Central / State Govt Pensioners**: Up to 18x Net Monthly Pension, max Rs. 11 Lakhs (up to age 72); Rs. 5 Lakhs (age 72-74); Rs. 2.5 Lakhs (age 74-76).
- **Family Pensioners**: Up to 18x Net Monthly Pension, capped at Rs. 5 Lakhs (up to age 72); Rs. 2.5 Lakhs (age 72-76).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & EMI Caps
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 60 months (5 years) for pensioners up to 72 years; 36 months (3 years) for pensioners above 72 years.
- **Net Monthly Pension Caps**: EMI cannot exceed 50% of pension (33% for family pensioners).
- **Repayment Mode**: Auto-debit from the pension disbursement savings account on the 1st of every month.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of loan amount (Min Rs. 500) + GST. Periodically 100% waived.
- **Guarantor Norm**: Spouse eligible for family pension joins as co-borrower/guarantor; for family pensioners, an adult earning son/daughter or third party provides guarantee.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: 3% on prepaid amount; NIL if renewed through SBI.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Pension Records**: Copy of Pension Payment Order (PPO), Pension passbook, and last 3 months' pension credit statements.
- **Undertaking**: Irrevocable standing instruction / non-transfer mandate form.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Eliminates financial distress for elderly citizens facing unexpected medical expenditures.
- Fully automated standing instruction debit prevents any missed payments or penalties.
