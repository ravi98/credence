# SBI Xpress Credit Personal Loan - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Xpress Credit is India's largest unsecured personal loan scheme by volume, catering exclusively to salaried employees holding a Salary Package account with State Bank of India. It provides instant, paperless liquidity for any legal personal expenditure, including medical emergencies, wedding expenses, travel, home renovation, or debt consolidation.

- **Real-Time Xpress Credit (RTXC)**: Pre-approved salary package holders can avail instant 100% digital disbursement via the SBI YONO app in 4 clicks without branch visit.
- **High Loan Limits**: Sanctions up to 24 times the net monthly income (NMI), capped at Rs. 35 Lakhs (up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for select corporate tiers).
- **Unsecured & Collateral-Free**: No security, guarantor, or hypothecation required.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Linked to SBI 2-Year MCLR or External Benchmark Lending Rate.
- **Floating Interest Slabs (Based on Corporate Category & CIBIL)**:
  - Central/State Govt & Defense (DSP/CSP): 11.15% - 12.30% p.a.
  - Tier-1 Corporate Salaried (CIBIL 800+): 11.30% - 12.60% p.a.
  - General Corporate (CIBIL 750 - 799): 12.30% - 13.50% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 13.50% - 14.30% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Permanent employees of Central/State Govt, Defense Services, Paramilitary Forces, PSUs, Educational Institutions, and Reputed Corporates with SBI Salary Package Account.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 58 years (or retirement age, whichever is earlier).
- **Minimum Net Monthly Income (NMI)**:
  - Government / Defense Employees: Rs. 20,000 per month.
  - Corporate Employees: Rs. 25,000 per month.
- **EMI/NMI Ratio (FOIR)**: Up to 50% to 65% of net monthly income.
- **Service Vintage**: Minimum 1 year continuous service.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Minimum Loan Amount**: Rs. 25,000.
- **Maximum Loan Amount**: Up to 24x Net Monthly Income, subject to a maximum of Rs. 35 Lakhs (up to Rs. 50 Lakhs under RTXC for platinum corporates).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Method
- **Tenure**: Flexible repayment from 6 months up to 84 months (7 years).
- **Repayment Mode**: Automated Standing Instruction (SI) debited directly on salary credit date from the SBI salary package account.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% to 1.50% + GST (Min Rs. 1,000, Max Rs. 15,000). 100% fee waivers frequently provided during festive campaigns.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: 3% on prepaid amount if prepaid within 3 years; zero penalty if account closed through a fresh SBI loan facility.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Income Proof**: Last 3 months' pay slips and Form 16.
- **Bank Statements**: Last 6 months' statement of SBI salary package account.
- **Employment Proof**: Corporate ID card / employer confirmation letter.
- *(Note: Fully paperless with zero document upload for pre-approved customers via YONO).*

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Available 24x7 through YONO with immediate fund transfer to savings account.
- Overdraft variant (SBI Xpress Bandhan) available for revolving credit needs.
