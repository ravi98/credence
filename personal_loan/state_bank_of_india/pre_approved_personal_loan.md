# SBI Pre-Approved Personal Loan (PAPL) - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Pre-Approved Personal Loan (PAPL) is a 100% digital, paperless, algorithmically underwritten instant credit facility extended via the SBI YONO mobile app and OnlineSBI internet banking. Eligible customers are identified through automated analytics of their banking history, salary inflows, and credit bureau behavior.

- **Instant 24x7 Disbursement**: Funds credited into the borrower's savings account in less than 60 seconds with just 4 clicks on YONO.
- **Zero Document Upload**: No physical forms, pay slips, ITRs, or branch visits required.
- **Pre-Determined Loan Quantum**: Loan limit and interest rate are pre-calculated and displayed transparently to the user prior to acceptance.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Linked to SBI MCLR / EBLR.
- **Interest Rate Bracket**: 11.15% to 13.50% p.a. based on customer credit rating tier.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Pre-selected existing SBI savings account or salary account customers.
- **Eligibility Check**: Customers can check eligibility instantly by SMS sending `PAPL <Last 4 digits of Account No>` to `567676` or checking the loan banner in YONO app.
- **Account Track Record**: Minimum 6 months of active, satisfactory account turnover without cheque bounces or overdrawn days.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 720+ evaluated through background bureau pull.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 25,000 up to Rs. 8 Lakhs (up to Rs. 15 Lakhs for high-net-worth salary package tiers).
- **Collateral**: 100% Unsecured; zero collateral or third-party guarantee required.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible options from 6 months to 60 months (5 years).
- **Repayment Mode**: Auto-configured standing instruction on the borrower's operative SBI savings account.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Nil or flat nominal fee (Rs. 500 to Rs. 1,000) during festival campaigns; standard fee up to 1.00% + GST.
- **Prepayment Penalties**: Nil or standard 3% as per bank guidelines.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Zero Documentation**: 100% STP (Straight-Through-Processing). Authentication completed via two-factor OTP on registered mobile number.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including bank holidays and weekends.
- Transparent EMI schedule displayed with full repayment amortization before final confirmation.
