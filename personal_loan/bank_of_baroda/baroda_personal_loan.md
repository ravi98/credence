# Baroda Personal Loan - Bank of Baroda

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Baroda Personal Loan is Bank of Baroda's flagship unsecured retail credit facility. It provides hassle-free funds for diverse personal requirements including weddings, medical contingencies, overseas or domestic holiday travel, home furnishing, education expenses, or debt consolidation.

- **High Loan Limits**: Funding up to Rs. 20 Lakhs for salaried and self-employed applicants in metro and urban locations.
- **BRLLR-Linked Transparency**: Interest rates are transparently tied to the Baroda Repo Linked Lending Rate with monthly resets.
- **Zero Prepayment Penalty**: Prepay or foreclose floating rate loans at any time with zero penalty.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BRLLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs (Based on CIBIL & Employer Category)**:
  - Government / Defense / PSU Salaried: 10.40% - 11.90% p.a.
  - Listed Corporates (CIBIL 750+): 11.40% - 12.90% p.a.
  - General Corporate & Self-Employed: 12.90% - 15.65% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**:
  - Salaried employees with minimum 1 year of continuous service.
  - Self-employed professionals (Doctors, CAs, Architects) and businessmen with 2 years of profitable track record.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years (salaried) and 65 years (self-employed).
- **Minimum Net Income**: Rs. 20,000 per month for salaried; Rs. 2 Lakhs annual profit for self-employed.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 701+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Metro & Urban Centers**: Up to Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- **Semi-Urban & Rural Centers**: Up to Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- **Multipliers**: Up to 15 to 20 times the Net Monthly Income.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 60 to 72 months (5 to 6 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Bank of Baroda savings account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% to 2.00% (Min Rs. 1,000, Max Rs. 10,000) + GST. Regularly waived during quarterly festive campaigns.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: NIL for individual floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 2 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts, 12 months' business bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick branch turnaround with minimal documentation.
- Available for both existing Bank of Baroda account holders and new-to-bank customers.
