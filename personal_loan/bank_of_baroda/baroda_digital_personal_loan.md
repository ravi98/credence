# Baroda Digital Personal Loan - Bank of Baroda

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Baroda Digital Personal Loan (marketed under the 'bob Insta Personal Loan' umbrella) is an end-to-end digital lending platform enabling customers to apply, authenticate, and receive personal loan funds directly into their account within minutes through bob World mobile banking or the bank's digital lending web portal.

- **100% Paperless STP Journey**: No branch visits, physical paperwork, or courier documents required.
- **Account Aggregator & Video KYC**: Leverages RBI's Account Aggregator framework and DigiLocker for instant automated income and identity verification.
- **Digital Balance Transfer**: Option to digitally transfer existing high-cost personal loans from other banks up to Rs. 20 Lakhs with NIL processing fees.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BRLLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 10.65% to 14.50% p.a. (Determined by automated credit evaluation).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Customer Profiles**:
  - Existing to Bank (ETB) customers maintaining an active savings account.
  - New to Bank (NTB) / New to Credit (NTC) customers with valid digital KYC.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 57 years (salaried) and 65 years (self-employed).
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 701+ strictly preferred.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **bob Insta Personal Loan (ETB)**: Up to Rs. 15 Lakhs.
- **Digital Personal Loan (NTB)**: Up to Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- **Digital Balance Transfer**: Up to Rs. 20 Lakhs.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 60 months (1 to 5 years).
- **Repayment**: Automated e-NACH / digital mandate on the borrower's operative bank account.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 2% of loan amount (Max Rs. 15,000) + GST. NIL for digital balance transfers.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Digital Verification**: Aadhaar OTP verification via DigiLocker, PAN card validation, and online net-banking/Account Aggregator statement access.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Immediate disbursal to bank account available 24x7 through bob World app.
- Transparent fee disclosure and instant digital loan sanction letter generation.
