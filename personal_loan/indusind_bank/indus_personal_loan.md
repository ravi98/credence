# Indus Personal Loan - IndusInd Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Indus Personal Loan is an unsecured retail personal credit solution offered by IndusInd Bank. Designed with a digital-first philosophy, it enables borrowers to secure immediate funds for personal commitments including weddings, home renovation, travel, medical treatment, or debt consolidation without pledging any security.

- **Instant Online Sanction**: Fast-track digital application with automated income verification via Account Aggregator and Video KYC.
- **Unsecured & Collateral-Free**: No security, mortgage, or guarantor required.
- **Competitive Pricing**: Starting rates from 10.49% p.a. for borrowers with clean credit histories.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IndusInd Benchmark Lending Rate.
- **Floating/Fixed Rates**: 10.49% to 18.50% p.a. (Determined by credit score, employer category, and net monthly income).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried employees and self-employed professionals/business owners.
- **Age Bounds**:
  - Salaried: 21 to 60 years.
  - Self-Employed: 25 to 65 years.
- **Minimum Net Income**:
  - Salaried: Rs. 25,000 net monthly salary.
  - Self-Employed: Rs. 4.8 Lakhs annual post-tax income.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+ (760+ secures optimal rates).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 30,000 up to Rs. 15 Lakhs (up to Rs. 25 Lakhs for select corporate profiles).
- **Multiplier**: Up to 15 to 20 times the Net Monthly Salary.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 60 months (1 to 5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via IndusInd Bank account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 2.50% to 3.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: Permissible after 12 EMIs with applicable charges as per loan agreement.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 2 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep concierge service for offline applicants.
- Seamless balance transfer options to lower monthly debt commitments.
