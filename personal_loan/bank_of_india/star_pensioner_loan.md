# BOI Star Pensioner Loan - Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
BOI Star Pensioner Loan is an exclusive credit facility offered by Bank of India to senior citizens and family pensioners whose monthly pension is credited directly into their Bank of India accounts. It provides financial assistance for medical emergencies, pilgrimage/travel, house repairs, or family social obligations.

- **Zero Processing Charges**: Complete fee exemption for all senior citizens and pensioners.
- **Both Clean and Secured Options**:
  - Clean (Unsecured): Up to 15 times net monthly pension.
  - Secured (against Term Deposit / LIC policy): Up to 20 times net monthly pension.
- **Generous Age Limit**: Repayment permissible up to age 75 years.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BOI RBLR.
- **Fixed/Floating Rate**: Starts at 11.15% to 11.85% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Central Govt, State Govt, Armed Forces, and PSU pensioners drawing pension via BOI.
- **Age Window**: Up to 75 years at the time of final loan repayment.
- **Mandate**: PPO lodged with BOI and irrevocable undertaking not to switch pension bank account.
- **Guarantee**: Spouse eligible for family pension joins as co-borrower/guarantor.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Clean Variant**: Up to 15x Net Monthly Pension, capped at Rs. 5 Lakhs to Rs. 10 Lakhs.
- **Secured Variant**: Up to 20x Net Monthly Pension, capped at Rs. 10 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Amount**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 60 months (5 years).
- **Repayment**: Standing instruction directly debited from the pension account on pension credit day.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: NIL.
- **Prepayment Penalties**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Pension Documents**: Copy of Pension Payment Order (PPO), Pension passbook, and last 3 months' pension credits.
- **Undertaking**: Irrevocable non-transfer mandate form.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick branch disbursal with minimal documentation.
- Ensures elderly individuals have immediate access to funds without liquidating long-term fixed deposits.
