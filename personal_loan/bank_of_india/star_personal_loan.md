# BOI Star Personal Loan - Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
BOI Star Personal Loan is a versatile unsecured retail credit offering from Bank of India. It provides funding for any legitimate personal financial need such as marriage ceremonies, medical hospitalizations, consumer durable purchases, domestic/international vacations, or debt consolidation.

- **High Multiple of Income**: Sized up to 30 times the Net Monthly Income (NMI) for salaried applicants with check-off arrangements.
- **Extended Repayment Tenure**: Repayment tenures up to 84 months (7 years), lowering the monthly EMI burden.
- **Clean Uncollateralized Loan**: No security or mortgage required for eligible salaried borrowers.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BOI RBLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - Salaried with Salary Tie-up/Check-off: 11.75% - 12.85% p.a.
  - Salaried without Tie-up: 12.85% - 13.85% p.a.
  - Self-Employed Individuals: 13.85% - 14.85% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Profiles**: Permanent salaried employees of Central/State Govt, PSUs, Reputed MNCs/Corporates, and established self-employed professionals.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years at loan maturity.
- **Minimum Net Take-Home Salary**: Must retain at least 40% of gross pay after all deductions and proposed EMI.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Salaried**: Up to 30x Net Monthly Income, subject to a cap of Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- **Self-Employed**: Up to 3x average annual taxable income, capped at Rs. 15 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Amount**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 84 months (7 years).
- **Repayment**: Automated Standing Instruction from BOI savings account, or employer check-off deduction.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% to 2.00% + GST. Frequently waived during festive campaigns.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL on floating rate accounts.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' bank statements (Salaried); 2 years' audited ITR, P&L, Balance Sheet (Self-employed).

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero collateral requirements for prime corporate/government profiles.
- Seamless top-up loan access after 12 months of prompt repayments.
