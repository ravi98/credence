# Tata Capital Personal Loan - Tata Capital

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Tata Capital Personal Loan is a premier multi-purpose unsecured financing solution from the trusted Tata Group. It is engineered to meet diverse financial requirements including medical emergencies, wedding expenses, home renovation, travel, debt consolidation, or higher education expenses with minimal documentation and swift approval timelines.

- **Trust of Tata Group**: Transparent terms, fair practices code, and no hidden surprises.
- **Substantial Collateral-Free Funding**: Quantum ranging up to Rs. 35 Lakhs (up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for high-income corporate cohorts).
- **Flexible Repayment Tenures**: Options to repay over 12 to 84 months (up to 7 years) with structured EMIs or step-up / step-down EMI choices.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Tata Capital Retail Lending Reference Rate (TCRLRR).
- **Interest Rate Range**: 10.99% to 19.99% p.a., determined on the basis of income level, employer category, credit rating, and repayment capacity.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried individuals (Govt, MNCs, Private/Public Ltd) and Self-Employed individuals/professionals.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 58 years (for salaried) / 65 years (for self-employed).
- **Minimum Net Monthly Salary**: Rs. 15,000 per month (Rs. 20,000+ for metro cities).
- **Work Experience**: Minimum 1 year of total work experience with at least 6 months in current employment.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL score of 700 or above (750+ qualifies for prime interest rates).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Minimum Rs. 40,000 up to Rs. 35 Lakhs (up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for select pre-approved or executive profiles).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 84 months (1 to 7 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: NACH auto-debit, e-mandate, standing instructions, and Tata Capital customer portal/app.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 2.75% of the sanctioned loan amount + applicable GST.
- **Foreclosure / Part Pre-Payment**: Part-prepayment permissible after 6 EMIs; foreclosure allowed with nominal charges as per NBFC policy and regulatory guidelines.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Identity & Address Proof**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, Driving License.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: Salary slips for the last 3 months, Form 16, bank statements for the last 6 months showing salary credits.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: ITR with computation of income for the last 2 years, Profit & Loss statement, Balance Sheet, and 6 months bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- End-to-end digital application journey via Tata Capital mobile app or website.
- Overdraft / Flexi EMI options available for borrowers seeking interest savings.
- Top-up loan facility available for existing borrowers with clean repayment track record.
