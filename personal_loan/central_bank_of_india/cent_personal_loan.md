# Cent Personal Loan - Central Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Cent Personal Loan is a clean unsecured personal credit facility offered by Central Bank of India to provide instant liquidity for personal and domestic expenditures such as medical emergencies, family weddings, holiday travel, education fees, or debt consolidation.

- **High Gross Multiplier**: Borrow up to 24 times the gross monthly salary.
- **Tenure up to 84 Months**: Repayment periods up to 7 years to reduce the monthly installment commitment.
- **Defense Concessions**: Zero processing fee and waiver of guarantor requirement for Armed Forces personnel.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Central Bank of India RLLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - Government / Defense / PSU Employees: 10.90% - 11.65% p.a.
  - Corporate Salaried (CIBIL 750+): 11.65% - 12.85% p.a.
  - General Salaried: 12.85% - 13.65% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Permanent employees of Government, PSUs, Railways, Schools, Hospitals, and Reputed Corporates.
- **Service Vintage**: Minimum 1 year for Govt/PSU employees; Minimum 3 years for private corporate employees.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years (or retirement age).
- **Net Take-Home**: Minimum 50% of gross pay must be retained after all deductions (40% permissible with employer check-off).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Up to 24x gross monthly salary, subject to a cap of Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Sanction**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 84 months (7 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions from Central Bank salary account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% of loan amount + GST (NIL for Defense Personnel).
- **Prepayment Penalty**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employment Proof**: Employee ID card and confirmation letter.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero third-party guarantor requirement for borrowers with CIBIL score 800+.
- Reliable branch-assisted processing pan-India.
