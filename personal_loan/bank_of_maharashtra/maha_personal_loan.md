# Maha Bank Personal Loan - Bank of Maharashtra

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Maha Bank Personal Loan is an unsecured retail personal finance solution provided by Bank of Maharashtra. It offers prompt liquidity for personal requirements including domestic/foreign vacations, medical expenditures, marriage ceremonies, educational costs, or household renovation.

- **High Quantum Limits**: Offers funding up to Rs. 20 Lakhs (or 20 times the gross monthly income).
- **Extended Tenure up to 84 Months**: Repayment tenures up to 7 years to minimize monthly installments.
- **Coverage for Professionals**: Dedicated eligibility criteria for Doctors, Chartered Accountants, and Architects.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Bank of Maharashtra RLLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - Salaried with Salary Account Tie-Up: 10.00% - 11.50% p.a.
  - Salaried without Tie-Up: 11.50% - 12.85% p.a.
  - Self-Employed Professionals: 12.50% - 13.90% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**:
  - Confirmed permanent employees of Central/State Govt, PSUs, Reputed Public/Private Ltd Companies, or MNCs.
  - Qualified professionals: Doctors (MBBS, MD, MS), Chartered Accountants, and Architects with at least 1 year of banking relationship.
- **Minimum Annual Income**: Rs. 3.00 Lakhs per annum.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years (or retirement age).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Up to 20x gross monthly income, capped at Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Amount**: Rs. 50,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 84 months (7 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via MahaBank salary account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% of loan amount + GST. Regularly waived during promotional festival drives.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate individual borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Professionals)**: Professional qualification degree, 2 years' audited ITR & P&L, 12 months' business bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero collateral security required for confirmed salaried employees.
- Fast processing and disbursement via MahaMobile banking application.
