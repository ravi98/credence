# UCO Cash Personal Loan - UCO Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
UCO Cash is an unsecured personal loan product provided by UCO Bank to meet diverse personal, domestic, travel, educational, or medical expenses without requiring any tangible collateral.

- **Preferential Government Terms**: Central/State Govt employees maintaining salary accounts with UCO Bank can borrow up to 20 times gross monthly income (max Rs. 20 Lakhs).
- **Fast Disbursement**: Quick processing through UCO Bank branch networks and mBanking+ app.
- **Nil Prepayment Penalties**: Borrowers can prepay or close floating rate loans early without penalty.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: UCO-RLLR.
- **Floating Rates**: 10.00% to 12.45% p.a. (Determined by employer category, salary tie-up, and CIBIL score).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**:
  - Central / State Government and PSU employees.
  - Employees of reputed public/private limited companies and educational institutions.
  - Non-salaried individuals with proven regular income streams.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years (or retirement age).
- **Service Vintage**: Minimum 2 years of continuous confirmed service.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Govt / PSU Employees (with UCO Salary Account)**: Up to 20x gross monthly income, capped at Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- **Govt / PSU Employees (without UCO Salary Account)**: Up to 5x gross monthly income, capped at Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- **Other Corporate Employees**: Up to 10x gross monthly income, capped at Rs. 10 Lakhs.
- **Non-Salaried Class**: Up to 5x gross monthly income, capped at Rs. 5 Lakhs.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 60 months (5 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions from UCO Bank salary account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Non-Salaried)**: 2 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Clean uncollateralized credit facility.
- Prompt processing for customers with established banking relationship.
