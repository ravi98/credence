# UCO Pensioner Loan - UCO Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
UCO Pensioner Loan is a specialized retail credit scheme offered by UCO Bank to retired government employees, defense veterans, UCO Bank ex-staff, and family pensioners who draw their monthly pension through UCO Bank branches.

- **Zero Processing Fees**: Complete 100% waiver of processing charges.
- **Family Pensioner Inclusion**: Dedicated credit lines up to Rs. 3 Lakhs for family pensioners.
- **Concessional Ex-Staff Terms**: 1.00% lower interest rate for retired UCO Bank employees.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: UCO Float Rate Linked.
- **Floating Rates**:
  - General Pensioners: ~11.55% p.a.
  - UCO Bank Ex-Staff: ~10.55% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Pensioners drawing pension through UCO Bank branches, including Central/State Govt, Railways, Defense, and UCO Bank retirees.
- **Age Bounds**: Up to 74 years of age at sanction (repayable before reaching age 75).
- **Mandate**: PPO lodged with UCO Bank with irrevocable undertaking not to change pension disbursement account.
- **Guarantee**: Spouse eligible for family pension joins as co-borrower/guarantor.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Pensioners with Family Pension Provision (Spouse Alive)**:
  - Up to 70 Years: 15 times monthly pension, capped at Rs. 10 Lakhs.
  - Age 71 to 74 Years: 15 times monthly pension, capped at Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- **Family Pensioners / Predeceased Spouse**: 10 times monthly pension, capped at Rs. 3 Lakhs.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 48 monthly installments (4 years).
- **Repayment**: Auto-debit from pension savings account on pension credit day.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: NIL.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Pension Documents**: Copy of Pension Payment Order (PPO), Pension passbook, and last 3 months' pension credits.
- **Undertaking**: Irrevocable standing instruction / non-transfer mandate form.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Immediate financial relief for senior citizens with minimal paperwork.
- Automated monthly debit removes any risk of overdue interest or missed payments.
