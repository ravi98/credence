# IB Clean Loan - Indian Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IB Clean Loan is an unsecured personal loan product from Indian Bank curated for salaried individuals. It enables borrowers to finance personal domestic needs like marriages, medical emergencies, travel, higher education, or consumer durable purchases without providing any tangible asset collateral.

- **High Gross Multiplier**: Allows borrowing up to 20 times the gross monthly salary.
- **Extended Repayment Window**: Repayment tenures up to 84 months (7 years), lowering the monthly EMI outgo.
- **Check-Off Concessions**: Preferential pricing for applicants whose employers provide a direct check-off salary deduction facility.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IB-EBLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - Salaried with Employer Check-Off: 9.90% - 11.40% p.a.
  - Salaried without Check-Off: 11.40% - 13.90% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Permanent confirmed employees of Central/State Government, PSUs, Reputed Corporate Companies, and educational institutions.
- **Service Vintage**: Minimum 2 years of continuous work experience.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years (or retirement age).
- **Net Take-Home (NTH)**: Minimum 40% of gross salary must remain after deducting all existing and proposed loan EMIs.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Up to 20x gross monthly salary, capped at Rs. 15 Lakhs to Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Amount**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 84 months (7 years).
- **Repayment**: Employer check-off deduction, or standing instruction from Indian Bank salary account.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% of loan amount + GST. Periodically discounted during festival drives.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employment Proof**: Employee ID card and employer service certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero collateral security required.
- Fast-track digital in-principle approval via Indian Bank IndOASIS mobile application.
