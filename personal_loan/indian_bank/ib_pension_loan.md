# IB Pension Loan - Indian Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IB Pension Loan is a specialized retail credit scheme offered by Indian Bank to retired government employees, defense veterans, and family pensioners who draw their monthly pension directly through Indian Bank branches. It provides immediate liquidity for medical needs, marriage expenses, home repairs, or domestic contingencies.

- **Concessional Pension Pricing**: Competitive rates starting from 10.15% p.a.
- **Extended Age Limit**: Maximum exit age up to 78 years for regular pensioners and up to 75 years for family pensioners.
- **Simple Branch Execution**: Fast approval within 24 hours without demanding extensive collateral or paperwork.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IB-EBLR.
- **Floating Rates**: 10.15% to 12.15% p.a. (Lower rates for shorter repayment horizons).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Central Government, State Government, Defense, and Indian Bank retired staff drawing monthly pension via Indian Bank.
- **Age Bounds**:
  - Regular Pensioners: Entry age up to 75 years; Exit age up to 78 years.
  - Family Pensioners: Entry age up to 70–72 years; Exit age up to 75 years.
- **Mandate**: PPO lodged with Indian Bank with irrevocable undertaking not to switch pension account.
- **Guarantee**: Guarantee of spouse or legal heir.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Regular Pensioners**: Up to 15 to 18 times monthly pension, capped at Rs. 5 Lakhs to Rs. 6 Lakhs.
- **Family Pensioners**: Up to 12 times monthly pension, capped at Rs. 2 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Sanction**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 60 months (5 years) or up to exit age 78 years.
- **Repayment**: Auto-debit from pension disbursement savings account on pension credit day.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: NIL or nominal documentation charge.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Pension Documents**: Copy of Pension Payment Order (PPO), Pension passbook, and last 3 months' pension credits.
- **Undertaking**: Irrevocable standing instruction / non-transfer mandate form.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Eliminates the need for senior citizens to rely on private moneylenders or informal loans.
- Seamless automated EMI recovery without branch visits.
