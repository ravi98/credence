# ICICI FlexiCash - ICICI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
ICICI FlexiCash is a 100% digital, unsecured revolving overdraft facility designed exclusively for ICICI Bank salary account holders. Unlike a traditional fixed-term personal loan with rigid monthly EMIs, FlexiCash assigns an approved overdraft limit linked directly to the borrower's operative salary account.

- **Interest Charged Solely on Utilized Funds**: Interest is calculated strictly on the daily utilized balance and only for the exact number of days the funds remain withdrawn.
- **Revolving Credit Limit**: Borrowers can draw, repay, and redraw funds as many times as needed within the approved credit line without submitting fresh applications.
- **Immediate Cash Cushion**: Protects against unexpected month-end deficits, medical emergency bills, or cheque bounces with 24x7 liquidity.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: ICICI Benchmark Overdraft Lending Rate.
- **Interest Slabs**: 12.50% to 15.50% p.a. (Only charged on the specific amount utilized). Zero interest charged on unutilized limit.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Active ICICI Bank salary account holders working with approved corporates. (Not available to self-employed individuals).
- **Eligibility Mode**: Pre-approved and pre-qualified based on regular salary inflows and credit bureau track record.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 58 years (or retirement age).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Up to 3 times the Net Monthly Salary (algorithmically calculated and pre-approved).
- **Collateral**: Nil (100% Unsecured).

## 5. Repayment Structure & Process
- **Tenure**: Sanctioned for 12 months (subject to automated annual renewal based on satisfactory account conduct).
- **Repayment Flexibility**: No mandatory fixed monthly EMI. Borrowers simply deposit funds into their salary account to pay down the utilized balance whenever cash becomes available. Monthly interest accrued is debited automatically from the account.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Nominal flat activation fee (typically Rs. 1,000 to Rs. 2,000) + GST.
- **Annual Renewal Fee**: Nominal annual maintenance/renewal charge.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL (Funds can be repaid at any time without any charges).

## 7. Required Documents
- **Zero Physical Documentation**: 100% paperless digital onboarding via ICICI NetBanking or iMobile Pay app. Instant limit activation with two-factor OTP authentication.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Direct integration into regular savings account: ATM cash withdrawals, UPI payments, and debit card transactions seamlessly tap into the FlexiCash limit if account balance runs dry.
- Avoids taking multiple high-cost short-term instant payday loans.
