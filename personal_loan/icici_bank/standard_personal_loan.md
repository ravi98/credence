# ICICI Standard Personal Loan - ICICI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
ICICI Standard Personal Loan is a multi-utility unsecured personal financing solution from ICICI Bank. It provides rapid funding for personal and household expenditures including wedding arrangements, international/domestic holidays, emergency medical bills, higher education, or consolidating existing higher-interest debts.

- **Instant Disbursals for Pre-Approved Customers**: Pre-approved ICICI salary account holders can get funds disbursed in 3 seconds directly via iMobile Pay or Net Banking.
- **High Uncollateralized Quantum**: Clean personal loan sanctions up to Rs. 50 Lakhs without any physical property hypothecation.
- **Doorstep Document Assistance**: Simplified onboarding with digital e-KYC or doorstep document pickup.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: ICICI I-RRR.
- **Fixed/Floating Rates**: 10.65% to 16.00% p.a., determined by applicant CIBIL score, corporate employer grading (Super Prime, Diamond, Gold, Silver), and net salary bracket.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried individuals and self-employed professionals/business owners.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 58 years (salaried) and 65 years (self-employed).
- **Minimum Net Monthly Salary**: Rs. 30,000 per month (Rs. 25,000 for select tier-2 cities).
- **Work Vintage**: Minimum 2 years of overall employment (at least 1 year with current employer).
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+ strictly preferred (750+ secures lowest interest rate tier).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Rs. 50,000 up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- **Multiplier**: Up to 15 to 20 times the Net Monthly Salary.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Flexible tenures from 12 to 72 months (1 to 6 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via ICICI Bank savings account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 2.50% of the loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure**: Permissible after 12 EMIs with applicable foreclosure charges (typically 3% to 5% on outstanding principal).

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 2 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- 100% paperless digital journey for eligible pre-approved customers.
- Complimentary personal accident and loan protection insurance covers available.
