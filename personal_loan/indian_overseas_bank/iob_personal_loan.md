# IOB Personal Loan Scheme - Indian Overseas Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IOB Personal Loan Scheme is a versatile retail credit facility offered by Indian Overseas Bank to meet diverse personal, domestic, travel, educational, or medical expenses. It categorizes borrowers across transparent tiers to provide high credit limits with flexible repayment schedules.

- **High Quantum Limits**: Offers funding up to Rs. 30 Lakhs for prime government, PSU, and corporate employees.
- **Extended Tenure up to 84 Months**: Repayment periods up to 7 years to keep monthly EMIs affordable.
- **Tiered Category Pricing**: Best interest rates starting from 10.25% p.a. for borrowers with high CIBIL scores.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IOB RLLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 10.25% - 10.85% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 10.85% - 11.75% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 11.75% - 12.85% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 12.85% - 13.90% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Borrower Categories**:
  - Category A: Permanent employees of Central/State Govt, PSUs, Defense, and Railways.
  - Category B: Permanent employees of Listed Corporates, MNCs, and Reputed Educational Institutions maintaining salary account with IOB.
  - Category C: Employees of other recognized private firms.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 60 years (or retirement age).
- **Net Take-Home**: Minimum 50% of gross pay after deducting proposed EMI.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Category A & B**: Up to 20 to 25 times gross monthly salary, capped at Rs. 30 Lakhs.
- **Category C**: Up to 10 times gross monthly salary, capped at Rs. 15 Lakhs.
- **Minimum Amount**: Rs. 25,000.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Tenure**: Up to 84 months (7 years).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instruction from IOB salary account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% to 1.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Employment Proof**: Employee ID card and service certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Straightforward personal credit lines without collateral requirements.
- Strong branch presence across South India and major national metropolitan centers.
