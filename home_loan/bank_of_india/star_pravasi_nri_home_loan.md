# BOI Star Pravasi Home Loan (NRI) - Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
BOI Star Pravasi Home Loan is a tailored housing finance program designed exclusively for Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs/OCIs) wishing to purchase, build, or renovate residential properties across India.

- **Cross-Border Remittance Servicing**: Repayable seamlessly through NRE or NRO accounts or direct inward foreign currency remittances.
- **Power of Attorney (POA) Processing**: Enables loan execution and property purchase via a locally appointed Power of Attorney holder residing in India.
- **Dedicated NRI Desks**: Priority processing through specialized NRI branches in major metropolitan and diaspora-heavy regions.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BOI RBLR.
- **Interest Slabs**: 7.35% to 9.15% p.a., determined by international credit ratings / domestic bureau scores where available.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for female applicants/co-applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Profiles**:
  - Non-Resident Indians holding a valid Indian Passport.
  - Persons of Indian Origin (PIO) / Overseas Citizens of India (OCI) holding a valid foreign passport.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years at loan maturity.
- **Employment Requirement**: Minimum 2 years of continuous overseas employment with a valid work permit or green card / permanent residency.
- **Minimum Income**: Equivalent to USD 2,000 to USD 3,000 per month (or foreign currency equivalent depending on country of residence).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 10 Crores (subject to overseas repayment capacity and RBI FEMA guidelines).
- **LTV Limits**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Repayment Modes**: Inward remittances through standard banking channels or debit from NRE/NRO savings accounts in India.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.35% (Min Rs. 5,000, Max Rs. 25,000) + GST.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: NIL for individual floating rate borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC & Visa**:
  - Valid Passport with valid Visa / Work Permit / Permanent Residence Card.
  - Overseas residential address proof (Utility bills, lease agreement, driving license).
  - Continuous Discharge Certificate (CDC) for marine crew applicants.
- **Income Proof**:
  - Last 6 months' overseas salary slips / salary certificate attested by Indian Embassy/Consulate or employer.
  - Overseas bank statements (last 6 to 12 months) showing salary credits.
  - Overseas tax returns (W2 in USA, P60 in UK, or local equivalent).
- **POA Documentation**: Registered and notarized Power of Attorney (consular legalized or apostilled).
- **Property Documents**: Registered sale agreement, sanctioned building plans, title search report.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Hassle-free coordination between overseas branches/desks and Indian branches.
- Advisory support on FEMA and RBI residential property acquisition regulations.
