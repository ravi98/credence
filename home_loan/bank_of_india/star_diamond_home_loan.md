# BOI Star Diamond Home Loan - Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
BOI Star Diamond Home Loan is a bespoke, luxury mortgage offering from Bank of India designed specifically for High Net Worth Individuals (HNIs) purchasing premium luxury apartments, penthouses, bespoke villas, or independent bungalows where loan ticket sizes exceed Rs. 7.5 Crores.

- **High-Ticket Specialization**: Catered specifically for loans starting above Rs. 7.5 Crores with customized credit structuring.
- **Flat Processing Cap**: Capped flat processing fee of Rs. 60,000 regardless of whether the loan amount is Rs. 10 Crores or Rs. 50 Crores.
- **Dedicated Relationship Manager**: Priority processing through BOI Large Corporate / HNI Branches.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BOI RBLR.
- **Interest Slabs**: 7.25% to 8.85% p.a. (Special competitive risk spreads for HNI profiles with CIBIL >= 800).
- **Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for women borrowers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Profile**: High-earning corporate CXOs, partners of reputed law/consulting firms, promoters, and established business owners.
- **Minimum Loan Threshold**: Strict minimum ticket size of Rs. 7.5 Crores.
- **Age Window**: 21 to 70 years at maturity.
- **Financial Eligibility**: Minimum certified annual net income of Rs. 1.5 Crores.
- **CIBIL Score**: CIBIL 750+ strictly preferred.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized from Rs. 7.5 Crores upwards with no prescribed upper limit.
- **LTV Ratio**: Up to 75% of property market value / registered purchase agreement.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Flexible terms up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction luxury residential developments.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: Flat Rs. 60,000 + GST.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure Fee**: Nil for individuals under floating rate terms.
- **Legal & Valuation Fees**: Exact actuals for dual-valuer independent evaluation.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Passport, Aadhaar.
- **Financial Proofs**:
  - Salaried: 6 months' detailed salary slips, Form 16 (3 years), ITR computation sheets (3 years), Wealth Statements / CA Net Worth certificate.
  - Business Promoters: 3 years' audited corporate financials, CA net worth certificate, proof of shareholding and dividend yields.
- **Property Records**: Approved architectural layout, clear title report, NOC from developer.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Express single-window approvals via BOI National Banking Group desks.
- Access to complimentary wealth management advisory and premium banking privileges.
