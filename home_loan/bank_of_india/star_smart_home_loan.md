# BOI Star Smart Home Loan - Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
BOI Star Smart Home Loan is an interest-saver overdraft mortgage solution offered by Bank of India. The facility links the borrower's home loan to a specialized Savings Bank (SB) or Current Deposit (CD) account, allowing borrowers to park liquid surplus funds and substantially decrease daily interest costs.

- **Interest Offset Mechanism**: Interest is calculated daily only on the net debit balance (Home Loan Outstanding minus Linked Account Balance).
- **Unrestricted Withdrawals**: Parked surplus funds are not locked; they can be withdrawn at any time via ATM, net banking, or cheque books.
- **Substantial Interest & Tenure Reduction**: Enables significant reduction in total interest burden without requiring formal principal prepayments.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BOI RBLR.
- **Floating Rate Range**: 7.35% to 9.25% p.a.
  - Note: Overdraft home loans above Rs. 2 Crores may attract an additional 0.25% spread depending on account tier.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Account Relationship**: Open to existing SB/CD account holders maintaining an average minimum balance of Rs. 5,000+ for the preceding 12 months, or new customers establishing a primary banking relationship.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 70 years at maturity.
- **Minimum Loan Quantum**: Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized based on applicant repaying capacity.
- **LTV Limits**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Repayment Functionality**: The drawing power of the overdraft reduces every month by the principal amount equivalent of the standard EMI.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.35% + GST (subject to cap).
- **Foreclosure Charges**: Nil for individual floating rate borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Income Details**:
  - Salaried: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16, 6 months' salary bank statement.
  - Self-Employed: 3 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts.
- **Property Documents**: Registered sale agreement, link title deeds (30 years), sanctioned building plan.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Optimal for self-employed professionals, traders, and salaried executives receiving periodic variable compensations.
- Seamless debit card and internet banking facilities linked directly to the overdraft account.
