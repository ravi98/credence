# BOI Star Home Loan - Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
BOI Star Home Loan is the flagship housing credit solution from Bank of India. It finances purchase of residential plots (with construction planned within 5 years), ready-built flats, under-construction apartments, home extension, and renovation/furnishing.

- **Complimentary Accidental Insurance**: Free personal accidental insurance cover up to Rs. 5 Crores for borrowers.
- **Floating & Fixed Rate Options**: Option to choose either RBLR-linked floating rates or fixed rate periods up to 5 years.
- **Family Income Aggregation**: Permits clubbing income of parents, spouse, or working children to optimize loan quantum.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Repo Based Lending Rate (RBLR).
- **Floating Rate Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 840: 7.30% - 7.50% p.a.
  - CIBIL 775 - 839: 7.50% - 7.85% p.a.
  - CIBIL 725 - 774: 7.85% - 8.35% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 725: 8.35% - 9.05% p.a.
- **Women Borrower Concession**: 0.05% concession on the applicable interest rate.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Borrower Profiles**: Resident Indians, NRIs, PIOs, pensioners, and business owners.
- **Age Span**:
  - Minimum Age: 18 years.
  - Maximum Exit Age: Up to 70 years (salaried) and 75 years (self-employed/pensioners).
- **Minimum Net Monthly Income**: Rs. 20,000 per month.
- **CIBIL Requirement**: Minimum 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 7.5 Crores under Star Home Loan (above Rs. 7.5 Cr falls under Star Diamond).
- **LTV Caps**:
  - Loans up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: 360 months (30 years).
- **Moratorium Period**: Up to 36 months for under-construction properties, or until completion certificate.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions, ECS / NACH mandates.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.35% of the loan amount (Min Rs. 2,000, Max Rs. 20,000) + GST. Periodic 100% festive waivers.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips and Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited Balance Sheet, P&L, and ITR (Self-employed).
- **Bank Statements**: 6 to 12 months' statements of primary salary/current account.
- **Property Documents**: Registered sale agreement, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, and non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick digital approval through BOI Omnineo mobile app.
- Attractive top-up facilities available after 12 months of satisfactory repayments.
