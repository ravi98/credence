# Union Home Loan - Union Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Union Home Loan is the flagship residential finance program offered by Union Bank of India (one of India's top 5 public sector banks). It finances purchase of ready-to-move flats/houses, plots plus construction, standalone home construction, repairs, renovation, and takeover of existing housing loans from other banks.

- **No Maximum Loan Limit**: Loan quantum is determined strictly by repayment ability and property valuation.
- **Moratorium Availability**: Up to 36 months repayment holiday for under-construction residential properties.
- **Women Borrower Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate when a woman is sole owner or primary co-borrower.
- **Zero Prepayment Penalty**: Floating rate loans carry 0% prepayment or foreclosure penalties.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR), tied to RBI Repo Rate.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 7.15% to 9.05% p.a., determined by credit score:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.15% - 7.35%
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.35% - 7.75%
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 7.75% - 8.25%
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.25% - 9.05%

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Borrower Category**: Resident Indian citizens, NRIs, and PIOs.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 70–75 years at loan completion.
- **Employment Stability**:
  - Salaried: Minimum 1 year of confirmed continuous service.
  - Self-Employed: Minimum 2 to 3 years of established business operation or professional practice.
- **Minimum Net Income**: Rs. 20,000 per month for salaried; Rs. 2.5 Lakhs annual net profit for self-employed.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized based on 45% - 60% FOIR (Fixed Obligation to Income Ratio).
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV) Norms**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium (Repayment Holiday)**: Up to 36 months for under-construction projects, or 18 months for plot + construction.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing instructions via Union Bank savings account, NACH / ECS mandates.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of loan amount (Capped at Rs. 15,000) + GST. (Full festive waivers frequently apply).
- **Prepayment Penalties**: Nil for floating rate loans.
- **Legal & Valuation**: Actual charges levied by panel valuers and legal counsel.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' business bank statements.
- **Property Records**: Registered sale deed/agreement, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned layout blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick digital approval via Union Bank 'Vyom' app.
- Top-up home loan facility available after satisfactory repayment track record.
