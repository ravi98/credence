# Union Smart Save Home Loan - Union Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Union Smart Save is an overdraft-linked smart home loan facility introduced by Union Bank of India. It enables borrowers to link their primary savings/current accounts with their housing credit line, depositing surplus savings to substantially lower interest payments.

- **Interest Offset Functionality**: Daily interest computation is done solely on the net balance (Loan Outstanding minus Surplus Credit in linked account).
- **Withdrawal Freedom**: Any surplus deposited can be withdrawn at will via ATM, UPI, net banking, or cheque without any prepayment paperwork.
- **Conversion Flexibility**: Borrowers are allowed to switch between the Smart Save option and a standard regular home loan up to two times during the overall loan tenure.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Union Bank EBLR.
- **Floating Rates**: 7.40% to 9.30% p.a.
  - Typically carries a nominal spread of 25 bps (0.25%) over the standard Union Home Loan rate to account for the flexible liquidity benefit.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest reduction for women borrowers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians and Non-Resident Indians (NRIs).
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 70 years at maturity.
- **Borrower Income**: Salaried or self-employed individuals with proven steady cash flows.
- **Minimum Sanction Amount**: Rs. 10 Lakhs.
- **CIBIL Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized as per regular housing loan criteria (No ceiling).
- **LTV Caps**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Repayment Mechanism**: Standard EMIs are debited every month from the linked account, reducing the sanctioned overdraft drawing power systematically.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% + GST (Max Rs. 15,000).
- **Foreclosure Charges**: Nil for individual floating rate borrowers.
- **Switching Fee**: Standard conversion fee if switching between regular and Smart Save.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Income Details**:
  - Salaried: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (2 years), 6 months' bank statements.
  - Self-Employed: 3 years' audited financial statements, tax audit reports, 12 months' operative bank account statements.
- **Property Documentation**: Registered agreement for sale, non-encumbrance certificate (30 years), approved blueprint.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Highly suitable for business owners, corporate executives with periodic bonuses, and consultants.
- Instant access to liquid funds in emergencies without having to apply for fresh loans.
