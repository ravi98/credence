# Union Awas Affordable Housing Loan - Union Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Union Awas is an affordable housing finance scheme specifically structured to facilitate homeownership across rural, semi-urban, and Tier-3/4 regions of India. It supports the purchase of ready houses, construction on owned plots, or repairs/improvements to existing residential dwellings in semi-urban and rural areas.

- **Concessional Rural Pricing**: Offers highly competitive rates tailored for regional, rural, and agricultural borrowers.
- **Affordable Loan Threshold**: Ticket sizes up to Rs. 20 Lakhs, minimizing debt strain on regional households.
- **Simplified Document Standards**: Flexible income validation suited for regional agriculturalists, artisans, and rural entrepreneurs.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Union Bank EBLR.
- **Floating Rate Range**: 6.90% to 8.50% p.a.
- **Additional Subsidies**: Seamlessly integrable with central and state affordable rural housing subsidy programs.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Beneficiaries**: Resident Indians living in rural and semi-urban center boundaries.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 70 years at maturity.
- **Occupational Profiles**: Small farmers, agricultural laborers, artisans, regional teachers, small shopkeepers, and semi-urban employees.
- **Income Profile**: Annual income from agriculture, allied activities, or unorganized trade verified via local revenue authorities or bank records.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**:
  - Construction / Purchase in Rural/Semi-Urban: Up to Rs. 20 Lakhs.
  - Repairs & Improvements: Up to Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- **Margin / LTV**: Up to 90% of property cost (Borrower contribution 10%).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Repayment Flexibility**: Half-yearly or annual repayment schedules permitted for agriculturalists to align with crop harvesting cycles.
- **Moratorium**: Up to 24 months for self-construction.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Discounted at 0.25% + GST.
- **Prepayment Penalties**: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN (or Form 60).
- **Income Proof**: Agricultural land holding records (7/12 extract, Patta/Khata), milk dairy slips, local panchayat certificates, or bank account passbook.
- **Property Records**: Village panchayat / Municipal approved plan, non-encumbrance certificate, clear property title deeds.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Direct agricultural cash-flow alignment for EMI payments.
- Extensive branch coverage in rural and semi-urban India.
