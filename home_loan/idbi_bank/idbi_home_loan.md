# IDBI Home Loan - IDBI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IDBI Home Loan is the flagship retail mortgage financing product from IDBI Bank. It covers financing for purchase of newly constructed or resale flats/houses, self-construction of residential houses, plot purchase plus construction, and home extension/improvement.

- **In-Principle Sanction Before Selection**: Allows prospective buyers to secure an in-principle approval before finalizing a specific residential property.
- **Nil Foreclosure Charges**: Zero prepayment fees for individual floating rate borrowers.
- **PMAY Support**: Integrated with government credit-linked interest subsidy schemes.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IDBI Bank RLLR (Repo Linked).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.35% - 7.65% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.65% - 8.10% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.10% - 8.70% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.70% - 9.35% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for female primary applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Profiles**: Resident Indians and NRIs.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 70 years at loan maturity.
- **Employment History**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years audited profitability for self-employed.
- **Minimum Income**: Rs. 20,000 per month (salaried) / Rs. 2 Lakhs annual profit (self-employed).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 10 Crores based on repayment ability.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via IDBI Bank account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of loan amount (Min Rs. 2,500, Max Rs. 15,000) + GST. Regularly waived during promotional periods.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' business bank statements.
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement to sell, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep document pickup and customer assistance.
- Fast turnaround time with digitized underwriting desks.
