# IDBI Home Loan Interest Saver - IDBI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IDBI Home Loan Interest Saver is an innovative smart overdraft mortgage product offered by IDBI Bank. It directly links the housing loan to a specialized savings/current operative account, allowing borrowers to park surplus liquidity to minimize daily interest accruals.

- **Interest Offset Functionality**: Daily interest computation is done solely on the net debit balance (Home Loan Outstanding minus Surplus Credit in the linked account).
- **Withdrawal Freedom**: Any surplus deposited can be withdrawn at will via ATM, UPI, net banking, or cheque without prepayment fees.
- **Tenure Reduction**: Regularly depositing salary, bonuses, or business cash surplus shortens overall repayment tenure and drastically cuts total interest outgo.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IDBI Bank RLLR.
- **Floating Rates**: 7.55% to 9.55% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians and NRIs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 70 years at maturity.
- **Borrower Income**: Salaried or self-employed individuals with proven steady cash flows.
- **Minimum Sanction Amount**: Rs. 10 Lakhs.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Minimum Rs. 10 Lakhs; up to Rs. 10 Crores based on repaying capacity.
- **LTV Caps**: Up to 75% - 85% of property market value.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Repayment Mechanism**: Monthly EMI reduces the drawing power / loan balance systematically.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% + GST.
- **Foreclosure Charges**: Nil for individual floating rate borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Income Details**:
  - Salaried: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (2 years), 6 months' bank statements.
  - Self-Employed: 3 years' audited financial statements, tax audit reports, 12 months' operative bank account statements.
- **Property Documentation**: Registered agreement for sale, non-encumbrance certificate (30 years), approved blueprint.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Optimal for self-employed professionals, traders, and salaried executives receiving periodic variable compensations.
- Instant access to liquid funds in emergencies without having to apply for fresh loans.
