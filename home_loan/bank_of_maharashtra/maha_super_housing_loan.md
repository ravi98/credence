# Maha Super Housing Loan - Bank of Maharashtra

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Maha Super Housing Loan is the premier home finance offering from Bank of Maharashtra. Known for some of the most competitive interest rates in the public banking sector, it finances purchasing ready/under-construction flats, houses, constructing residential dwellings, plot purchase with construction, home extension, and renovations.

- **Market-Leading Starting Rates**: Starting rates from 7.00% p.a. for borrowers with high CIBIL scores.
- **Maha Super Flexi Option**: Available with an overdraft facility that offsets interest using liquid savings balances.
- **Cross-Product Concessions**: Borrowers get preferential interest rates on car and education loans.
- **Special Concessions**: 0.05% interest concession for women borrowers and defense personnel; 0.10% concession for green building certification.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Bank of Maharashtra RLLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.00% - 7.30% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.30% - 7.70% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 7.70% - 8.25% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.25% - 9.65% p.a.
- **Flexi Overdraft Variant**: Priced at 0.25% above the standard Maha Super Housing Loan rate.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians, NRIs, PIOs, and OCIs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 75 years at loan maturity.
- **Income Requirements**: Minimum annual income of Rs. 3 Lakhs.
- **Employment Stability**: Minimum 2 years of continuous employment for salaried; 3 years of business operations for self-employed.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Based on Net Take-Home (NTH) pay (FOIR up to 60-70%).
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months) or age 75 years.
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via MahaMobile app / Bank of Maharashtra account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.25% of loan amount (Maximum Rs. 25,000) + GST. (Full festive waivers frequently apply).
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips and Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts (Self-employed).
- **Property Documentation**: Registered agreement for sale, link title documents for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero prepayment, pre-closure, or part-payment charges.
- Digital application and fast-track processing via MahaBank portal and mobile app.
