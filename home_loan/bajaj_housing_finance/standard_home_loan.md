# Bajaj Housing Finance Standard Home Loan - Bajaj Housing Finance

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Bajaj Housing Finance Standard Home Loan is a flagship retail mortgage product offered by Bajaj Housing Finance Limited (BHFL), a 100% subsidiary of Bajaj Finance Limited. It finances the purchase of ready-to-move-in flats/apartments, under-construction properties, independent villas, self-construction on owned land, and plot acquisition with construction.

- **Longest Repayment Tenure in Industry**: Offers extended repayment tenures up to 32 years (384 months) to maximize affordability and lower EMIs.
- **Fastest Digital Disbursals**: End-to-end digital verification with provisional approval issued in as little as 10 minutes.
- **Zero Prepayment Penalties**: Individual borrowers on floating rate loans can prepay or foreclose with zero charges.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Bajaj Housing Finance Prime Lending Rate (BHFL-PLR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - Salaried Borrowers: 7.25% to 10.25% p.a. (CIBIL 750+ qualifies for 7.25% - 7.65%).
  - Self-Employed Borrowers: 7.70% to 10.65% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for female primary applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Profiles**: Resident Indian Citizens and salaried Non-Resident Indians (NRIs).
- **Age Window**:
  - Salaried / Professionals: 23 to 65 years at loan maturity.
  - Self-Employed: 23 to 75 years at loan maturity.
- **Employment Stability**: Minimum 2 years continuous service for salaried; 3 years audited business vintage for self-employed.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 725+ preferred for optimal terms.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 15 Crores based on repayment capacity.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 32 years (384 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: NACH mandate, online portal auto-debit, or standing instructions.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.25% to 0.50% of the loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate individual borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' business bank statements.
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement to sell, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Online self-service customer portal for instant statement downloads and EMI payments.
- Attractive top-up facilities available after 12 months of clean repayment track record.
