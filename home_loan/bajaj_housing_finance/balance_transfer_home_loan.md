# Bajaj Housing Finance Home Loan Balance Transfer - Bajaj Housing Finance

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Bajaj Housing Finance Home Loan Balance Transfer allows existing home loan borrowers to switch their high-cost housing loans from commercial banks, HFCs, or NBFCs to Bajaj Housing Finance at competitive rates, substantially lowering their monthly EMI obligations and overall interest costs.

- **High-Value Top-Up Loan**: Borrowers transferring their balance can simultaneously avail a high-value Top-Up Loan of up to Rs. 1 Crore or higher for personal/business needs with minimal additional paperwork.
- **Extended Tenure up to 32 Years**: Option to recalibrate the remaining loan tenure up to 32 years to optimize cash flow.
- **Hassle-Free Handover**: Bajaj Housing Finance assists with retrieving original property title deeds directly from the existing lender upon foreclosure payment.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BHFL-PLR.
- **Floating Rates**:
  - Salaried Borrowers: 7.30% to 10.35% p.a.
  - Self-Employed Borrowers: 7.95% to 10.80% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest discount applies.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Profiles**: Resident Indian Citizens and salaried NRIs.
- **Existing Loan Track**: Minimum 12 consecutive months of regular, unblemished EMI repayments with the current financier.
- **Age Window**: 23 to 65 years (salaried) and 75 years (self-employed) at maturity.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 725+ preferred.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Full outstanding principal balance of the existing home loan, plus top-up loan based on property equity headroom.
- **Combined LTV**: Total exposure capped within 75% - 80% of current fair market property valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 32 years (384 months).
- **Repayment Modes**: NACH mandate, online portal auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Discounted processing fees / flat nominal fee + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for individual floating rate borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips and Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR & financial statements (Self-employed).
- **Existing Loan Documents**:
  - Foreclosure letter / Outstanding balance certificate from current lender.
  - List of Documents (LOD) held by current financier.
  - Last 12 months' loan repayment account track record.
- **Property Records**: Photocopies of registered sale deed and approved building plan.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Direct savings of several lakhs of rupees over the remaining loan horizon.
- Zero prepayment charges allow accelerated debt liquidation whenever surplus liquidity is generated.
