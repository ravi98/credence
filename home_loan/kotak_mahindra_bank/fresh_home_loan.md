# Kotak Fresh Home Loan - Kotak Mahindra Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Kotak Fresh Home Loan is Kotak Mahindra Bank's core residential mortgage offering. Known for highly aggressive competitive pricing and seamless digital underwriting, it finances the purchase of ready-to-move-in flats/apartments, under-construction properties, independent villas, self-construction, and balance transfer of existing home loans.

- **High Quantum Availability**: Sanction limits available up to Rs. 40 Crores for luxury and prime residential developments.
- **Fast Digital Journey**: End-to-end digital documentation, instant in-principle sanction, and doorstep service.
- **Zero Foreclosure Penalties**: Prepay or foreclose floating rate loans at any time without fees.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Kotak Repo Linked Benchmark Rate (K-RLLR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.60% - 7.85% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.85% - 8.20% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.20% - 8.65% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.65% - 9.15% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for women primary applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Profiles**: Resident Indians and NRIs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 65 years (salaried) and 70 years (self-employed).
- **Employment Stability**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years audited profitability for self-employed.
- **Minimum Net Income**: Rs. 20,000 - Rs. 25,000 net monthly salary (salaried); Rs. 2.5 Lakhs annual profit (self-employed).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+ (750+ secures prime rate band).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 40 Crores based on income multiplier and FOIR.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 25 years (300 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential projects.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Kotak 811 / regular savings account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of the loan amount (Min Rs. 10,000) + GST. Regularly waived during promotional periods.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate individual borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' business bank statements.
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement to sell, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep document verification and priority processing.
- Customized loan structuring for business promoters and self-employed professionals.
