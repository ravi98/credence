# Kotak Home Renovation Loan - Kotak Mahindra Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Kotak Home Renovation Loan (Home Improvement Loan) provides secured credit lines for current homeowners who want to renovate, modernize, expand, or carry out structural repairs on their residential property. It covers interior design, modular kitchen installation, bathroom renovations, flooring, painting, roofing, and civil additions.

- **Secured Mortgage Pricing**: Priced close to prime home loan interest rates, offering massive interest savings compared to personal loans.
- **Tax Benefits**: Eligible for income tax deductions on interest paid under Section 24(b) of the Income Tax Act up to Rs. 30,000 per financial year.
- **Fast Appraisal**: Streamlined documentation and expedited disbursement against civil work progress.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Kotak K-RLLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 7.99% to 9.45% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for female applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians and NRIs owning an existing residential flat, villa, or independent house.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years (salaried) and 70 years (self-employed).
- **Employment History**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years for self-employed.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Based on certified contractor/architect renovation estimate and borrower income.
- **Margin / LTV**: Up to 80% - 90% of the renovation estimate.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 20 years (240 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 3 to 6 months during renovation execution.
- **Disbursement Mode**: Staged disbursements directly released to vendors/contractors or homeowner based on inspection.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for individual floating rate borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips and Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR & financial statements (Self-employed).
- **Renovation Cost Estimate**: Detailed itemized estimate / Bill of Quantities (BOQ) prepared by a registered architect or chartered engineer.
- **Property Records**: Registered title deed of the existing property, latest property tax receipt, society NOC.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Can be bundled with primary home loan or taken as an independent facility.
- Significant boost to property valuation and rental yields upon completion.
