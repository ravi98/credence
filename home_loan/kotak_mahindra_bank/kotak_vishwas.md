# Kotak Vishwas Affordable Home Loan - Kotak Mahindra Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Kotak Vishwas is an affordable housing loan initiative designed by Kotak Mahindra Bank to empower first-time homebuyers and individuals in the unorganized or semi-formal economic sectors to achieve homeownership. It offers simplified eligibility norms and flexible cash-flow assessment.

- **Broader Accessibility**: Sized for low-to-middle income groups who may not possess conventional ITRs or corporate salary slips.
- **Funding up to 90% LTV**: Offers financing up to 90% of the property value for entry-level homes.
- **Transparent Underwriting**: Clear, customer-friendly documentation with no hidden fine print.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Kotak Affordable Housing Reference Benchmark.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 9.25% to 11.25% p.a.
- **Concessions**: Special terms available for female co-applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried individuals in small enterprises, micro-entrepreneurs, traders, and small businessmen.
- **Income Thresholds**:
  - Salaried: Minimum monthly income of Rs. 12,000.
  - Self-Employed: Minimum annual income of Rs. 1.5 Lakhs.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years at maturity.
- **Credit Track Record**: CIBIL 600+ or New-to-Credit (NTC) evaluated through banking turnover and utility payment history.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Maximum Sanction**: Up to Rs. 1 Crore.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Up to 90% of property cost (Borrower equity 10%).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**:
  - Salaried Borrowers: Up to 25 years.
  - Self-Employed Borrowers: Up to 20 years.
- **Repayment Modes**: NACH mandate, post-dated cheques, or standing instruction.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 1.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**: Salary certificate, passbook entries (6-12 months), trade invoices, cash-flow ledger.
- **Property Records**: Agreement for sale, title clearance certificate, municipal / gram panchayat layout approval.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Overcomes rigid corporate underwriting barriers for self-employed and informal earners.
- Doorstep relationship executive support through every step of property verification.
