# IOB Subha Gruha Housing Loan - Indian Overseas Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IOB Subha Gruha is the core residential mortgage scheme from Indian Overseas Bank (a premier public sector bank). The product finances the purchase of ready-to-move houses or flats, under-construction apartments from builders/developers, construction of residential homes on owned land, and plot acquisition with construction.

- **Joint Loan Facility**: Income of spouse, working children, or parents can be aggregated to maximize loan eligibility.
- **Nil Prepayment Penalties**: Complete waiver of prepayment charges on floating rate home loans.
- **Concessional Green Variant**: Option to avail IOB Harit Subhagruha for certified sustainable housing.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IOB RLLR (Repo Linked).
- **Floating Rates**: 7.25% to 9.15% p.a., determined by credit score:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.25% - 7.50% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.50% - 7.85% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 7.85% - 8.45% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.45% - 9.15% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest discount for women primary applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians, NRIs, and PIOs.
- **Age Window**:
  - Minimum Age: 21 years.
  - Maximum Age: 60 to 70 years at loan maturity (if age is above 55, spouse or legal heir must join as co-applicant).
- **Employment History**: Minimum 2 years of steady employment or 3 years of established business practice.
- **Net Take-Home**: Minimum 40% to 50% take-home salary after factoring all loan EMIs.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized based on repayment ability.
- **LTV Caps**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via IOB savings account, NACH / ECS mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.50% of loan amount (Min Rs. 2,000, Max Rs. 20,000) + GST. Periodically waived during festive promotions.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips and Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts (Self-employed).
- **Property Documentation**: Registered agreement for sale, link title documents for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Wide branch presence across South India and major national metropolitan centers.
- Top-up loan (Subha Gruha Top Up) available after 1 year of satisfactory repayment.
