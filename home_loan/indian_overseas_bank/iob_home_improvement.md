# IOB Home Improvement Scheme - Indian Overseas Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IOB Home Improvement Scheme is a dedicated retail housing finance facility curated for existing house owners who wish to renovate, modernize, re-tile, paint, or undertake structural repairs and extensions on their residential property.

- **Dedicated Renovation Funding**: Covers civil repairs, tiling, plumbing, electrical rewiring, wood-work, modular kitchens, and structural reinforcement.
- **Cheaper than Personal Loans**: Substantially more affordable interest rates compared to unsecured personal loans or home renovation consumer cards.
- **Comfortable Repayment Horizon**: Extended tenures up to 15 years, minimizing the monthly EMI burden.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IOB RLLR.
- **Floating Rates**: 7.75% to 9.60% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for women applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians and NRIs owning an existing residential property.
- **Age Span**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years at maturity.
- **Property Condition**: The house/apartment being improved must not be older than 30–35 years and must have a clear residual structural life.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 25 Lakhs to Rs. 50 Lakhs based on architect/engineer cost estimate.
- **Margin**: Minimum 20% to 25% of the renovation estimate (LTV up to 75% - 80%).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 15 years (180 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 3 to 6 months during the repair/renovation work.
- **Disbursement Mode**: Phased disbursement released directly to contractors/vendors or homeowner against work progress inspection.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.50% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: Salary slips and Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR (Self-employed).
- **Renovation Cost Estimate**: Detailed itemized estimate prepared and signed by a chartered engineer or certified architect.
- **Title Documents**: Existing registered title deed of the property, tax paid receipt, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Protects property value and improves residential comfort at standard mortgage pricing.
- Available for both standalone houses and apartment flats.
