# ICICI Regular Home Loan - ICICI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
ICICI Regular Home Loan is one of India's leading private retail housing loan solutions. It finances the purchase of ready-to-move flats and villas, under-construction residential properties, plot purchase plus construction, self-construction of residential houses, and balance transfer of existing home loans.

- **Fastest Digital Sanctions**: Instant digital approval via ICICI iMobile Pay and net banking portal.
- **Simplified Processing**: Minimal documentation requirements for pre-qualified and pre-approved ICICI account holders.
- **Nil Foreclosure Charges**: Zero prepayment or foreclosure charges for individual floating rate borrowers.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: ICICI Real Estate Repo Linked Rate (I-RRR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.45% - 7.75% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.75% - 8.10% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.10% - 8.55% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.55% - 9.10% p.a.
- **Special Women Concession**: 0.05% interest discount when woman applicant is primary borrower.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians, NRIs, and PIOs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years (salaried) and 70 years (self-employed).
- **Work Experience**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years for self-employed in same business line.
- **Minimum Net Income**: Rs. 25,000 net monthly salary for salaried; Rs. 3 Lakhs annual profit for self-employed.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 10 Crores+ based on FOIR and property valuation.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential units.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via ICICI Bank savings account, NACH mandate, or auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% to 1.00% of loan amount (Min Rs. 3,000) + GST. Periodically discounted during festive drives.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' business operative bank statements.
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement to sell, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep document pick-up and personalized relationship manager support.
- Pre-approved loan offers accessible on ICICI iMobile Pay app.
