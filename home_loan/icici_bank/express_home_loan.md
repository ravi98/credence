# ICICI Express Home Loan - ICICI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
ICICI Express Home Loan is an online-first digital mortgage facility enabling homebuyers to secure an instant, provisional sanction letter entirely paperless within a few clicks through ICICI Bank's digital portal or iMobile app.

- **Instant Online Sanction Letter**: Generates a downloadable, legally valid provisional in-principle sanction letter in minutes.
- **Minimal Documentation**: Integrates with Account Aggregator (AA) framework and DigiLocker for instant automated financial verification.
- **Full Digital Tracking**: Track real-time application and legal/technical verification status on your mobile phone.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: ICICI I-RRR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 7.45% to 9.10% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest discount applies.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians and NRIs (both salaried and self-employed).
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years (salaried) and 70 years (self-employed).
- **Minimum Monthly Salary**: Rs. 25,000 net monthly salary.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+ required for automated STP approval.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Digital pre-sanctions up to Rs. 5 Crores.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Up to 90% for loans up to Rs. 30 Lakhs; up to 80% for Rs. 30 Lakhs - Rs. 75 Lakhs; up to 75% for loans above Rs. 75 Lakhs.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions from ICICI savings account, auto-debit, NACH.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Discounted express processing fee (often flat Rs. 6,500 + GST under online promotional campaigns) or 0.50%.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Identity & Address (Instant via DigiLocker)**: Aadhaar, PAN.
- **Income Proof (Instant via Account Aggregator)**: Automated 6 months' bank statement fetching via RBI Account Aggregator framework, or uploaded salary slips/ITR.
- **Property Documentation (Post-Sanction Stage)**: Title documents, allotment letter, builder agreement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast-track underwriting drastically cuts turnaround time from weeks to days.
- Zero foreclosure charges on floating rates.
