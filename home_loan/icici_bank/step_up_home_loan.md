# ICICI Step Up Home Loan - ICICI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
ICICI Step Up Home Loan is structured specifically for young, upwardly mobile salaried professionals working with premier corporates, multinationals, and top technology firms. It addresses the reality that young professionals currently earning moderate salaries will experience significant salary increments over the coming 3 to 7 years.

- **Up to 20% Higher Loan Eligibility**: Enables borrowers to qualify for a loan amount up to 20% higher than traditional underwriting calculations.
- **Two-Tiered Repayment Structure**:
  - Phase 1 (Years 1 to 3 or 5): Borrower pays lower, interest-only payments, preserving liquid cash flow.
  - Phase 2 (Remaining Loan Term): Regular, stepped-up amortizing EMIs covering both principal and interest.
- **Enables Earlier Home Acquisition**: Allows young executives to buy larger, centrally located homes earlier in life.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: ICICI I-RRR.
- **Floating Rate Range**: 7.60% to 9.25% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried individuals working with select corporate entities recognized by ICICI Bank. (Not available to self-employed individuals).
- **Age Ceiling**: Maximum age up to 40 years at the time of loan sanction.
- **Minimum Net Monthly Salary**: Rs. 20,000 to Rs. 30,000 per month.
- **No Existing Home Loan**: The applicant must not have an existing active housing loan obligation.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Maximum Sanction Quantum**: Up to Rs. 3 Crores.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 20 years (240 months).
- **Initial Term**: 3 to 5 years (interest servicing only).
- **Secondary Term**: Remaining tenure (amortized stepped-up EMI).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% to 1.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for individual floating rate borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Employment Proof**: Corporate appointment letter, employee ID, 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Property Documentation**: Registered agreement for sale, sanctioned building blueprints, 30-year non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero lock-in period for making partial prepayments during secondary phase.
- Seamlessly integrated into ICICI iMobile digital ecosystem.
