# PSB Apna Ghar (Apna Vihar) Home Loan - Punjab & Sind Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PSB Apna Ghar (historically documented in regional schemes as PSB Apna Vihar) is the primary retail housing finance product from Punjab & Sind Bank (a reputed nationalized public sector bank). It supports purchasing ready-to-move or under-construction houses/apartments, constructing residential units on owned plots, plot acquisition with construction, and home renovations.

- **Flexible Repayment Horizons**: Tenures up to 30 years (with extended variants up to 40 years for young applicants).
- **Zero Prepayment Penalty**: Full waiver of prepayment and foreclosure charges for individual floating rate borrowers.
- **Concessional Public Sector Pricing**: Preferential rate concessions for government employees, defense personnel, and women applicants.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PSB-EBLR (External Benchmark Lending Rate linked to RBI Repo Rate).
- **Floating Rates**: 7.30% to 9.75% p.a.
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.30% - 7.60% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.60% - 8.00% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.00% - 8.65% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.65% - 9.75% p.a.
- **Concessions**: 0.05% interest rebate for permanent female government and public sector employees.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indian citizens and NRIs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 65–70 years at loan maturity.
- **Employment Stability**: Minimum 2 years of service for salaried; 3 years for self-employed/business applicants.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized based on repayment capacity and FOIR.
- **LTV Caps**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%
  - Repairs & Renovations: Capped at Rs. 20 Lakhs.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via PSB savings account, NACH / ECS mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.25% to 0.50% of loan amount + GST. Frequently waived during festive campaigns.
- **Prepayment Penalties**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips and Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts (Self-employed).
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement of sale, link title documents for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- PSB Apna Ghar Top Up facility available up to Rs. 10 Lakhs for personal needs after 12 months of clean repayment.
- PSB Kisan Home Loan available for farmers with agricultural income documentation.
