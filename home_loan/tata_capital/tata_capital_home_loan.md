# Tata Capital Home Loan - Tata Capital

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Tata Capital Home Loan is a trusted, versatile mortgage finance solution provided by Tata Capital Financial Services Limited (a subsidiary of Tata Sons). It finances purchasing ready-to-move-in homes, under-construction apartments from leading developers, residential land/plots, self-construction of houses, home expansion/renovation, and home loan balance transfers.

- **Backed by Tata Trust & Heritage**: High governance standards, absolute fee transparency, and no hidden charges.
- **Fast Digital Approvals**: Online application journey with provisional in-principle sanction in as little as 48 hours.
- **Top-Up Mortgage Access**: Existing borrowers can seamlessly avail top-up home loans without pledging additional collateral.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Tata Capital Retail Prime Lending Rate (RPLR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.50% - 7.85% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.85% - 8.35% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.35% - 9.05% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 9.05% - 9.85% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for female primary applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians, NRIs, and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs).
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years at loan maturity.
- **Employment Stability**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years audited business continuity for self-employed.
- **Minimum Net Income**: Rs. 30,000 net monthly salary (salaried) or Rs. 3.5 Lakhs annual profit (self-employed).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+ preferred.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Minimum Loan Amount**: Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- **Maximum Loan Amount**: Rs. 7.5 Crores.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: NACH mandate, online portal auto-debit, or standing instructions.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% to 1.00% of the loan amount + GST. Regularly discounted during seasonal promotions.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate individual borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' business bank statements.
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement to sell, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep relationship management and digital document upload.
- Full tax benefits eligible under Section 80C (principal) and Section 24(b) (interest).
