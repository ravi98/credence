# Cent Home Loan - Central Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Cent Home Loan is the standard residential credit program offered by Central Bank of India (one of India's oldest nationalized public sector banks). It finances acquisition, construction of new residential units, purchase of existing houses/flats, plot purchase with construction, and home extension/repairs.

- **Generous Moratorium Window**: Up to 48 months moratorium for long-term construction schedules.
- **Extended Age Limit**: Borrowers can repay up to 75 years of age.
- **Processing Fee Concessions**: Regular 100% processing fee waivers offered during promotional festival periods.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Central Bank of India RLLR (Repo Linked).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.15% - 7.45% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.45% - 7.85% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 7.85% - 8.35% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.35% - 8.95% p.a.
- **Concessions**: 0.05% interest rebate for women primary applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Profile**: Salaried employees, self-employed professionals, businessmen, farmers, and NRIs.
- **Age Window**:
  - Minimum Age: 18 years.
  - Maximum Age: 75 years at loan maturity.
- **Service Stability**: Minimum 1 year continuous service for salaried; 2 years established track record for self-employed.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Based on Net Take-Home (NTH) / FOIR up to 60-65% of income.
- **Margin / LTV**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%
  - Repairs & Renovation: Up to 75% (Capped at Rs. 10 Lakhs in rural/semi-urban and Rs. 20 Lakhs in urban/metro).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months) or age 75 years (10 years for repairs).
- **Moratorium**:
  - Up to 48 months for construction of house.
  - Up to 36 months for purchase of plot + construction.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions from Central Bank account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.50% of loan amount (Maximum Rs. 20,000) + GST.
- **Foreclosure Charges**: Nil for individual floating rate borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts (Self-employed).
- **Property Records**: Registered sale agreement, link title documents for 30 years, sanctioned building blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Wide rural, semi-urban, and urban branch reach across pan-India.
- Straightforward balance transfer options from existing non-banking lenders.
