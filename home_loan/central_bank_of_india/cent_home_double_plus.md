# Cent Home Double Plus - Central Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Cent Home Double Plus is a versatile 3-in-1 combo mortgage solution developed by Central Bank of India. It combines a primary housing loan with an overdraft facility and an additional personal term loan against the equitable mortgage of residential property.

- **Combo Structure**:
  1. Primary Housing Term Loan: For construction or purchase of home.
  2. Overdraft Facility: For cash flow management, parking surplus funds, and minimizing daily interest.
  3. Additional Term Loan: For personal needs like home furnishing, interior decoration, debt consolidation, or emergency requirements.
- **Enhanced Financial Liquidity**: Provides emergency liquidity on demand without the need to take high-cost uncollateralized personal loans.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Central Bank of India RLLR.
- **Interest Slabs**: 7.35% to 9.20% p.a.
  - Primary home loan and overdraft components linked directly to RLLR.
- **Concessions**: 0.05% interest rebate for women applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Profile**: Salaried individuals, professionals, business entrepreneurs, and agriculturists with demonstrable stable earnings.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 75 years at loan completion.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.
- **Property Requirement**: Clear, marketable title of residential property being mortgaged.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Primary Home Loan**: Up to 75% - 90% LTV as per RBI guidelines.
- **Overdraft & Term Loan Combo**: Aggregate sanctioned exposure capped within 75% of fair market property valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Home Loan Component**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Term Loan Component**: Up to 7 to 10 years.
- **Overdraft Component**: Reviewed annually with reducing drawing power.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of aggregate sanction (Max Rs. 20,000) + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL on floating rate accounts.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips and Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited financial statements and ITR (Self-employed).
- **Property Documentation**: Registered sale agreement/deed, non-encumbrance certificate (30 years), approved layout plan.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- All-in-one financial product eliminating multiple disparate loans.
- Substantial interest savings via the overdraft component.
