# IB Home Loan Plus - Indian Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IB Home Loan Plus is a top-up mortgage and equity financing product offered by Indian Bank to existing home loan borrowers or outright owners of residential properties. It allows homeowners to unlock the built-up equity in their residential property for any legitimate personal, educational, medical, or business purpose.

- **Multipurpose Flexibility**: Funds can be utilized for home renovation, child's education, family weddings, medical expenses, or business working capital.
- **Cheaper than Personal Loans**: Substantially lower interest rate compared to unsecured personal loans or credit card borrowing.
- **Simplified Processing**: Faster documentation for existing Indian Bank mortgage borrowers since property title deeds and security are already verified.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IB-EBLR.
- **Floating Rate Range**: 8.25% to 10.10% p.a.
- **Concessions**: 0.05% rebate for women primary applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Existing satisfactory Indian Bank home loan borrowers (minimum 12 months of clean repayment history) or individuals offering clear, unencumbered self-occupied residential property.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 70 years at maturity.
- **Income Profile**: Salaried or self-employed with adequate Debt Service Coverage / FOIR headroom.
- **Credit Track Record**: CIBIL 680+ with zero 30+ DPD defaults in the past 12 months.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Maximum Quantum**: Up to Rs. 1 Crore.
- **Combined LTV Limit**: Total outstanding primary home loan plus IB Home Plus loan cannot exceed 75% of the prevailing market valuation of the mortgaged property.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 15 years (180 months) or remaining tenure of primary home loan, whichever is lower.
- **Moratorium**: Nil or up to 3 months.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions / NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.50% of loan amount + GST.
- **Foreclosure Charges**: Nil for individual floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar.
- **Income Documents**: Latest 3 months' pay slips / 2 years' ITR, 6 months' operative bank account statements.
- **Loan Track**: Clean repayment track record of primary home loan.
- **Property Documentation**: Existing property mortgage verification / updated title search.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Immediate access to substantial low-cost liquidity against mortgaged residential asset.
- No restriction on the end-use of funds (except speculative/capital market trading).
