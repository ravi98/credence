# IB Plot Loan - Indian Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IB Plot Loan is a specialized land acquisition loan offered by Indian Bank to individuals wishing to buy a residential plot/site for future house construction.

- **Mandatory Construction Clause**: The borrower must commence residential construction within 2 to 3 years from the date of loan disbursement.
- **Location-Based Caps**: Clear tiered sanction limits tailored for rural, semi-urban, urban, and metro regions.
- **Conversion to Housing Loan**: Seamless transition into an IB Home Construction Loan once house building starts.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IB-EBLR.
- **Floating Interest Rates**: 8.15% to 10.40% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for women applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians and NRIs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years at maturity.
- **Employment History**: Minimum 2 years continuous employment for salaried; 3 years audited operations for self-employed.
- **Income Formula**: Loan eligibility computed up to 30 times the gross monthly income.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Maximum Sanction Limits**:
  - Rural Areas: Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
  - Semi-Urban Areas: Up to Rs. 1 Crore.
  - Urban & Metro Areas: Up to Rs. 2 to 3 Crores.
- **Margin**: Minimum 25% of the registered plot cost / guideline value (LTV up to 75%).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 15 years (180 months).
- **Moratorium**: Maximum 12 to 24 months, until construction commences.
- **Repayment**: Monthly EMI via NACH / Standing Instruction.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.50% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips / 2 years' Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR & P&L (Self-employed).
- **Plot Documents**:
  - Approved layout plan from Development Authority (e.g., BDA, CMDA, MMRDA, DDA, DTCP, or local town planning).
  - Registered title deed of the seller with 30 years' parent deeds.
  - Non-encumbrance certificate (EC) for 30 years.
  - Possession certificate and tax paid receipts.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Ensures legal verification of title and layout validity through empanelled bank lawyers.
- Option to apply for composite plot + construction loan in a single application.
