# IB Home Loan - Indian Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IB Home Loan is Indian Bank's flagship retail mortgage product. It supports financing for buying ready-built apartments/houses, construction on owned plots, purchase of land with immediate construction, and expansion/renovation of existing residential property.

- **High Quantum Limits**: Offers funding up to Rs. 10 Crores across urban, semi-urban, and metropolitan centers.
- **IB Home Advantage**: Optional overdraft feature allowing borrowers to deposit surplus liquidity to offset interest costs.
- **No Foreclosure Charges**: Nil penalty for floating rate loans.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IB-EBLR (External Benchmark Lending Rate linked to RBI Repo Rate).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.25% - 7.50% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.50% - 7.85% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 7.85% - 8.40% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.40% - 9.40% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for women primary borrowers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Borrower Types**: Resident Indians, Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs).
- **Age Parameters**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 70 years at completion of loan repayment.
- **Employment Requirement**:
  - Salaried: Minimum 2 years of continuous employment.
  - Self-Employed: Minimum 3 years in profitable business or professional practice.
- **Minimum Net Take-Home Pay**: Minimum 40% of gross salary after all EMI deductions.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Sanction Limit**: Up to Rs. 10 Crores based on income criteria and property evaluation.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium Period**: Up to 36 months for under-construction houses or until possession certificate.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions, ECS / NACH mandate, or IndOASIS digital banking app.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.25% to 0.40% (Min Rs. 2,000, Max Rs. 20,000) + GST. (Subject to full festival waivers).
- **Legal & Valuation Fees**: Paid at actuals to panel advocates and valuers.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for individual floating rate borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' salary slips, Form 16 (2 years), 6 months' bank statements for salaried; 3 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, and Profit & Loss accounts for self-employed.
- **Property Documentation**: Registered sale agreement, link title documents for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, and non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast-track digital in-principle sanctions through Indian Bank's IndOASIS mobile platform.
- Top-up home loan facility available after satisfactory track record.
