# LIC HFL Griha Varishtha - LIC Housing Finance

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
LIC HFL Griha Varishtha is a pioneering, specialized residential mortgage product tailored specifically for retired individuals and senior citizens who receive pension benefits under Defined Benefit Pension Schemes (DBPS). It enables senior citizens to purchase ready-to-move homes, under-construction apartments, or self-construct residential dwellings.

- **Repayment Horizon up to Age 80**: Crucially breaks traditional mortgage age barriers by allowing repayment tenures up to the age of 80 years.
- **Joint Application with Legal Heirs**: Pensioners can club the pension entitlement with the active salaried income of their adult earning children/heirs to maximize total loan sanction capacity.
- **Dedicated Senior Citizen Channel**: Priority underwriting through LIC HFL's extensive pan-India network and 'HomY' digital application platform.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: LIC HFL Prime Lending Rate (LHPLR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.60% - 7.85% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.85% - 8.25% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.25% - 8.75% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.75% - 9.15% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for female applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Retired employees of Central/State Government, Defense, PSUs, PSBs, and recognized corporations receiving defined pensions.
- **Age Bounds**: Entry age up to 65 years; Exit age up to 80 years at loan maturity.
- **Income Assessment**: Based on regular monthly pension credits evidenced via bank statements and PPO, clubbed with children's income if co-borrowers.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 680+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 5 Crores.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years or age 80 years, whichever is earlier.
- **Moratorium**: Standard construction moratorium for under-construction residential properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via pension credit bank account, NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.25% to 0.50% + GST.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: NIL for floating rate individual borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Pension Documents**:
  - Pension Payment Order (PPO) copy.
  - Pension passbook / last 6 months' bank statements showing regular pension credits.
  - Co-applicant salary slips and Form 16 (if clubbing children's income).
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement for sale, sanctioned blueprint, 30 years' title search report, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Solves the critical housing gap for pensioners who are otherwise turned down by commercial retail banks.
- Straightforward transition of title and mortgage obligations to co-borrower legal heirs upon succession.
