# LIC HFL Griha Suvidha - LIC Housing Finance

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
LIC HFL Griha Suvidha is an inclusive housing loan scheme crafted specifically for the "missing middle" segment of the Indian housing market. It caters to borrowers who receive part of their salary in cash, contract/temporary workers, or small micro-business owners who lack formal audited financial documentation.

- **Mortgage Guarantee Backed**: Backed by a recognized Mortgage Guarantee institution (such as IMGC - India Mortgage Guarantee Corporation), mitigating lender risk while expanding borrower eligibility.
- **Banking Surrogate Underwriting**: Analyzes average monthly bank balances and transaction patterns rather than relying purely on tax returns or corporate pay slips.
- **PMAY Compatibility**: Eligible for interest subsidies under Pradhan Mantri Awas Yojana (PMAY-CLSS) for affordable housing applicants.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: LIC HFL Prime Lending Rate (LHPLR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 8.15% to 10.25% p.a.
- **Variant Sub-pricing**: The 'Asha' sub-variant offers dedicated terms for Class IV / Group D government staff and 2-year ITR self-employed borrowers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Salaried individuals with informal cash components, contract professionals, small retail traders, and self-employed artisans.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years at maturity.
- **Income Parameters**: Verified through surrogate banking footprints, utility bill payments, and on-site business turnover assessment.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 600+ or New-to-Credit (NTC) accepted with mortgage guarantee backing.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs to Rs. 1 Crore.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Up to 85% - 90% of property market value.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Standard construction moratorium for under-construction residential properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: NACH mandate, post-dated cheques, or digital auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of loan amount + GST (Plus nominal one-time mortgage guarantee premium).
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**: Salary certificate, 6–12 months' bank passbook/statement, proof of business premises (rent agreement, electricity bill, trade license).
- **Property Records**: Agreement for sale, title search report, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Mortgage guarantee protection allows higher loan eligibility for non-standard profiles.
- Expedited provisional sanction through LIC HFL 'HomY' mobile application.
