# SBI Step Up Home Loan (Flexipay) - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Step Up Home Loan (also marketed under the Flexipay umbrella) is tailored specifically for young salaried professionals and executives who are in early stages of their career. Recognizing their high future income growth trajectory, SBI offers higher loan sanction amounts (up to 20% higher eligibility) by factoring in graduated repayments.

- **Stepped Repayment Structure**: Lower EMIs during initial 3 to 5 years, stepping up progressively as income increases.
- **Pre-EMI / Interest-Only Moratorium**: Option to pay only pre-EMI interest during construction/moratorium period.
- **Enhanced Affordability**: Enables purchase of larger, better-located residential homes earlier in career.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Linked to SBI EBLR (Repo Linked).
- **Floating Rate Bracket**: 7.45% p.a. to 9.15% p.a. depending on CIBIL score:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.45% - 7.75%
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.75% - 8.05%
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.05% - 8.45%
  - CIBIL 650 - 699: 8.45% - 8.95%
- **Women Borrower Concession**: 5 bps (0.05%) rebate available.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Segment**: Salaried employees working with reputed MNCs, listed private firms, PSUs, Central/State Governments.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 45 years at loan sanction (ensuring remaining working horizon).
- **Minimum Work Experience**: Minimum 2 years of verifiable continuous employment.
- **Minimum Monthly Take-Home Income**: Rs. 30,000 per month.
- **CIBIL Score**: 700 and above.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Minimum Loan Amount**: Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- **Maximum Loan Amount**: No upper statutory cap; calculated dynamically based on stepped income models.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: 90%
  - Above Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (or up to age of retirement, maximum 60 years).
- **Repayment Phasing**:
  - Phase 1 (Years 1 to 3 or 5): Lower, stepped-down EMI or interest-only service.
  - Phase 2 (Remaining Tenure): Stepped-up equated monthly installments factoring projected salary increments.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.35% of the loan sanction amount (Min Rs. 2,000, Max Rs. 10,000) + GST.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: Zero penalty on floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Identity & Address**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport / Voter ID.
- **Employment & Income**:
  - Latest 3 months' salary slips with break-up of components.
  - Form 16 / ITR for last 2 years.
  - Last 6 months' salary bank account statements.
  - Employment confirmation letter / corporate ID card.
- **Property Documents**: Registered sale agreement, builder allotment letter, approved floor plans, title search report.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Overcomes standard Debt-to-Income / FOIR constraints for young earners.
- Zero prepayment charges allow accelerated principal repayment when bonus or stock options vest.
