# SBI Privilege Home Loan - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Privilege Home Loan is a dedicated housing finance solution exclusively designed for government sector employees, including Central Government, State Government, Public Sector Banks (PSBs), Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs), and other autonomous statutory authorities.

- **Zero Processing Fee**: Complete 100% processing fee waiver.
- **Check-off Concession**: Substantial interest rate concessions if salary check-off arrangement is executed with the employer.
- **Extended Age Horizon**: Loan repayment permissible up to age 75 years (pension period factored in).

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: SBI EBLR (Repo Linked).
- **Floating Rates**: 7.15% to 8.65% p.a.
  - With Salary Check-off: Starts at 7.15% p.a. for CIBIL >= 800.
  - Without Check-off: Starts at 7.25% p.a.
- **Women Borrower Concession**: 5 bps (0.05%) rebate available.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Eligible Entities**: Permanent employees of:
  - Central Government (Civil, Railways, Postal, Central Secretariat, etc.).
  - State Government departments.
  - Public Sector Undertakings (Navratna, Maharatna, Miniratna).
  - Public Sector Banks and financial institutions.
- **Age Criteria**:
  - Entry Age: Minimum 18 years.
  - Maximum Exit Age: Up to 75 years (repayment continues through post-retirement pension).
- **Service Status**: Confirmed/Permanent service status.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 650+ (preferable 750+ for lowest rate tier).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Loan Amount**: Determined by remaining service years, pension calculations, and LTV.
- **LTV Limits**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: 90%
  - Above Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years or age 75 years, whichever is earlier.
- **Pension Coverage**: Pension income of applicant and spouse can be clubbed to compute extended repayment capability post-superannuation.
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction properties.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: NIL (100% Waived).
- **Prepayment Penalty**: Zero penalty for early repayment or balance transfer.
- **Stamp Duty & Valuation**: Actuals as per local jurisdiction and valuer charges.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Government Official Identity Card.
- **Income Proof**:
  - Latest 3 months' salary slips with official seal/digital signature.
  - Form 16 / ITR for last 2 years.
  - Last 6 months' salary account bank statements.
  - Undertaking letter / Check-off authorization from employer DDO (Drawing and Disbursing Officer), if applicable.
- **Property Documents**: Allotment letter, title deed, NOC from housing board / society.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Lower interest burden compared to standard retail home loans.
- Smooth digital processing through YONO and dedicated PSB relationship desks.
