# SBI Regular Home Loan - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
The SBI Regular Home Loan is the flagship housing finance product from India's largest public sector bank. It covers financing for purchase of ready-built flats/houses, under-construction properties, construction of residential units, plot purchase plus construction, and home extension or repair.

- **Interest Rate Calculation**: Daily reducing balance method.
- **Interest Concession**: Special concession of 5 bps (0.05%) available for women borrowers.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: Zero penalty on floating rate loans for individual borrowers.
- **Overdraft Option**: Can be linked with SBI Maxgain (home loan overdraft variant).

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Base Rate / Benchmark**: Linked to SBI's External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR), tied to the RBI Repo Rate.
- **Floating Rate Bracket**: 7.25% p.a. to 8.95% p.a., varying according to CIBIL / credit score bands:
  - CIBIL 800+: 7.25% - 7.55%
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.55% - 7.85%
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 7.85% - 8.25%
  - CIBIL 650 - 699: 8.25% - 8.75%
  - CIBIL 550 - 649: 8.75% - 8.95%

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Nationality**: Resident Indian.
- **Entry Age**: Minimum 18 years.
- **Exit Age**: Maximum 70 years at the time of loan maturity.
- **Employment Category**:
  - Salaried employees of Central/State Govt, PSUs, Reputed Corporates.
  - Self-employed professionals (Doctors, CAs, Architects, Engineers, Consultants).
  - Self-employed businessmen with minimum 3 years of continuous operations.
- **Minimum Net Monthly Income**: Typically Rs. 15,000 for salaried and Rs. 25,000 for self-employed applicants.
- **Minimum Credit Score**: 650+ preferred (lower scores subject to additional risk spread).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
Loan amount is calculated based on repayment capacity, Debt-to-Income (FOIR) ratio, and property valuation according to RBI Loan-to-Value (LTV) limits:
- **Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs**: Up to 90% LTV (Minimum 10% borrower margin).
- **Above Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs**: Up to 80% LTV (Minimum 20% borrower margin).
- **Above Rs. 75 Lakhs**: Up to 75% LTV (Minimum 25% borrower margin).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years or until applicant attains 70 years of age, whichever is earlier.
- **Moratorium Period**: Up to 36 months for under-construction properties, or until possession/construction completion.
- **Repayment Mode**: Equated Monthly Installments (EMI) via NACH / ECS / Standing Instruction from SBI savings account.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.35% of the loan amount (Minimum Rs. 2,000, Maximum Rs. 10,000) plus GST. Waived periodically during festive campaigns.
- **Advocate Investigation / Title Verification Fee**: Actual fees paid to panel advocate.
- **Valuer / Inspection Fee**: Actual fees payable to empanelled property valuer.
- **CIBIL / Credit Bureau Report Fee**: Rs. 50 to Rs. 100 per applicant.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure Charges**: Nil for floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
### KYC & Identity Proof
- PAN Card (Mandatory), Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID, or Driving License.
- Proof of Current Residential Address (Utility bills, Aadhaar, Lease deed).
- 3 passport-sized photographs of applicant and co-applicants.

### Income Documents (Salaried)
- Last 3 months' salary slips.
- Form 16 (Part A & B) or ITR for last 2 assessment years.
- Last 6 months' bank account statement showing salary credits.

### Income Documents (Self-Employed)
- Last 3 years' audited Financial Statements (Balance Sheet, Profit & Loss A/c).
- Income Tax Returns (ITR) with computation of income for last 3 years.
- Business profile, proof of business existence (GST registration, Shop Act license).
- Last 12 months' operative bank current account statements.

### Property Documents
- Registered Agreement for Sale / Sale Deed.
- Allotment letter and possession certificate (for builder purchases).
- Approved building layout plan from competent civic authority.
- Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for 13–30 years.
- NOC from society / builder.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Wide branch network and doorstep documentation assistance.
- Complimentary SBI Credit Card and insurance tie-ups.
- Facility to add co-borrowers (spouse, parents, siblings) to enhance borrowing eligibility.
