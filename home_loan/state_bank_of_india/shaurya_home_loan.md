# SBI Shaurya Home Loan - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Shaurya Home Loan is an exclusive, dedicated housing finance scheme crafted for defense personnel and armed forces of India. It caters to personnel of the Indian Army, Indian Navy, Indian Air Force, and Paramilitary Forces (CRPF, BSF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, Assam Rifles, Coast Guard).

- **Complete Processing Fee Waiver**: 100% discount on processing charges.
- **Concessional Interest Rates**: Preferential pricing lower than standard retail home loan rates.
- **Extended Repayment Window**: Maximum loan exit age up to 75 years, ensuring seamless repayment transition into military pension.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Linked to SBI External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR).
- **Floating Rate Bracket**: 7.15% p.a. to 8.65% p.a.
  - Defense personnel with DSP (Defense Salary Package) account: Lowest tier starting at 7.15% p.a.
- **Women Borrower Concession**: 5 bps (0.05%) rebate available if woman is sole or primary co-borrower.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Serving and retired personnel of:
  - Indian Army
  - Indian Air Force
  - Indian Navy
  - Indian Coast Guard
  - Paramilitary Forces (BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP, SSB, NSG)
- **Age Window**:
  - Minimum Age: 18 years.
  - Maximum Age at Loan Maturity: Up to 75 years.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 650 and above.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Tailored to military rank, basic pay, military service pay (MSP), allowances, and pension entitlement.
- **Regulatory LTV Caps**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Above Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years or age 75 years.
- **Repayment Mechanism**: Standing Instruction via Defense Salary Package (DSP) / Central Armed Police Salary Package (CAPSP) account or Defense Pension account.
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction houses.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fees**: Zero / Fully Waived.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure Fees**: Nil.
- **Administrative / Legal Charges**: At actuals.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: Defense Service Identity Card, PAN Card, Aadhaar Card.
- **Service & Income Proof**:
  - Last 3 months' salary slips indicating rank, service number, and allowances.
  - Form 16 / ITR for last 2 years.
  - Last 6 months' statement of DSP / CAPSP salary account.
  - Service book extract / Certificate from Commanding Officer (CO).
  - Pension Payment Order (PPO) copy for ex-servicemen/veterans.
- **Property Documentation**: Approved building plan, registered agreement of sale, title clearance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Dedicated defense customer support desks in cantonment and military station branches.
- Family members can be co-borrowers to enhance loan sanction limits.
- Easy branch portability across military postings in India.
