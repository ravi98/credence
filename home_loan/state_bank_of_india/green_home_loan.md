# SBI Green Home Loan - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Green Home Loan is an environmental and sustainability-focused mortgage variant launched by State Bank of India to promote eco-friendly residential architecture, energy-efficient housing, and renewable energy adoption.

- **Preferential Green Pricing**: Special interest rate concession of 10 bps (0.10%) to 25 bps (0.25%) lower than standard home loans.
- **Processing Fee Discount**: Up to 50% reduction on standard administrative processing charges.
- **Certified Property Coverage**: Covers properties pre-certified or certified by recognized green rating councils (IGBC, GRIHA, LEED India, or EDGE).

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: SBI EBLR (Repo Linked).
- **Floating Interest Rates**: 7.15% p.a. to 8.85% p.a.
  - Baseline rate reduced by 10 bps to 25 bps for certified green projects.
- **Additional Concessions**: 5 bps rebate for women borrowers stackable on green concession.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Borrower Profile**: Resident Indians (Salaried and Self-Employed).
- **Age Bounds**: 18 years to 70 years at maturity.
- **Property Green Criteria**: The housing project or independent bungalow must satisfy at least one of the following:
  - IGBC (Indian Green Building Council) Certification (Silver, Gold, or Platinum).
  - GRIHA (Green Rating for Integrated Habitat Assessment) 3-star rating or higher.
  - LEED India (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design) Gold or Platinum.
  - Incorporation of on-grid rooftop solar PV, rainwater harvesting, and certified energy-saving insulation.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 650+ (750+ gets best pricing slab).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to 90% of total project cost (including cost of green/solar installations).
- **LTV Caps**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years.
- **Moratorium Period**: Up to 36 months for under-construction certified green residential projects.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: Concessional (50% of standard fee, typically 0.175% to 0.20% or flat Rs. 2,000 to Rs. 5,000 + GST).
- **Prepayment Charges**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: Salary slips (3 months) / ITR with computation (3 years), bank statements (6-12 months).
- **Green Compliance Certificate**:
  - Certificate of Rating from IGBC / GRIHA / LEED provided by developer or licensed environmental auditor.
  - Project specification sheet highlighting energy efficiency, water harvesting, and solar installations.
- **Title Documents**: Sale agreement, title search report, sanctioned building blueprint.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Promotes long-term operational utility savings (lower electricity and water bills).
- Faster loan processing through expedited green-channel approvals.
