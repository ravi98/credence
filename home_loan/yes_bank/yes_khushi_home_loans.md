# YES Khushi Affordable Home Loan - YES Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
YES Khushi Home Loans is a dedicated affordable housing program launched by YES Bank to cater to borrowers who face challenges accessing formal credit due to limited or non-traditional income documentation. It finances purchase of ready-built homes, under-construction properties, resale units, self-construction, plot plus construction, and home renovation.

- **Minimal Documentation Requirement**: Evaluates borrower creditworthiness based on actual banking turnover, household cash-flow surrogate analysis, and localized field verification.
- **Compact Property Coverage**: Applicable for small compact homes starting from 200 sq. ft. (studio apartments, 1 RK, and 1 BHK units).
- **Inclusion of Informal Cash Incomes**: Accommodates micro-entrepreneurs, small shopkeepers, delivery partners, and service personnel who receive income in cash or through UPI transactions.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: YES Bank Affordable Housing Benchmark Rate.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 9.50% to 12.50% p.a. (Determined by risk grade and degree of documentation).
- **PMAY Compatibility**: Compatible with central and state affordable housing subsidy schemes.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Micro-business owners, drivers, traders, self-employed artisans, and entry-level salaried staff.
- **Minimum Family Income**: Minimum combined family income starting at Rs. 9,000 per month.
- **Age Bounds**:
  - Salaried: Up to 60 years at loan maturity.
  - Self-Employed: Up to 65 years at loan maturity.
- **Credit Track Record**: CIBIL 600+ or New-to-Credit (NTC) accepted.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Minimum Loan Amount**: Rs. 1 Lakh.
- **Maximum Loan Amount**: Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Up to 85% - 90% of property market valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 25 years (300 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 18 to 24 months for self-construction.
- **Repayment Modes**: NACH mandate, post-dated cheques (PDCs), or standing instruction.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 1.00% to 1.50% of the loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fees**: NIL for individual floating rate borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card (or Form 60), Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Flexible)**:
  - Salaried: Salary certificate from employer, 6 months' bank statement or passbook copy.
  - Self-Employed: Proof of business location, trade invoices, local references, and 6–12 months' bank passbook.
- **Property Records**: Sale agreement, municipal or panchayat approved building sketch, title search report.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep relationship executive assists with legal title verification.
- Enables first-generation formal borrowers to build a positive credit bureau footprint.
