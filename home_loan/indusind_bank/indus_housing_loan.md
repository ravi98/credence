# Indus Housing Loan - IndusInd Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Indus Housing Loan is IndusInd Bank's comprehensive residential credit solution. It caters to financing ready-to-move-in apartments, under-construction housing projects, independent residential construction, plot purchase with construction, home extension, and balance transfer of existing home loans.

- **Doorstep Concierge Service**: Complete doorstep relationship assistance from application pickup to final title disbursement.
- **Tailored Options for NRIs**: Dedicated NRI housing loan solutions with Power of Attorney (POA) assistance and NRE/NRO payment channels.
- **Zero Prepayment Penalties**: Floating rate loans carry zero prepayment or foreclosure fees.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IndusInd Bank Repo Linked Benchmark Lending Rate (RLLR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.65% - 7.95% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.95% - 8.35% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.35% - 8.90% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.90% - 9.45% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for women primary applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians, NRIs, and PIOs.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years (salaried) and 70 years (self-employed).
- **Work Stability**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years audited profitability for self-employed.
- **Minimum Monthly Salary**: Rs. 25,000 net monthly salary (salaried); Rs. 3 Lakhs annual net income (self-employed).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized up to Rs. 10 Crores based on repayment ability and FOIR up to 60%.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via IndusInd Bank account, NACH mandate, or net banking auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% to 1.00% of loan amount + GST. Regularly waived during promotional festival offers.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' business bank statements.
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement to sell, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick in-principle sanction with minimal turnaround time.
- Top-up home loan options available for personal and business liquidity after satisfactory repayment.
