# Axis Bank Regular Home Loan - Axis Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Axis Bank Regular Home Loan is a flexible and feature-rich housing loan offered by India's third-largest private sector bank. It covers financing for purchase of ready-built flats, under-construction apartments, independent villas, self-construction on owned plots, plot plus construction, and home balance transfers.

- **Shubh Aarambh Benefit**: 12 EMI waivers (4 EMIs waived at end of years 4, 8, and 12) for borrowers maintaining timely, disciplined repayments.
- **Doorstep Assistance**: Dedicated loan relationship managers provide complete doorstep document collection and assistance.
- **Fast End-to-End Processing**: Rapid appraisal through digital channels and automated bureau scoring.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Axis Bank Repo Linked Lending Rate (RLLR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.45% - 7.75% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.75% - 8.15% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.15% - 8.65% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.65% - 9.15% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate when female borrower is primary applicant.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians and NRIs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years (salaried) and 70 years (self-employed).
- **Employment Stability**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years audited continuity for self-employed.
- **Minimum Net Income**: Rs. 25,000 net monthly salary (salaried) or Rs. 3 Lakhs annual net profit (self-employed).
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+ preferred.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 10 Crores+ based on income multiplier and FOIR.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Axis Bank account, NACH / ECS mandates.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 1.00% of loan amount (Min Rs. 10,000) + GST. Full waivers regularly extended during festive and quarterly drives.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' business bank statements.
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement to sell, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- 12 EMI waiver feature rewards consistent on-time payment track record.
- Top-up home loan options available up to Rs. 50 Lakhs after 1 year.
