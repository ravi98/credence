# Axis Bank Asha Home Loan - Axis Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Axis Bank Asha Home Loan is an affordable housing finance scheme designed to bring homeownership within reach for lower-to-middle income groups (LIG/EWS). It caters to individuals who might have modest or non-standard income documentation, assessing cash-flow patterns through surrogate evaluation.

- **12 EMI Waivers**: Offers 12 EMI waivers at no extra cost (4 EMIs waived off at the end of the 4th, 8th, and 12th year) for customers with an uninterrupted on-time repayment track record.
- **Affordable Loan Amounts**: Ticket sizes from Rs. 1 Lakh up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- **Flexible Surrogate Underwriting**: Accommodates applicants with informal income streams, petty cash revenues, or local trade income.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Axis Bank Affordable Housing Benchmark.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 9.50% to 11.25% p.a.
- **Concessions**: Women co-applicants receive priority underwriting and interest rebates.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: First-time homebuyers, low-income salaried workers, artisans, shop owners, and transport operators.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years at maturity.
- **Minimum Combined Family Income**: Rs. 8,000 to Rs. 10,000 per month (depending on rural/urban location).
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 600+ considered, or New-to-Credit (NTC) applicants evaluated via banking transaction history.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Minimum Loan Amount**: Rs. 1 Lakh.
- **Maximum Loan Amount**: Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Up to 90% for loans up to Rs. 30 Lakhs; up to 80% for loans above Rs. 30 Lakhs.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Repayment Modes**: NACH mandate, post-dated cheques, or standing instruction.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 1.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**: Salary slips/salary certificate (Salaried); business dairy, bank passbook/statement (6-12 months), trade license/gumasta (Self-employed).
- **Property Records**: Agreement for sale, title clearance certificate, sanctioned layout or gram panchayat approval.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- 12 EMI waiver significantly lowers effective interest burden over the loan lifecycle.
- Doorstep relationship executive helps guide first-time borrowers through legal documentation.
