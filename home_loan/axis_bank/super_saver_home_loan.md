# Axis Bank Super Saver Home Loan - Axis Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Axis Bank Super Saver Home Loan is an overdraft-based mortgage product designed for borrowers with surplus liquid funds or fluctuating cash flows. It links the home loan account to a dedicated Super Saver current/savings operative account.

- **Interest Offset Advantage**: Any surplus deposit parked in the linked account directly reduces the outstanding principal balance on which daily interest is calculated.
- **Liquidity & Accessibility**: Surplus funds deposited are not locked away; the borrower can withdraw funds via ATM, UPI, net banking, or cheque books at any time without prepayment formalities.
- **Substantial Interest Savings**: Significantly shortens overall loan tenure and curtails total interest outgo without requiring rigid prepayment commitments.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Axis Bank RLLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 7.65% to 9.35% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians and NRIs (Salaried executives with variable bonuses, business entrepreneurs, professionals).
- **Minimum Loan Amount**: Strict minimum loan ticket size of Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 70 years at maturity.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Minimum Rs. 50 Lakhs; no upper ceiling (Subject to repayment capacity).
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Up to 75% - 80% of property market value.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Repayment Functionality**: Standard EMIs are debited monthly from the linked account, reducing the sanctioned overdraft drawing power systematically over time.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 1.00% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure Fees**: NIL for floating rate individual borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips and Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, and Profit & Loss accounts (Self-employed).
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement for sale, link title documents for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Two-in-one financial tool offering maximum liquidity alongside optimal interest savings.
- Eliminates the need to apply for short-term personal or business emergency loans.
