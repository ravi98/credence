# Canara Housing cum Solar Loan - Canara Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Canara Housing cum Solar Loan is a specialized green financing product that combines residential property finance with dedicated capital for grid-connected rooftop solar photovoltaic (PV) power systems.

- **Dual-Purpose Financing**: Single consolidated or bundled credit line financing both the residential unit and a rooftop solar system.
- **PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana Integration**: Directly aligned with the Government of India's rooftop solar subsidy scheme, with central financial assistance (subsidy) credited directly to the loan account.
- **Lower Operating Costs**: Offsets household grid electricity bills by 70% to 90%, freeing up household cash flows for debt servicing.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Canara Bank RLLR.
- **Interest Rate Bracket**: 7.15% to 9.50% p.a.
  - The solar component is priced at the same concessional housing loan rate, rather than higher unsecured or consumer equipment rates.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for female applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Eligible Applicants**: Resident Indians, NRIs, salaried employees, professionals, and farmers.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65–70 years at maturity.
- **Property Ownership**: Must own or be in the process of purchasing the residential property where the rooftop solar setup is installed (clear roof rights required).
- **Service Requirement**: Minimum 3 years of work experience or business continuity.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Housing Component**: Sized as per regular housing loan eligibility.
- **Solar Component**: Up to Rs. 10 Lakhs depending on the capacity (kWp) of the solar power system.
- **Margin**:
  - Solar Component: Typically 10% to 15% (subsidies under PM Surya Ghar are adjusted against borrower margin/principal).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years (or aligned with the primary home loan tenure; standalone solar loans up to 15-20 years).
- **Moratorium**: Aligned with house completion or up to 6 months for solar installation and net-metering grid connection.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% (Max Rs. 10,000) on housing portion; zero or subsidized processing fee on the solar component.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: Salary slips (3 months) / Form 16 / ITR (3 years).
- **Solar Quotation & System Details**:
  - Proforma invoice / quotation from an approved MNRE / DISCOM empanelled solar vendor.
  - Technical feasibility report and roof suitability certificate.
  - Registration reference under PM Surya Ghar portal.
- **Property Documents**: Registered sale deed/agreement showing roof rights.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Direct feed-in tariff / net-metering benefits reduce monthly household power bills.
- Eco-friendly home upgrade financed at prime home mortgage interest rates.
