# Canara Housing Loan - Canara Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Canara Housing Loan is a comprehensive residential credit facility provided by Canara Bank. It provides funding for buying ready-built homes/apartments, building houses on owned sites, purchasing plots with subsequent construction, renovating or expanding existing units, and acquiring a second house.

- **No Maximum Loan Cap**: Quantum is tailored dynamically based on the borrower's repaying power.
- **Nil Prepayment Penalties**: Zero charges on floating rate home loans.
- **Complimentary Credit Card**: Borrowers are eligible for an attractive pre-approved Canara Bank credit card.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Canara Bank RLLR (Repo Linked).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 7.15% to 9.90% p.a., determined by credit score:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.15% - 7.45%
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.45% - 7.85%
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 7.85% - 8.35%
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.35% - 9.90%
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for women primary borrowers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Borrower Profile**: Resident Indians, NRIs, and PIOs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 70 years at loan maturity.
- **Employment History**:
  - Salaried: Minimum 3 years of continuous work experience.
  - Self-Employed: Minimum 3 years in the same line of business/profession.
- **Net Take-Home (NTH)**: Must maintain minimum 25% (or Rs. 10,000) take-home salary after factoring all loan EMIs.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Calculated based on 4 to 5 times the annual gross income (or up to 60-70% FOIR).
- **Margin / LTV**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**:
  - Up to 36 months for under-construction houses/apartments.
  - Up to 2 months for ready-built properties.
- **Repayment**: Standing instruction from Canara Bank savings account, NACH mandate, or internet banking auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.50% of the loan amount (Min Rs. 1,500, Max Rs. 10,000) + GST. Regularly waived during promotional campaigns.
- **Foreclosure Charges**: Nil for individual floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**:
  - Salaried: 3 months' salary slips, Form 16 (2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
  - Self-Employed: 3 years' audited Balance Sheet & P&L, 3 years' ITR with computation, 12 months' operative bank statements.
- **Property Documents**: Registered sale agreement, link title deeds for 30 years, approved blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Straightforward takeover/balance transfer options with nominal switching fees.
- Specialized variants available for digital-first borrowers with alternative credit assessment.
