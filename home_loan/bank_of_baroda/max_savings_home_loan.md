# Baroda Max Savings Home Loan - Bank of Baroda

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Baroda Max Savings Home Loan is an innovative smart overdraft / interest-saver mortgage facility designed to optimize interest payments. The loan account is directly linked to a dedicated Current/Savings operative account.

- **Interest Offset Advantage**: Any surplus deposit or idle balance kept in the linked operative account is subtracted from the outstanding loan balance for calculating daily interest.
- **Full Liquidity & Accessibility**: Unlike traditional prepayments which lock money away, funds parked in the linked account can be withdrawn via ATM, net banking, UPI, or cheque at any time without charge.
- **Accelerated Loan Amortization**: Substantially shortens effective loan repayment tenure and saves lakhs in interest costs without requiring fixed prepayment commitments.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BRLLR (Monthly Reset linked to Repo Rate).
- **Floating Rates**: 7.25% to 9.15% p.a. (Competitive floating spread tied to CIBIL tier).
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest discount applies.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Audience**: Resident Indians, NRIs, PIOs, and Self-Employed professionals or entrepreneurs who experience cyclical surplus liquidity (such as bonuses, quarterly dividends, commissions, or receivables).
- **Age Window**:
  - Minimum: 21 years.
  - Maximum: 70 years at maturity.
- **Minimum Loan Threshold**: Rs. 10 Lakhs.
- **Credit Score Requirement**: CIBIL 701 and above.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Sanction Capacity**: Up to Rs. 20 Crores in major metropolitan centers.
- **LTV Distribution**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Max Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Repayment Flow**: Scheduled EMIs are automatically debited each month from the linked account. The principal component reduces the loan limit systematically over time.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.25% to 0.50% + GST (Min Rs. 8,500, Max Rs. 25,000).
- **Foreclosure Charges**: NIL for individual borrowers on floating rates.
- **Cheque Book / Debit Card**: Standard charges as applicable to the linked operative savings/current account.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC & Residence**: PAN card, Aadhaar card, Passport, Voter Card.
- **Income Proof**:
  - Salaried: Salary slips (3 months), Form 16 (2 years), bank account statements (6 months).
  - Self-Employed: 3 years' audited financial statements, tax audit reports, computation sheets, 12 months' business bank statements.
- **Property Documentation**: Registered sale contract, NOC from society/builder, 30-year non-encumbrance certificate, layout approval copy.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- 2-in-1 convenience: Acts both as an overdraft account and an affordable long-term mortgage.
- Ideal for business owners, tech professionals with RSUs/bonuses, and consultants with irregular cash flows.
