# Baroda Home Loan - Bank of Baroda (BoB)

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Baroda Home Loan is Bank of Baroda's primary retail housing finance product. It facilitates the purchase of plots, flats, apartments, villas, construction on self-owned land, extension, and comprehensive renovation of existing properties.

- **Interest Rate Benchmark**: Fully linked to Baroda Repo Linked Lending Rate (BRLLR) with monthly rate resets.
- **Top-Up Facility**: Seamless access to top-up home loans for personal/business needs.
- **Free Add-ons**: Complimentary Bank of Baroda credit card and concession on locker rentals.
- **Nil Foreclosure Charges**: Zero prepayment fees for floating rate loans.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BRLLR (Linked to RBI Repo Rate).
- **Floating Interest Slabs (Spread based on CIBIL)**:
  - CIBIL >= 801: 7.20% - 7.50% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 800: 7.50% - 7.85% p.a.
  - CIBIL 701 - 749: 7.85% - 8.35% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 701: 8.35% - 9.10% p.a.
- **Special Concessions**: 0.05% interest rebate for women primary applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Borrower Category**: Resident Indians, NRIs (Non-Resident Indians), PIOs (Persons of Indian Origin).
- **Age Parameters**:
  - Minimum Age: 21 years.
  - Maximum Exit Age: Up to 70 years (salaried) and 75 years (self-employed).
- **Employment Stability**:
  - Salaried: Minimum 1 year of continuous service.
  - Self-Employed: Minimum 2 years of established business operation or professional practice.
- **Income & Debt Ratios**: Total proposed EMI plus existing obligations should not exceed 50% - 60% of net monthly income (FOIR).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Maximum Sanction Quantum**:
  - Major Metros (Mumbai, Delhi-NCR, Bangalore, etc.): Up to Rs. 20 Crores.
  - Tier-1 / Tier-2 Cities: Up to Rs. 10 Crores.
  - Rural / Semi-Urban: Up to Rs. 1 Crore.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV) Norms**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Repayment Period**: Up to 360 months (30 years).
- **Construction Moratorium**: Up to 36 months or 1 month after completion of construction/possession, whichever is earlier.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions (SI), NACH mandate, or digital auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.25% to 0.50% (Min Rs. 8,500, Max Rs. 25,000) + GST. Full waivers regularly extended during festive and quarterly drives.
- **Legal & Valuation Charges**: Actual fees levied by empanelled legal counsel and technical evaluators.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: 0% on floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Identity & Address Proof**: Aadhaar, PAN card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**:
  - 3 months' salary slips.
  - Form 16 & ITR for previous 2 financial years.
  - Last 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**:
  - 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, and ITR.
  - Tax audit report where applicable.
  - Last 12 months' business bank account statements.
- **Property Papers**: Sale deed, agreement to sell, non-encumbrance certificate (13-30 years), sanctioned building approval blueprint.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Digital loan approvals available via Bank of Baroda's 'bob World' platform.
- Flexibility to club income of spouse, children, or parents to increase maximum loan limit.
