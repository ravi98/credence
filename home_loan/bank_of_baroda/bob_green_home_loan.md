# bob Green Home Loan - Bank of Baroda

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
The bob Green Home Loan is an initiative by Bank of Baroda to incentivize climate-resilient and energy-conscious residential living. It is tailored for borrowers purchasing flats or constructing homes within projects certified by recognized green building rating systems.

- **Green Concession**: Flat 10 bps (0.10%) discount on the prevailing standard home loan interest rate.
- **Eco-Efficiency Coverage**: Supports installations of solar rooftop units, waste management units, and rainwater conservation equipment.
- **Flexible Use**: Applicable for purchase of ready-to-move green properties, under-construction green buildings, or green renovations.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BRLLR.
- **Floating Rates**: 7.10% p.a. to 8.85% p.a. (10 bps lower than the regular Baroda Home Loan rack rate).
- **Women Borrower Benefit**: Additional 0.05% interest rebate can be combined with the green concession.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Eligible Applicants**: Indian Citizens, NRIs, and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs).
- **Age Span**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65–70 years at loan maturity.
- **Property Criteria**: The residential project must be pre-certified or certified by:
  - Indian Green Building Council (IGBC)
  - GRIHA (Green Rating for Integrated Habitat Assessment)
  - LEED (Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design)
  - BEE (Bureau of Energy Efficiency) Star-rated residential projects
- **Credit Profile**: Minimum credit score 701+ recommended.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 20 Crores (depending on metropolitan location and borrower income).
- **Margin / LTV**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: 90% LTV
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: 80% LTV
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: 75% LTV

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Flexible repayment up to 30 years (360 monthly installments).
- **Moratorium**: Construction moratorium up to 36 months for under-construction certified green units.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Discounted processing fees (50% concession on standard rates or waived during promotional periods).
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure Fees**: NIL on floating rate options.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Identity & Address Proof**: Standard KYC (Aadhaar, PAN, Voter ID, Passport).
- **Income Proof**: Salary slips and Form 16 (Salaried) / 3 years' audited ITR & P&L (Self-employed).
- **Green Building Proof**:
  - Valid Green Rating Certificate or Provisional Letter issued by IGBC, GRIHA, or LEED.
  - Developer's environmental specification report.
- **Property Documentation**: Sanctioned plan, sale agreement, chain of title deeds.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Promotes lower carbon footprint living and significant electricity billing savings.
- Fast-track underwriting through Bank of Baroda's specialized green lending cell.
