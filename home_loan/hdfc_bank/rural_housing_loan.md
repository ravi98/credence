# HDFC Rural Housing Finance - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Rural Housing Finance is a dedicated rural and semi-urban mortgage initiative designed to foster residential homeownership across rural India. It specifically caters to agriculturists, planters, horticulturists, dairy farmers, and rural entrepreneurs seeking to purchase or build a home in rural or semi-urban areas.

- **No Agricultural Land Mortgage Required**: Crucially, borrowers are NOT required to mortgage their farming/agricultural land; only the residential property being financed is mortgaged.
- **Exemption from IT Returns**: Farmers and agriculturists who do not file standard Income Tax Returns (ITR) are eligible based on land revenue records, crop sale receipts, and bank passbook analysis.
- **Customized Cash Flow Alignment**: Flexible repayment terms aligned with crop harvesting and agricultural realization seasons.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Benchmark Rate.
- **Floating Rate Range**: 7.50% to 9.25% p.a.
- **Concessions**: 0.05% interest rebate for women co-applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Audience**: Farmers, plantation owners, dairy/poultry farmers, village entrepreneurs, and salaried employees in rural centers.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65–70 years at loan maturity.
- **Agricultural Landholding**: Verifiable operational agricultural land or plantation with minimum 2–3 years of steady crop yield.
- **Credit Track**: Clean credit bureau history (CIBIL 650+ or positive surrogate credit evaluation).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized up to Rs. 75 Lakhs (up to Rs. 1.5 Crores in affluent agricultural belts).
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Up to 80% - 85% of total residential construction / purchase cost.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 20 to 30 years (dependent on applicant age and agricultural stability).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 18 to 24 months for self-construction of residential homes.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card (or Form 60), Voter ID.
- **Income & Farming Proof**:
  - Land revenue records (7/12 extract, Patta / Chitta / Khata).
  - Crop pattern certificates, Mandi sale slips, dairy payment receipts.
  - Bank account passbook / statements for the last 12 months.
- **Property Documentation**: Village panchayat / Block Development Officer (BDO) approved plan, clear title deeds of the homestead land.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Protects ancestral agricultural holdings by strictly limiting security to the residential structure.
- Broad network of specialized rural and semi-urban HDFC Bank branch hubs across India.
