# HDFC Plot Loan - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Plot Loan is a specialized land financing credit line tailored for borrowers wishing to purchase a vacant residential plot of land located within urban, semi-urban, or designated metropolitan development authority borders.

- **Dual-Phase Flexibility**: Allows purchasing a plot today with the provision to easily layer a top-up Home Construction Loan when ready to build.
- **Strict Legal Verification**: Detailed title due-diligence conducted by HDFC legal counsel ensuring the plot is free from municipal disputes or agricultural zoning restrictions.
- **Independent Plot Coverage**: Available for direct allotment from government/statutory development authorities or resale from private societies.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Rate Benchmark.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 7.75% to 9.40% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest discount applies.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians, NRIs, and PIOs.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 70 years at loan maturity.
- **Employment History**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years for self-employed.
- **Mandatory Condition**: The plot must be earmarked exclusively for residential development (commercial and pure agricultural land excluded).
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 5 Crores (subject to location and borrower income).
- **Margin / LTV**:
  - Up to 75% to 80% of the registered plot value / circle rate (Minimum 20% - 25% borrower equity).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 15 years (180 months).
- **Moratorium**: Typically nil or up to 6 months.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instruction / NACH auto-debit.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Penalties**: Nil for floating rate individual borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss statements (Self-employed).
- **Plot Documents**:
  - Approved layout blueprint from local planning authority (e.g. BDA, CMDA, MMRDA, DDA, DTCP).
  - Registered sale agreement / allotment letter.
  - Mother deed / chain of title deeds for 30 years.
  - Encumbrance Certificate (EC) for 30 years.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Seamless top-up construction sanction available without separate mortgage registration.
- Expert guidance on layout regularization and municipal conversion clearances.
