# HDFC Standard Home Loan - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Standard Home Loan is India's premier private sector residential financing solution following the mega-merger of HDFC Ltd. into HDFC Bank. It covers financing for purchase of newly constructed flats/apartments, resale properties, self-construction of residential houses, and balance transfer of existing home loans.

- **Fastest Digital Processing**: Streamlined digital approvals with in-principle sanctions delivered via HDFC NetBanking and WhatsApp banking.
- **Special Women Pricing**: 0.05% interest concession when a female applicant is the primary or sole borrower.
- **Flexible Interest Structures**: Offers adjustable (floating) rate loans linked directly to the RBI Repo Rate, with options for fixed-rate periods.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Linked Benchmark Rate (RLLR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.40% - 7.65% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.65% - 8.00% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.00% - 8.50% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.50% - 9.05% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Borrower Profiles**: Resident Indians, Non-Resident Indians (NRIs), and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs).
- **Age Window**:
  - Minimum Age: 18 years.
  - Maximum Age: 70 years at loan maturity (salaried up to 65 years, self-employed up to 70 years).
- **Work Experience / Stability**:
  - Salaried: Minimum 2 years of continuous employment.
  - Self-Employed: Minimum 3 years of audited profitable business activity.
- **Minimum Monthly Income**: Rs. 25,000 net monthly salary.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+ strictly preferred for prime rates.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized based on gross income, FOIR, and credit appraisal (up to Rs. 10 Crores+).
- **Regulatory LTV Framework**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential properties (interest-only tranche payments).
- **Repayment Mode**: Auto-debit / Standing Instruction through HDFC Bank savings/salary account, NACH.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 0.50% of the loan amount or Rs. 3,000 (whichever is higher), capped at Rs. 15,000 + GST.
- **Prepayment / Foreclosure Fees**: NIL for floating rate individual borrowers.
- **Legal & Technical Fee**: Actuals as per empanelled legal/technical agencies.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (Part A & B for last 2 years), 6 months' salary account bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' operative bank statements.
- **Property Documents**: Registered agreement to sale / allotment letter, approved blueprint, chain of title deeds (30 years), non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep documentation and dedicated relationship managers across 8,000+ branches.
- Complimentary HDFC Bank credit card and discounted property insurance covers.
