# HDFC House Renovation Loan - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC House Renovation Loan (Home Improvement Loan) provides convenient financing for current homeowners seeking to enhance, remodel, repair, or upgrade their existing residential property. It caters to internal remodeling, flooring, tiling, painting, bathroom fittings, roofing, structural repairs, and exterior upgrades.

- **High Funding Ratio**: Funds up to 90% of the certified renovation and civil estimate.
- **Fast-Track Appraisal**: Faster underwriting for borrowers who already have their primary home mortgage with HDFC Bank.
- **Cheaper than Personal Loans**: Offers interest rates comparable to standard home loans, substantially lower than personal loans.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Linked Benchmark Rate.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 7.75% to 9.35% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest discount applies.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians and NRIs who own existing residential premises.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 70 years at maturity.
- **Employment History**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years for self-employed.
- **Property Status**: The property must have clear marketable title and sufficient remaining structural life.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized based on the certified architect/contractor estimate and applicant income.
- **Margin**: Minimum 10% to 15% of the total renovation cost estimate (LTV up to 85% - 90%).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 15 years (180 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 3 to 6 months during renovation execution.
- **Disbursement Mode**: Staged disbursements released against civil contractor invoices and inspection milestones.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: Up to 0.50% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate individual borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR & financial statements (Self-employed).
- **Renovation Cost Estimate**: Certified quotation / itemized bill of quantities (BOQ) prepared by a registered architect or structural engineer.
- **Property Documents**: Registered title deed of the existing property, latest property tax receipt, society NOC.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Can be bundled with primary home loan or taken as an independent facility.
- Significant boost to property valuation and rental yields upon completion.
