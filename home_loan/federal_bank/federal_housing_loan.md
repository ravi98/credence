# Federal Housing Loan - Federal Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Federal Housing Loan is the premier retail mortgage product from Federal Bank (a major private sector bank with pan-India presence and market leadership in NRI banking). It facilitates financing for purchasing ready-built flats/villas, residential construction, plot purchase with construction, home renovation, and balance transfers.

- **High Quantum Limits**: Offers funding up to Rs. 15 Crores for prime residential acquisitions.
- **Mahilamitra Plus Concessions**: Exclusive interest rate discounts and fee reductions for women borrowers.
- **Deep NRI Integration**: Industry-leading cross-border digital remittance and NRI mortgage fulfillment channels.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Federal Bank Repo Linked Lending Rate (RLLR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.30% - 7.60% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.60% - 8.05% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.05% - 8.65% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.65% - 9.25% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for female applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians, NRIs, and Persons of Indian Origin (PIOs).
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years (salaried) and 70 years (self-employed).
- **Work Stability**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years for self-employed.
- **Minimum Net Income**: Rs. 20,000 per month (salaried) / Rs. 2.5 Lakhs annual profit (self-employed).
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+ (750+ secures best spread).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Up to Rs. 15 Crores based on repayment ability.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction residential properties.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Federal Bank account, FedMobile app, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of loan amount (Min Rs. 3,000, Max Rs. 15,000) + GST. Regularly waived during promotional periods.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rate individual borrowers.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' business bank statements.
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement to sell, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast-track digital approvals through FedMobile app.
- Step-up and step-down structured repayment options to align with life stage milestones.
