# PNB Pride Housing Loan - Punjab National Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Pride Housing Loan is a specialized, concessional housing finance package curated for employees of Central Government, State Governments, Defense Personnel, Paramilitary Forces, and Public Sector Undertakings (PSUs).

- **Full Processing Fee Exemption**: Nil processing fee and documentation charges.
- **Preferential Rate Slabs**: Rate concessions lower than general market housing loans.
- **Extended Repayment Window**: Repayment permissible up to age 75 years, allowing seamless transition from regular salary to pension.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB RLLR.
- **Floating Rates**: 7.15% to 8.65% p.a.
  - Government employees with salary accounts at PNB get the lowest rate bucket starting at 7.15% p.a.
- **Additional Rebate**: 0.05% discount for women co-applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**:
  - Permanent employees of Central / State Government departments.
  - Armed Forces (Army, Air Force, Navy).
  - Paramilitary forces (CRPF, BSF, ITBP, CISF, SSB).
  - PSUs, autonomous government universities, and statutory corporations.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 75 years at loan completion.
- **Service Security**: Minimum 1 year of confirmed government service.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 650+ (scores above 750 achieve minimum spread).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sized based on Net Monthly Income (NMI) and pension projections.
- **LTV Limits**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Above Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years or age 75 years.
- **Moratorium**: Up to 36 months for under-construction houses.
- **Repayment**: Direct salary check-off, ECS / NACH mandate, or PNB salary account standing instruction.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: NIL (Fully waived).
- **Documentation & Legal Fee**: NIL or subsidized at actual panel rates.
- **Prepayment Penalty**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Identity & Address**: Official Government ID Card, PAN, Aadhaar Card.
- **Income & Employment**:
  - 3 months' salary slips with official DDO signature/stamp.
  - Form 16 / ITR for last 2 years.
  - 6 months' salary bank statements.
  - Check-off authority letter from employer (if applicable).
- **Property Records**: Approved plan, builder agreement / sale deed, 30-year search report.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Substantial interest savings over a 20-30 year horizon.
- Quick sanctions via dedicated Government Employee Loan Desks at PNB circles.
