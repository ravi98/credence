# PNB Housing Roshni Affordable Home Loan - PNB Housing

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Housing Roshni is a flagship affordable housing loan scheme specifically conceptualized to cater to first-time homebuyers from low-income, middle-income, and informal earning backgrounds. It supports the purchase of apartments, self-construction of houses, plot plus construction, and home expansion.

- **Informal Income Assessment**: Evaluates applicants who may not have standard ITR or Form 16, utilizing customized surrogate cash-flow underwriting.
- **Affordable Loan Sizing**: Tailored loan ticket sizes between Rs. 5 Lakhs and Rs. 35 Lakhs.
- **Support for Women Co-Owners**: Incentivizes inclusion of female family members as co-owners and co-borrowers.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB Housing Prime Lending Rate (LHPLR).
- **Rate Range**: 8.75% to 11.50% p.a. (Floating interest rate based on risk profile and documentation tier).

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Beneficiaries**:
  - Salaried employees in small businesses, micro-enterprises, and unorganized sectors.
  - Self-employed shopkeepers, artisans, small traders, drivers, contractors, and service providers.
- **Applicant Age**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years at loan maturity.
- **Household Income**: Suitable for annual household incomes between Rs. 1.8 Lakhs and Rs. 6 Lakhs (covering EWS & LIG segments).
- **Credit Track Record**: CIBIL 600+ considered, or first-time borrowers with zero credit history (NTC - New To Credit).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Minimum Loan Amount**: Rs. 5 Lakhs.
- **Maximum Loan Amount**: Rs. 35 Lakhs (higher amounts eligible under standard housing loans).
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**: Up to 90% of property market valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Repayment Horizon**: Up to 30 years (360 months), ensuring affordable monthly installments.
- **Repayment Channel**: NACH mandate, ECS, or post-dated cheques (PDCs).

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 1.00% to 1.50% of the loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Penalties**: Nil for individual floating rate borrowers.
- **Legal & Technical Verification**: At actuals.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Identity & Residence Proof**: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Voter ID, Ration Card.
- **Income Proof (Flexible)**:
  - Salaried: Salary slips or salary certificate from employer, 6 months' bank statement (or cash-flow diary).
  - Self-Employed: Business ledger, bank statement (6 to 12 months), proof of business continuity (Shop act, trade license, or local references).
- **Property Documents**: Allotment paper, sale agreement, registered patta/khata/title chain.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep relationship assistance for non-tech-savvy borrowers.
- Streamlined documentation process designed for first-time formal borrowers.
