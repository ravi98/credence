# PNB Gen-Next Housing Finance Scheme - Punjab National Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Gen-Next Housing Finance Scheme is structured specifically for generation-next salaried professionals, including IT/ITeS engineers, financial analysts, corporate executives, and young officers in PSUs/reputed MNCs. It provides up to 125% higher loan eligibility than traditional loan matrices by factoring in career progression and graduated earnings.

- **Higher Loan Sanctions**: Computes higher borrowing eligibility based on projected income growth.
- **Moratorium with Interest-Only Payment**: Option to pay only simple interest during initial period, graduating to regular EMIs.
- **Portability**: Easy balance transfer facility from existing high-interest lenders.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB RLLR.
- **Floating Rate Bracket**: 7.35% to 9.15% p.a.
- **Concessions**: 0.05% interest rebate for women applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Segment**: Confirmed salaried employees in IT/Software, MNCs, Listed Entities, PSUs, and Financial Institutions.
- **Age Window**:
  - Minimum Age: 21 years.
  - Maximum Age: 40 years at loan entry.
- **Minimum Experience**: Minimum 2 years in continuous employment (at least 1 year with current employer).
- **Minimum Monthly Income**: Rs. 35,000 net monthly salary.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Minimum Loan Amount**: Rs. 20 Lakhs.
- **Maximum Quantum**: No ceiling; sanctioned up to 1.25x of standard formula limits.
- **Margin / LTV Norms**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years (or up to age of retirement, maximum 60 years).
- **Phased EMIs**: Lower EMI in initial 3 years, followed by regular stepped-up EMIs in later years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.35% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL for floating rates.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport.
- **Salary / Income**:
  - Latest 3 months' pay slips.
  - Form 16 / ITR for previous 2 years.
  - 6 months' salary bank statements.
  - Corporate badge / Employment appointment letter.
- **Property Documentation**: Registered sale agreement, builder allotment certificate, sanction blueprint.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Enables young homebuyers to purchase spacious homes earlier in life without compromising cash flow.
- Prepayment options allow lumpsum reduction whenever bonuses or stock grants vest.
