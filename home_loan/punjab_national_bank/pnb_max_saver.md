# PNB Max Saver Housing Loan - Punjab National Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Max Saver is an overdraft-linked housing loan scheme from Punjab National Bank. It provides the customer with an operative account linked to the home loan, enabling them to park surplus cash and drastically minimize interest payments while retaining complete withdrawal flexibility.

- **Smart Overdraft Facility**: Daily interest is calculated solely on the net balance (Outstanding Loan Principal minus credit balance in the linked operative account).
- **Unrestricted Liquidity**: Surplus funds can be deposited or drawn out through debit card, net banking, UPI, or branch cheques up to the drawing power.
- **Tenure Reduction**: Continual parking of excess funds accelerates repayment and saves substantial interest over the loan horizon.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB Repo Linked Lending Rate (RLLR).
- **Rate Range**: 7.30% to 9.15% p.a., determined by credit score:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.30% - 7.55%
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.55% - 7.90%
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 7.90% - 8.40%
  - CIBIL 650 - 699: 8.40% - 9.15%
- **Women Borrower Concession**: 5 bps (0.05%) reduction in floating interest rate.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Eligible Applicants**: Resident Indians, NRIs, and Persons of Indian Origin.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum exit age 70 years.
- **Employment Categories**: Salaried employees, self-employed professionals, and business owners with proven regular income.
- **Minimum Threshold**: Loan amount must be at least Rs. 10 Lakhs.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 680 and above.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Minimum Rs. 10 Lakhs, with no maximum ceiling subject to repayment capacity and property value.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90%
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80%
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75%

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Drawing Power Reduction**: The overdraft limit / drawing power reduces each month by the equivalent principal component of the EMI.
- **Moratorium**: Permissible up to 36 months for under-construction properties.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.35% of loan amount (Min Rs. 2,500, Max Rs. 15,000) + GST. Regularly waived during festive offers.
- **Foreclosure Charges**: Nil for individual floating rate borrowers.
- **Documentation / Inspection Fee**: Actuals as per bank scale.

## 7. Required Documents
- **Identity & Address**: PAN, Aadhaar, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**:
  - Salaried: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16, 6 months' bank statements.
  - Self-Employed: 3 years' audited ITR, balance sheets, computation of income, 12 months' operative bank account statements.
- **Property Documents**: Allotment letter, agreement for sale, encumbrance certificate, approved layout blueprint.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Zero lock-in period for deposited surplus cash.
- Ideal for business owners, corporate executives, and commission earners.
