# Saraswat Vastu Siddhi Home Loan - Saraswat Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Saraswat Vastu Siddhi Home Loan is the flagship housing credit solution from Saraswat Bank, India's largest Scheduled Urban Co-operative Bank. It finances the construction of houses, purchase of ready-to-move-in or resale flats/houses, purchase of under-construction residential properties, and balance transfer from other commercial banks or housing finance institutions.

- **Largest Urban Co-Operative Bank**: Combines personalized community banking relationships with state-of-the-art modern digital banking technology.
- **Vastu Siddhi for NRIs**: Dedicated variant available for Non-Resident Indians seeking residential property in India with inward remittance repayments.
- **Cross-Sell Perks**: Eligible borrowers qualify for pre-approved car loans and credit cards.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Saraswat Bank Prime Lending Rate (S-PLR).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.35% - 7.60% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.60% - 8.05% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 8.05% - 8.55% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.55% - 9.10% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest concession available for women borrowers under priority sector lending.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians, NRIs, and PIOs.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 18 years; Maximum 60–65 years at loan completion.
- **Employment Requirement**:
  - Salaried: Minimum 2 years of confirmed continuous service.
  - Self-Employed: Minimum 3 years of established business practice.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Maximum Quantum**: Up to Rs. 300 Lakhs (Rs. 3 Crores) per individual borrower.
- **Margin**: Minimum 10% to 20% of (Agreement Cost + Stamp Duty + Registration Charges).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 20 years (240 months).
- **Moratorium**: Up to 24 months for under-construction residential units.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Saraswat Bank account, NACH mandate.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of loan amount + GST.
- **Membership Share**: Nominal co-operative nominal/regular shareholding as per Co-operative Societies Act norms.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL on floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof (Salaried)**: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (last 2 years), 6 months' salary bank statements.
- **Income Proof (Self-Employed)**: 3 years' audited P&L, Balance Sheet, computation of income, ITR, 12 months' business bank statements.
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement to sell, link title deeds for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Swift branch-level decision making and flexible customer service.
- Easy balance transfer facility with nominal transfer charges.
