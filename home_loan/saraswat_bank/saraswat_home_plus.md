# Saraswat Home Plus - Saraswat Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Saraswat Home Plus is a versatile top-up mortgage facility designed by Saraswat Bank. It enables existing home loan borrowers who have established a satisfactory repayment record, as well as outright property owners, to leverage their residential property equity for additional personal or business requirements.

- **Multipurpose End-Use**: Can be utilized for children's higher education, marriage expenses, home renovation, medical contingencies, or business working capital.
- **Lower Cost Credit**: Substantially more affordable than unsecured personal loans or business overdrafts.
- **Fast-Track Processing**: Minimized documentation for existing borrowers since mortgage security and legal titles are already cleared with the bank.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Saraswat Bank S-PLR.
- **Floating Interest Slabs**: 8.15% to 9.85% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for eligible categories.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Existing Saraswat Bank home loan borrowers with at least 12 months of clean repayment history, or individuals owning unencumbered residential property.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 65 years at maturity.
- **Employment History**: Minimum 2 years for salaried; 3 years for self-employed.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Maximum Sanction**: Up to Rs. 1 Crore.
- **Overall LTV Cap**: Total combined exposure (Existing Home Loan + Saraswat Home Plus) must not exceed 75% of the verified fair market property valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 15 years (180 months) or remaining tenure of primary home loan.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via Saraswat Bank savings account, NACH.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% of loan amount + GST.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL on floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN, Aadhaar.
- **Income Proof**: Latest 3 months' salary slips / 2 years' Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR & financial statements (Self-employed).
- **Repayment Track**: Statement showing satisfactory track record of primary home loan.
- **Property Records**: Existing title deed verification / updated search report.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick release of funds upon validation of repayment track.
- Flexible partial prepayments with zero penalties.
