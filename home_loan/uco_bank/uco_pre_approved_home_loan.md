# UCO Pre-Approved Home Loan - UCO Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
UCO Pre-Approved Home Loan is an advance sanction facility extended by UCO Bank that assesses a borrower's creditworthiness and income eligibility, issuing a formal "in-principle" loan sanction letter before the borrower has finalized a specific residential property.

- **4-Month Sanction Validity**: The in-principle sanction remains valid for 4 months, providing ample time to scout, negotiate, and select a property.
- **Enhanced Bargaining Power**: Having a pre-approved bank sanction letter enables buyers to negotiate better pricing and discounts directly with builders and property sellers.
- **Expedited Final Disbursement**: Once the property is selected, the final disbursement process is significantly faster since the borrower's personal KYC, credit score, and financial documents are already verified.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: UCO-RLLR.
- **Interest Slabs**: 7.35% to 9.10% p.a. (Same competitive rates as UCO Home Loan, locked in upon application).
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Profiles**: Salaried professionals, corporate executives, and self-employed businessmen.
- **Age Window**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 70 years at maturity.
- **Employment Stability**: Minimum 2 years continuous employment / business operations.
- **Credit Score**: CIBIL 700+ preferred for seamless pre-approval.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Sanctioned in-principle based on income multiplier and Debt-to-Income / FOIR.
- **LTV**: Final disbursement capped at 75% - 90% of the selected property's verified valuation.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium**: Standard construction moratorium available once property is finalized.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charge**: 0.50% + GST. A nominal initial fee may be collected at the in-principle stage, adjusted against total processing charges upon final disbursement.
- **Prepayment Fee**: NIL.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport.
- **Income Proof (Pre-Approval Stage)**:
  - Salaried: 3 months' pay slips, Form 16 (2 years), 6 months' bank statements.
  - Self-Employed: 3 years' ITR, computation sheets, audited Balance Sheets and P&L.
- **Property Documents (Final Stage)**: Title deeds, builder allotment/sale contract, approved building layout plan, and non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Eliminates uncertainty during home hunting by establishing clear budget parameters.
- Straightforward conversion into full disbursement upon submission of property title papers.
