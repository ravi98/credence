# UCO Home Loan - UCO Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
UCO Home Loan is the flagship retail mortgage product from UCO Bank (a leading public sector financial institution). It provides funds for purchasing ready-built flats/houses, constructing on owned land, purchasing residential plots with subsequent construction, extension/repairs, and taking over existing home loans from other financial institutions.

- **Financing for Older Properties**: Permits renovation/repairs for residential structures up to 50 years old with sound structural integrity.
- **Furnishing Cost Inclusion**: Provides facility to bundle up to 10% to 15% of loan quantum towards interior furnishing.
- **Daily Reducing Balance**: Calculates interest on daily outstanding principal, maximizing interest savings.

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: UCO-RLLR (Repo Linked).
- **Floating Interest Slabs**:
  - CIBIL >= 800: 7.35% - 7.55% p.a.
  - CIBIL 750 - 799: 7.55% - 7.90% p.a.
  - CIBIL 700 - 749: 7.90% - 8.45% p.a.
  - CIBIL < 700: 8.45% - 9.10% p.a.
- **Women Concession**: 0.05% interest rebate for women primary applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Target Category**: Resident Indians, NRIs, and PIOs.
- **Age Bounds**: Minimum 21 years; Maximum 75 years inclusive of the repayment period.
- **Employment Stability**: Minimum 2 years of service for salaried; 3 years audited track record for self-employed.
- **Credit Score**: Minimum CIBIL 650+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Margin (LTV)
- **Quantum**: Based on Net Monthly Income (NMI) and Debt-to-Income / FOIR limits.
- **Loan-to-Value (LTV)**:
  - Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs: Up to 90% (10% Margin)
  - Rs. 30 Lakhs to Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 80% (20% Margin)
  - Above Rs. 75 Lakhs: Up to 75% (25% Margin)

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Moratorium
- **Maximum Tenure**: Up to 30 years (360 months).
- **Moratorium Period**: Up to 36 months for under-construction houses.
- **Repayment Modes**: Standing Instructions via UCO Bank savings account, NACH / ECS mandates.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Fee**: 0.50% of loan amount (Min Rs. 1,500, Max Rs. 15,000) + GST. Regularly waived during festival campaigns.
- **Prepayment Penalties**: Nil on floating rate loans.

## 7. Required Documents
- **KYC**: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, Voter ID.
- **Income Proof**: 3 months' pay slips and Form 16 (Salaried); 3 years' audited ITR, Balance Sheets, Profit & Loss accounts (Self-employed).
- **Property Records**: Registered agreement of sale, link title documents for 30 years, sanctioned blueprint, non-encumbrance certificate.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Straight-Through Processing (STP) home loans for rapid digital in-principle sanction.
- Straightforward balance transfer options from existing non-banking lenders.
