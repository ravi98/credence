# Cent Vidyarthi Scheme - Central Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Cent Vidyarthi is Central Bank of India's flagship educational loan program providing structured credit assistance to meritorious students pursuing graduate, post-graduate, and specialized professional education.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs
- **0.50%**: 0.50% interest concession for female applicants
- **Up**: Up to 15 years repayment following moratorium period

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Central Bank RBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.50% to 10.25% p.a.
- **Concessions**: 0.50% for girl students.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian national with secured admission through merit or entrance test.
- Co-borrower mandatory.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 20 Lakhs (higher for premier institutes); Abroad: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: Nil up to Rs. 4 Lakhs; 5% India; 15% Abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 1 year; Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil for India.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, mark sheets, fee structure, KYC, co-borrower income documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- CSIS interest subsidy eligible.
- Section 80E tax benefits.
