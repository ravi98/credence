# PSB Education Loan Scheme - Punjab & Sind Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PSB Education Loan provides reliable credit support for students pursuing professional degrees, engineering, medicine, management, and doctorate programs in recognized domestic and foreign universities.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs under CGFSEL
- **0.50%**: 0.50% interest concession for girl students
- **Repayment**: Repayment up to 15 years post moratorium

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PSB RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.60% to 10.35% p.a.
- **Concessions**: 0.50% for girl students.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian citizen securing admission through merit/entrance.
- Co-borrower required.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: Nil up to Rs. 4 Lakhs; 5% India; 15% Abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 1 year; Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil for inland education.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, mark sheets, fee structure, KYC, parent income proof.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- CSIS interest subsidy eligible.
- Section 80E tax exemption.
