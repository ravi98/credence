# IB Education Loan Scheme - Indian Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IB Education Loan provides comprehensive financing for higher education courses across India and international universities. It covers course fees, hostel charges, books, computers, and travel expenses.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs under CGFSEL
- **0.50%**: 0.50% interest concession for female students
- **Repayment**: Repayment up to 15 years post-moratorium

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Indian Bank IND EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.55% to 10.35% p.a.
- **Girl Child Rebate**: 0.50%.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian citizen with secured admission through entrance/merit.
- Co-borrower: Parents/guardian.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: Nil up to Rs. 4 Lakhs; 5% India; 15% Abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 12 months; Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil for India.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Academic mark sheets, admission letter, fee structure, KYC, parent income proof.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- CSIS interest subsidy eligible.
- Section 80E tax deduction.
