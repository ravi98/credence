# IB Skill Loan Scheme - Indian Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IB Skill Loan Scheme supports students and youth acquiring technical skills and vocational certifications from ITIs, Polytechnics, and training centers aligned with the National Skill Development Corporation (NSDC).

- **Covers**: Covers vocational training, equipment, examination, and certificate charges
- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free facility covered by National Credit Guarantee Trustee Company (NCGTC)
- **Flexible**: Flexible 3 to 7-year repayment window

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Indian Bank IND EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.40% to 9.75% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian national enrolled in NSDC/ITI/Polytechnic approved skill certification course.
- Co-borrower: Parents/guardian.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Rs. 5,000 to Rs. 1.50 Lakhs.
- Margin: Nil.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 6 months.
- Repayment: Up to 3 to 7 years depending on loan amount.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission proof to vocational training institute, course fee schedule, KYC documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Promotes Skill India initiative.
- Zero collateral and zero processing charges.
