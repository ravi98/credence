# HDFC Credila Customized Student Loan - HDFC Credila

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Credila is India's pioneer specialized education loan NBFC. It has funded over 1.5 lakh students across 50+ countries. It offers customized loan solutions, doorstep service, and pre-admission sanctions.

- **Specialized**: Specialized student loan company with customized terms for 50+ countries
- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free funding up to Rs. 60 Lakhs for top global universities
- **Sanction**: Sanction before confirmed admission and pre-visa disbursal available

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Credila Benchmark Lending Rate (CBLR).
- **Effective Rate**: 9.75% to 14.25% p.a. depending on student academic merit, GRE/GMAT score, country, university tier, and co-borrower credit profile.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Admission or prospective admission to recognized degree programs abroad or in India.
- Co-borrower (parents/guardian/relatives) with stable income.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore (secured) / up to Rs. 60 Lakhs (unsecured).
- Margin: Flexible; up to 100% financing of living and tuition costs.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 6 to 12 months (options for simple interest or partial interest during moratorium).
- Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.25% - 2.00% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil charges.

## 7. Required Documents
- Academic mark sheets, entrance test scores, university admit letter/I-20, co-borrower KYC, salary slips, 2 years ITR, bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Doorstep service and paperless digital collection.
- Direct payment of living expenses via prepaid student cards.
- Tax savings under Section 80E.
