# Star Education Loan - Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
BOI Star Education Loan is designed to empower students pursuing graduation, post-graduation, and specialized professional certifications in recognized domestic and overseas institutions.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free funding up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs under CGFSEL
- **0.50%**: 0.50% interest rate rebate for girl students
- **Generous**: Generous moratorium period covering complete course duration plus 1 year

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BOI Repo Based Lending Rate (RBLR).
- **Effective Rate**: 8.40% to 10.25% p.a.
- **Concessions**: 0.50% for women borrowers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian citizen with secured admission through merit or entrance exam.
- Co-borrower: Parents/spouse.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Studies in India: Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: Nil up to Rs. 4 Lakhs; 5% for India; 15% for abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course period + 12 months.
- Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Nil processing fees for inland studies; Rs. 5,000 for abroad (refunded on disbursement).
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission proof, academic mark sheets, co-borrower KYC, income documents, collateral property papers.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) eligible.
- Full 80E tax deduction benefits.
