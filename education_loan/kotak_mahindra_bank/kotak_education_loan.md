# Kotak Education Loan - Kotak Mahindra Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Kotak Education Loan is designed to finance undergraduate and postgraduate education in India and overseas. It offers flexible structuring, quick digital sanctions, and unsecured limits for top-tier universities.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for select universities
- **Pre-visa**: Pre-visa sanction letter for study abroad candidates
- **Flexible**: Flexible repayment tenure up to 15 years

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Kotak Repo Linked Benchmark.
- **Effective Rate**: 9.75% to 13.50% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Confirmed admission in recognized institution.
- Earning co-applicant (parents/spouse).
- Minimum CIBIL score of 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs (unsecured) / up to Rs. 1.00 Crore (with collateral).
- Margin: 5% to 15%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 6 to 12 months; Repayment up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: Zero penalty.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, mark sheets, standardized test scores, KYC, co-applicant income documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Direct overseas university wire transfer.
- Section 80E tax deduction benefits.
