# SBI Global Ed-Vantage Scheme - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Global Ed-Vantage is SBI's dedicated overseas education loan scheme providing high-value financial assistance for students pursuing technical, professional, or postgraduate degree courses abroad. It mandates tangible collateral security and provides loan sanction letters for international visa issuance.

- **High**: High quantum up to Rs. 1.50 Crore to cover tuition, living expenses, and travel
- **In-principle**: In-principle sanction letter issued prior to I-20 / Visa application
- **Tax**: Tax deduction on interest under Section 80E with no upper ceiling

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: SBI EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.50% to 10.75% p.a.
- **Concession**: 0.50% concession for girl students.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Admission**: Admission to full-time degrees (Graduation/Post-Graduation/Doctorate) in USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany, Singapore, etc.
- **Co-borrower**: Parents/Spouse/Father-in-law/Mother-in-law.
- **Collateral**: 100% tangible collateral security (house, commercial property, fixed deposits).

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **Quantum**: Rs. 7.50 Lakhs up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- **Margin**: 15% (Scholarships/assistantships counted towards margin).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Moratorium**: Course duration + 6 months.
- **Repayment**: Maximum repayment tenure of up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charges**: Rs. 10,000 + applicable GST.
- **Prepayment**: Zero prepayment penalty.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter with I-20 or equivalent foreign university document.
- GRE / GMAT / IELTS / TOEFL scorecard.
- Property documents for collateral evaluation and legal title search.
- Income proof and ITR of co-applicant.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Pre-visa sanction letter enables seamless visa approvals.
- Disbursement in foreign currency directly to foreign university and student living expense account.
- Comprehensive student loan insurance cover.
