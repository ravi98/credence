# SBI Student Loan Scheme - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Student Loan Scheme is India's flagship educational financing facility aligned with the Indian Banks' Association (IBA) model. It covers course fees, examination/library/laboratory fees, hostel charges, books, equipment, travel expenses, and purchase of computers/laptops.

- **Concession**: Concession of 0.50% in interest rate for girl students
- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free borrowing up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs under CGFSEL cover
- **Repayment**: Repayment starts only after completion of course + 12-month moratorium period

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: SBI External Benchmark Lending Rate (EBLR).
- **Effective Rate**: 8.15% to 10.65% p.a. depending on institute tier, collateral pledged, and gender.
- **Concessions**: 0.50% rebate for female applicants; 0.50% concession under SBI Rinn Raksha or loan insurance.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Nationality**: Indian citizen.
- **Academic Record**: Secured admission through Entrance Test / Merit-based selection in approved university/college.
- **Co-borrower**: Mandatory parent/guardian/spouse as co-applicant with independent KYC.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **India Studies**: Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- **Abroad Studies**: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- **Margin**: Up to Rs. 4 Lakhs: Nil. Above Rs. 4 Lakhs: 5% for India, 15% for Studies Abroad. Scholarship/assistantship included in margin.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Moratorium**: Course period + 12 months (or 6 months after securing employment, whichever is earlier).
- **Repayment**: Up to 15 years after moratorium.
- **Modes**: Standing Instructions, NACH auto-debit, SBI YONO app.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charges**: Nil for studies in India. Abroad: Rs. 10,000 + GST (adjusted against margin/disbursal once availed).
- **Prepayment Penalty**: Zero penalty for early repayment or accelerated EMIs.

## 7. Required Documents
- Mark sheets of 10th, 12th, Degree examinations.
- Offer letter / Admission letter with fee breakdown.
- Co-borrower KYC: PAN, Aadhaar, Proof of Residence.
- Co-borrower Income: Form 16 / ITR last 2 years, 6 months bank statements.
- Property documents if collateral is pledged (for loans above Rs. 7.5L).

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Tax deduction under Section 80E of Income Tax Act for up to 8 years on entire interest paid.
- Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) available for economically weaker sections (family income up to Rs. 4.5 LPA).
- Quick sanction via Vidyalakshmi portal integration.
