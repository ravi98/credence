# SBI Scholar Loan Scheme - State Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
SBI Scholar Loan is an exclusive educational credit facility designed for students admitted to India's top premier higher educational institutions. Institutions are categorized into List AA, List A, List B, and List C, offering high collateral-free limits, rock-bottom interest rates, and zero processing fees.

- **100%**: 100% collateral-free financing up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for List AA (Top IIMs/IITs)
- **Zero**: Zero margin money required for List AA and List A premier colleges
- **Completely**: Completely waived processing fee and expedited on-campus sanction stalls

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: SBI EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 7.95% to 8.45% for List AA/A; 8.45% to 9.15% for List B/C.
- **Concessions**: 0.50% concession for girl students across all tiers.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- **Admission**: Confirmed admission to approved institutions listed in List AA, A, B, or C.
- **Courses**: Full-time regular Degree/Diploma courses.
- **Co-borrower**: Parents/Guardians as primary co-borrower.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- **List AA Institutes**: Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs without tangible collateral.
- **List A Institutes**: Up to Rs. 40 Lakhs without tangible collateral.
- **List B Institutes**: Up to Rs. 20 Lakhs without collateral (up to Rs. 30 Lakhs with collateral).
- **List C Institutes**: Up to Rs. 7.5 Lakhs without collateral (up to Rs. 30 Lakhs with collateral).
- **Margin**: Nil for List AA and List A institutes.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- **Moratorium**: Course period + 12 months.
- **Repayment**: Up to 15 years post moratorium period.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- **Processing Charges**: 100% Nil.
- **Prepayment Charges**: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Letter of admission / offer from premier institution.
- Entrance examination scorecard (CAT, JEE Advanced, NEET, CLAT, etc.).
- KYC of student and co-borrower.
- Academic records and fee schedule.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Covers tuition fees, accommodation, laptop/computer, exchange programs, and study material.
- Campus kiosk and digital paperless sanction.
- Full 80E tax benefit eligibility.
