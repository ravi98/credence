# Union Education Premier Scheme - Union Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Union Education Premier Scheme is tailored for students pursuing courses in top-ranked premier institutes across India. It offers competitive rates, zero processing charges, and high collateral-free limits.

- **Up**: Up to Rs. 40 to 50 Lakhs without tangible security for List A institutes
- **Zero**: Zero margin money required
- **Fast-track**: Fast-track processing and priority sanction

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Union Bank EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.10% to 8.95% p.a.
- **Concessions**: 0.50% concession for girl students.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Admission to approved premier institute list.
- Co-borrower required.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs collateral-free.
- Margin: Nil.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 1 year; Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, entrance exam scorecard, KYC, fee structure.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- 100% financing for tuition, hostel, computer, and living costs.
- Full 80E tax deduction.
