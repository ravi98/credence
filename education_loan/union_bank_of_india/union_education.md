# Union Bank Union Education Scheme - Union Bank of India

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Union Education is Union Bank of India's flagship educational financing facility. It assists meritorious students in pursuing higher technical, professional, and vocational education in India and overseas.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs under CGFSEL
- **0.50%**: 0.50% interest concession for female students
- **Flexible**: Flexible 15-year repayment window

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Union Bank EBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.50% to 10.35% p.a.
- **Concessions**: 0.50% for girl students.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian citizen with confirmed admission through merit/entrance.
- Co-borrower mandatory.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: Nil up to Rs. 4 Lakhs; 5% India; 15% Abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course period + 12 months; Repayment up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Nil for India; Prepayment penalty: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, academic records, KYC, co-borrower income documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- CSIS interest subsidy eligible.
- Section 80E tax benefits.
