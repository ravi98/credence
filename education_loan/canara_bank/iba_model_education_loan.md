# Canara Bank IBA Model Education Loan - Canara Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Canara Bank's standard education loan conforming to the IBA model scheme covers tuition, examination, books, equipment, hostel, and travel expenses for higher technical and professional education.

- **No**: No collateral required up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs (CGFSEL covered)
- **0.50%**: 0.50% concession for girl students
- **15-year**: 15-year post-moratorium repayment duration

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Canara Bank RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.65% to 10.40% p.a.
- **Concession**: 0.50% for women students.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian citizen securing admission in approved university/college.
- Co-borrower: Parents/guardian.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: Nil up to Rs. 4 Lakhs; 5% India; 15% Abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 1 year.
- Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil for India; nominal for abroad.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Academic marksheets, admission letter, KYC, parent income proof, property title deeds (above Rs. 7.5L).

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) eligible.
- 80E tax deduction benefits.
