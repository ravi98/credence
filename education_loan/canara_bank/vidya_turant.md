# Canara Bank Vidya Turant Scheme - Canara Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Canara Bank Vidya Turant is an expedited, collateral-free education loan scheme exclusively designed for students admitted to India's top premier institutions. The bank maintains an approved list of institutions with pre-determined high sanction limits.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for top tier institutes
- **Zero**: Zero margin money requirement
- **Instant**: Instant paperless sanction with zero processing fees

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Canara Bank RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.15% to 8.90% p.a.
- **Girl Student Concession**: 0.50% interest concession.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Admission confirmed in premier institutions on the Canara Bank Vidya Turant approved list.
- Co-borrower: Parents/guardian.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs without tangible security.
- Margin: Nil.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 1 year.
- Repayment: Up to 15 years post-moratorium.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil.
- Prepayment: Zero penalty.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, scorecards (JEE/CAT), KYC, fee schedule.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Campus counters for instant in-principle sanction.
- Section 80E tax benefits.
