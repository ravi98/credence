# HDFC Domestic Education Loan - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Domestic Education Loan is a swift private-sector credit facility financing higher education in India. It offers high collateral-free limits for premier institutions and custom repayment options.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 45-50 Lakhs for select premier institutes
- **Fast-track**: Fast-track digital approval within 48 to 72 hours
- **Flexible**: Flexible repayment tenures up to 15 years

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Benchmark Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 9.50% to 13.00% p.a. depending on institute ranking and co-borrower credit profile.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian citizen admitted to recognized university/college in India.
- Co-borrower (parents/spouse) with stable income.
- Minimum CIBIL score of 700+ for co-borrower.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- Margin: Nil for premier institutes; 5% to 10% for other colleges.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 6 to 12 months; Repayment up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.50% of loan amount + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil after first EMI.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, entrance exam scorecard, KYC, co-borrower salary slips/ITR and bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Comprehensive education insurance cover.
- Section 80E tax deduction benefits.
