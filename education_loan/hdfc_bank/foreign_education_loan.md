# HDFC Foreign Education Loan - HDFC Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
HDFC Foreign Education Loan offers extensive financing for Indian students pursuing international education. It includes pre-visa sanction letters, disbursement directly in foreign currency, and living expense coverage.

- **Pre-visa**: Pre-visa sanction letter for hassle-free visa approvals
- **Unsecured**: Unsecured funding up to Rs. 45 Lakhs for top global universities
- **Covers**: Covers 100% of tuition, living expenses, health insurance, and airfare

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: HDFC Policy Repo Benchmark Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 9.75% to 13.50% p.a.
- Rate determined by university ranking, GRE/GMAT scores, and co-applicant income.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Admission confirmed in accredited foreign university.
- Financial co-applicant required (parents/spouse/siblings).
- Co-applicant credit score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore (secured) / up to Rs. 45 Lakhs (unsecured).
- Margin: 10% to 15%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 6 to 12 months; Repayment up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Foreign university offer letter (I-20/CAS), standardized test scores, co-borrower income documents, property collateral documents if applicable.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Direct foreign exchange remittance.
- Tax savings under Section 80E.
