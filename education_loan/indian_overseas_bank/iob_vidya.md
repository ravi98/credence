# IOB Vidya Scheme - Indian Overseas Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
IOB Vidya is Indian Overseas Bank's primary education loan product offering funding for students pursuing graduation, post-graduation, professional, and doctorate courses.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs under CGFSEL
- **0.50%**: 0.50% concession for girl students
- **Repayment**: Repayment tenure of up to 15 years post-moratorium

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: IOB RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.65% to 10.35% p.a.
- **Concession**: 0.50% for female students.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian national with secured admission through entrance test/merit.
- Co-borrower: Parents/guardian.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 40 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: Nil up to Rs. 4 Lakhs; 5% India; 15% Abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 12 months; Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Nil for India.
- Prepayment: Zero penalty.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, mark sheets, fee structure, KYC, parent income proof.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Vidyalakshmi portal integration.
- Section 80E tax deduction.
