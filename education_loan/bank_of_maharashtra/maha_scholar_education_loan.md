# Maha Scholar Education Loan Scheme - Bank of Maharashtra

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Maha Scholar Education Loan is Bank of Maharashtra's specialized higher education financing program offering low interest rates and generous collateral waivers for students admitted to accredited premier colleges.

- **High**: High collateral-free limits for premier institutions
- **0.50%**: 0.50% interest concession for female students
- **Up**: Up to 15 years repayment post-moratorium

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: BOM RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.25% to 9.60% p.a.
- **Concession**: 0.50% for girl students.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Admission to recognized colleges in India or overseas.
- Co-borrower: Parents/guardian.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 40 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: Nil for premier institutes; 5% India; 15% Abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 12 months; Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil for India.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, mark sheets, fee breakdown, KYC, co-borrower income proof.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- CSIS eligible.
- Section 80E tax benefits.
