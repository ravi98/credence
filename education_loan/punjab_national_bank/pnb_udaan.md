# PNB Udaan Foreign Education Loan - Punjab National Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Udaan is Punjab National Bank's foreign education loan scheme providing end-to-end financial support for Indian students admitted to accredited universities across the globe.

- **High**: High quantum up to Rs. 1.50 Crore to cover tuition fees and overseas living expenses
- **In-principle**: In-principle sanction letter for foreign embassy visa interviews
- **0.50%**: 0.50% interest concession for female students

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.50% to 10.40% p.a.
- **Girl Student Concession**: 0.50%.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Admission letter with I-20 or equivalent from recognized foreign university.
- Tangible collateral required for loans above Rs. 7.50 Lakhs.
- Co-borrower mandatory.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- **Margin**: 15% (scholarships counted towards margin).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 12 months.
- Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 1% (adjustable/refundable upon loan drawal).
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- I-20 / CAS letter, TOEFL/IELTS/GRE marksheet, Co-borrower income proof, Collateral title deeds.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Seamless international wire transfers.
- Tax rebate under Section 80E.
