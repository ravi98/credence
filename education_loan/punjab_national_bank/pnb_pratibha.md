# PNB Pratibha Education Loan - Punjab National Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Pratibha is a privileged education loan scheme designed exclusively for students securing admission to top-ranked premier institutions across India. It features minimal documentation, zero processing fees, competitive low rates, and high collateral-free thresholds.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loan up to Rs. 40 Lakhs to Rs. 50 Lakhs for top tier institutes
- **Zero**: Zero margin money required for categorized premier colleges
- **Quick**: Quick digital sanctions and fast-tracked branch approvals

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 7.95% to 8.90% p.a.
- **Concession**: 0.50% rebate for girl students.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Admission to institutes listed under PNB Pratibha Tier-1, Tier-2, or Tier-3 lists.
- Co-borrower: Parents or guardian.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Tier-1 (Top IIMs/IITs): Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs without tangible security.
- Tier-2 (NITs/other IIMs): Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs without tangible security.
- Margin: Nil.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Course duration + 1 year moratorium; Up to 15 years repayment.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil.
- Foreclosure: Zero charges.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, scorecards (JEE/CAT/NEET), KYC, fee schedule.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Covers laptop, library fees, living costs, and academic field trips.
- Section 80E tax benefits.
