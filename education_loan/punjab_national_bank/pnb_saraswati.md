# PNB Saraswati Education Loan - Punjab National Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
PNB Saraswati is Punjab National Bank's comprehensive educational loan scheme for higher studies in India. It caters to degree, post-graduate, engineering, medical, and agricultural programs in UGC/AICTE approved universities.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs with CGFSEL credit guarantee
- **0.50%**: 0.50% concession for girl students
- **Covers**: Covers tuition fees, hostel, books, uniforms, and study tours

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: PNB Repo Linked Lending Rate (RLLR).
- **Effective Rate**: 8.50% to 10.25% p.a.
- **Concession**: 0.50% interest concession for female applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian national with secured admission through entrance test / merit.
- Co-applicant: Parents/Guardian.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Need-based financing up to Rs. 7.5 Lakhs (unsecured) and higher with collateral.
- Margin: Up to Rs. 4 Lakhs: Nil; Above Rs. 4 Lakhs: 5%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 1 year.
- Repayment: Up to 15 years after moratorium.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil for Indian education.
- Prepayment: Zero charges.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission proof, marksheets, fee structure, KYC, and parent income proof.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Central Sector Interest Subsidy (CSIS) eligible.
- Tax exemption under Section 80E.
