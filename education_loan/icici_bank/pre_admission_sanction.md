# ICICI Pre-Admission Sanction Education Loan - ICICI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
ICICI Bank's Pre-Admission Sanction scheme provides students with an in-principle loan sanction letter before securing final admission, fulfilling the financial solvency proof required by top global universities and embassies.

- **In-principle**: In-principle sanction based on academic profile and co-borrower financials
- **Valid**: Valid for foreign university applications and visa filing
- **Digital**: Digital processing through ICICI iMobile and internet banking

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: ICICI Repo Linked Lending Benchmark.
- **Effective Rate**: 9.50% to 12.50% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- High academic standing, standardized test scores (GRE, GMAT, IELTS).
- Creditworthy financial co-applicant.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 1.00 Crore.
- Margin: 10% to 15%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course period + 6 months; Repayment up to 12 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.00% - 2.00% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Past academic records, standardized test results, co-applicant KYC and income proof.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Sanction letter issued within 48-72 hours.
- Converts smoothly into disbursed loan upon final admission.
