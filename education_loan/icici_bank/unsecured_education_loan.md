# ICICI Unsecured Education Loan - ICICI Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
ICICI Unsecured Education Loan provides collateral-free financial support for students pursuing courses in premier domestic and international universities, eliminating the need to pledge real estate or liquid assets.

- **100%**: 100% collateral-free up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for top global universities
- **Covers**: Covers tuition fees, hostel, books, laptop, and living expenses
- **Quick**: Quick digital sanction with minimal paperwork

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: ICICI Repo Linked Lending Benchmark.
- **Effective Rate**: 9.75% to 13.25% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Confirmed admission to approved domestic/international universities.
- Earning co-borrower (parents/spouse).
- Co-borrower credit score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 40 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- Margin: Nil for premier institutes; 5% to 10% for others.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 6 months; Repayment up to 12 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, academic records, co-borrower KYC, Form 16, 6 months salary account statement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Fast disbursement directly to university.
- Section 80E tax deduction benefits.
