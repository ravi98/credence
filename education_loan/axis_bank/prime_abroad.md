# Axis Bank Prime Abroad Education Loan - Axis Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Axis Bank Prime Abroad is a premium education financing facility designed for international studies. It provides unsecured loans up to Rs. 75 Lakhs for premier global colleges and secured loans up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.

- **Unsecured**: Unsecured funding up to Rs. 75 Lakhs for top global universities
- **Multi-currency**: Multi-currency disbursement covering living expenses and tuition
- **Pre-visa**: Pre-visa sanction letter for visa processing

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Axis Bank RBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 9.75% to 13.50% p.a. based on institute tier and co-applicant income profile.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Confirmed admission to recognized overseas university.
- Co-applicant (parents/spouse/siblings) with verified income.
- Minimum credit score 700+.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 75 Lakhs (unsecured) / up to Rs. 1.50 Crore (secured).
- Margin: Nil for prime institutes; up to 15% for others.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course period + 6 months; Repayment up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Foreign university offer letter (I-20/CAS), GRE/GMAT/IELTS scorecards, co-applicant KYC and income proofs.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Seamless outward remittance directly via Axis Bank forex.
- Section 80E tax deduction benefits.
