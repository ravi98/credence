# Axis Bank Prime Domestic Education Loan - Axis Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Axis Bank Prime Domestic offers tailored education loans for premier Indian institutions. It covers 100% of education expenses with zero margin for top-tier categorized institutes.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loan up to Rs. 50 Lakhs for premier Indian institutes
- **Zero**: Zero margin money required for top categorized colleges
- **Doorstep**: Doorstep document pickup and fast-track digital approval

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Axis Bank RBLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 9.50% to 12.75% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Confirmed admission in approved premier institutions.
- Co-applicant: Parents/guardian.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs collateral-free (up to Rs. 75 Lakhs with collateral).
- Margin: Nil for premier institutes.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course period + 6 months; Repayment up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.00% - 1.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, entrance exam scorecard, KYC, co-applicant income documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Covers tuition, accommodation, laptop, and study trips.
- Section 80E tax deduction.
