# Baroda Gyan Education Loan - Bank of Baroda

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Baroda Gyan is Bank of Baroda's core educational financing product for domestic higher studies. It supports recognized degree and diploma courses conducted by colleges approved by UGC, AICTE, IMC, and government bodies.

- **No**: No collateral security required for loans up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs
- **0.50%**: 0.50% interest concession for female students
- **Covers**: Covers 100% of college tuition, examination, hostel, and equipment costs

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Baroda Repo Linked Lending Rate (BRLLR).
- **Effective Rate**: 8.55% to 10.15% p.a.
- **Girl Child Rebate**: 0.50% concession.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian national who has secured admission to recognized undergraduate or postgraduate programs.
- Co-borrower required: Parents or legal guardian.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 80 Lakhs depending on course and collateral.
- **Margin**: Up to Rs. 4 Lakhs: Nil; Above Rs. 4 Lakhs: 5%.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 1 year.
- Repayment: Up to 15 years after moratorium.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil for inland education loans.
- Foreclosure: Zero prepayment penalties.

## 7. Required Documents
- Mark sheets and entrance exam score card.
- Admission letter with fee schedule.
- KYC and income documents of co-borrower.
- Property papers if loan exceeds Rs. 7.5 Lakhs.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Section 80E tax deduction benefits.
- Vidyalakshmi portal integration.
- Flexible repayment via digital channels.
