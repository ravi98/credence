# bob Eco-Saarthi Education Loan - Bank of Baroda

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
bob Eco-Saarthi is Bank of Baroda's specialized green educational loan scheme incentivizing students pursuing careers in environmental conservation, renewable energy technologies, climate finance, and sustainable engineering in India or abroad.

- **Discounted**: Discounted interest rates compared to standard education loans
- **Concession**: Concession for courses in renewable energy, solar engineering, and climate analytics
- **Full**: Full tuition, laboratory, and living expenses covered

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Baroda BRLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.15% to 9.40% p.a. (up to 25-50 bps lower than standard rates).
- **Girl Student Concession**: Additional 0.50% concession.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Admission to verified green/sustainability/renewable energy courses in recognized institutions.
- Co-borrower mandatory.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 80 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: 5% for India, 15% for Abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Course duration + 1 year moratorium; Up to 15 years repayment.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Waived for domestic studies; subsidized for abroad.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Course syllabus highlighting sustainability/green tech orientation.
- Admission letter, fee structure, academic records.
- Co-borrower KYC and income proof.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Promotes environmental education aligned with ESG goals.
- Priority digital processing.
- Full 80E tax deduction.
