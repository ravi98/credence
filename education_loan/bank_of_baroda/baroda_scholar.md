# Baroda Scholar Education Loan - Bank of Baroda

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Baroda Scholar is Bank of Baroda's flagship international education loan scheme. It offers high quantum funding for students admitted to recognized universities in USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Singapore, and Europe, categorized into Specified Premier Universities and Non-Specified Universities.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 40 Lakhs for Premier Overseas Universities List
- **100%**: 100% refund of processing fee upon taking the first disbursement
- **Sanction**: Sanction letters provided for proof of funds during student visa interview

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Baroda BRLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.55% to 10.65% p.a. based on institute ranking and collateral.
- **Concessions**: 0.50% concession for girl students.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Confirmed admission in recognized foreign university.
- Co-borrower (parents/guardian) mandatory.
- Minimum qualifying marks in graduation/qualifying exams.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore for specified premier universities (up to Rs. 80 Lakhs for other institutions).
- **Margin**: 10% to 15% (scholarships counted towards margin).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 12 months.
- Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: 1% (Max Rs. 10,000 + GST), fully refunded once loan is disbursed.
- Zero foreclosure or part-payment penalty.

## 7. Required Documents
- I-20 / CAS letter or formal admission letter.
- TOEFL/IELTS/GRE/GMAT scorecards.
- Financial and KYC records of co-applicant.
- Collateral property documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Seamless outward remittance directly to foreign universities.
- Covers living expenses, health insurance, and laptop purchases.
- Section 80E tax benefits.
