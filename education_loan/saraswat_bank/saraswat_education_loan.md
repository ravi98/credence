# Saraswat Bank Education Loan - Saraswat Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Saraswat Bank Education Loan caters to students pursuing engineering, medicine, management, and technical degrees in recognized Indian colleges and overseas universities.

- **Attractive**: Attractive co-operative banking rates
- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs
- **Personalized**: Personalized branch service and quick sanction

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Saraswat Base Lending Rate.
- **Effective Rate**: 9.50% to 11.50% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian citizen securing admission through merit.
- Co-borrower mandatory.
- Nominal shareholding membership in Saraswat Bank required.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 75 Lakhs.
- Margin: 5% for India; 15% for Abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 6 months; Repayment up to 10 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 0.50% - 1.00% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission proof, mark sheets, KYC, co-borrower income documents, property papers if applicable.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Section 80E tax deduction.
- Transparent charges with no hidden fees.
