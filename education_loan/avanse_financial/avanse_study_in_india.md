# Avanse Study in India & Executive Education Loan - Avanse Financial

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Avanse Study in India & Executive Education Loan funds degree courses, vocational certifications, and executive MBA programs conducted by premier Indian institutions like ISB, IIMs, and private business schools.

- **Specialized**: Specialized funding for Executive MBA and working professional upskilling
- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 40 Lakhs for premier Indian B-schools
- **Flexible**: Flexible repayment models including EMI or partial interest during course

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Avanse Base Rate (ABR).
- **Effective Rate**: 10.25% to 14.50% p.a.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Admission to recognized Indian institute / Executive program.
- Salaried professionals self-sponsoring or co-borrower support.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Up to Rs. 50 Lakhs.
- Margin: Nil for premier institutes; up to 10% for others.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 6 months (or immediate EMI for working executives); Repayment up to 10 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.50% - 2.00% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, work experience certificate (for executive courses), KYC, salary slips, 2 years ITR, 6 months bank statement.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Enables mid-career career acceleration without liquidation of savings.
- Full 80E tax deduction benefits.
