# Avanse Study Abroad Education Loan - Avanse Financial

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
Avanse Financial Services is a premier education-focused NBFC providing student loans for study abroad. Avanse covers 100% cost of education including tuition, travel, living expenses, books, and laptops.

- **100%**: 100% financing of education cost with zero margin required
- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free loans up to Rs. 75 Lakhs for premier foreign universities
- **Pre-visa**: Pre-visa disbursal and fast sanction within 3 business days

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: Avanse Base Rate (ABR).
- **Effective Rate**: 10.00% to 14.50% p.a. based on institute ranking, country, and co-borrower financial standing.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Confirmed admission or conditional offer from recognized foreign university.
- Financial co-borrower (parents/guardian/siblings) residing in India.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- Need-based up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: Nil (100% financing).

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 6 months.
- Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Up to 1.50% - 2.50% + GST.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- University admission letter / I-20, academic scorecards, co-borrower KYC, income documents, bank statements.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- Quick online sanction letter for visa interview.
- Section 80E tax deduction benefits.
- Living expense disbursement in student's overseas bank account.
