# UCO Education Loan Scheme - UCO Bank

## 1. Overview & Key Highlights
UCO Education Loan Scheme provides financial support to deserving and meritorious students to pursue higher education in recognized educational institutions in India and abroad.

- **Collateral-free**: Collateral-free funding up to Rs. 7.50 Lakhs
- **0.50%**: 0.50% interest concession for girl students
- **Moratorium**: Moratorium covering entire course duration plus 1 year

## 2. Interest Rates & Benchmarks
- **Benchmark**: UCO Bank RLLR.
- **Effective Rate**: 8.55% to 10.30% p.a.
- **Concession**: 0.50% rebate for women applicants.

## 3. Eligibility Criteria
- Indian citizen with secured admission through merit/entrance.
- Co-borrower mandatory.

## 4. Loan Quantum & Limits
- India: Up to Rs. 30 Lakhs; Abroad: Up to Rs. 1.50 Crore.
- Margin: Nil up to Rs. 4 Lakhs; 5% India; 15% Abroad.

## 5. Repayment Tenure & Process
- Moratorium: Course duration + 12 months; Repayment: Up to 15 years.

## 6. Processing Fees & Other Charges
- Processing Fee: Nil for India.
- Prepayment: Nil.

## 7. Required Documents
- Admission letter, mark sheets, fee structure, KYC, co-borrower income documents.

## 8. Special Features & Benefits
- CSIS interest subsidy eligible.
- Section 80E tax deduction.
